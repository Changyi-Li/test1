### Settings – List type Requirements – Summarized
These settings are available for the list type Requirements – Summarized.

#### Price alternative
Enables displaying the requirements in amounts in the company currency.
Here you select the price that should be used in the list. The price alternatives that can be selected are:
- Customer price – loads prices from the customer link. Default option.
- Price list – This alternative will activate the next field where you can select a price list.

#### Price list
If you have selected the alternative Price list in the setting above, you can here select which price list to use.

#### Use first period containing requirement as first shown period in the result under the List tab
If this setting is activated, the period within the selected interval which has the earliest requirement becomes the period column which is presented as the first visible period column, furthest to the left in the list.

#### Show deviation alerts and warnings
With this setting you can choose to show call-offs that have alerts for deviations or warning, in the list. Deviation warnings are indicated with a yellow background color, deviation alerts are indicated with a red background color.
