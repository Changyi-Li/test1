### Settings

#### Show employees
With this setting you determine which employees should be included in the list:
- All – This option will include all employees which have Time recording configured to Attendance/work and Schedule management set to According to schedule cycle. Employees who are blocked are not shown.
- For which I am main signer – This alternative will include all employees for whom you are the main signer.
- All I can adjust – This alternative will show the persons for whom I am allowed to adjust work and/or attendance. The persons for whom no signer have been registered in the personnel records are also shown.

#### Only employees with planned absence
With this setting you determine if only employees that have planned absence will be included in the list, except for employees that are blocked. The list only shows persons who in the personnel register have Time recording set to Attendance/work as well as Schedule management configured to According to schedule cycle.

#### Date
Here you enter the date interval you want to show in the list. The default interval here is from today's date and two weeks on.

#### Absence status
This setting determines the absence statuses shown in the list. The following options are available:
- Applied for
- Authorized
- Denied
