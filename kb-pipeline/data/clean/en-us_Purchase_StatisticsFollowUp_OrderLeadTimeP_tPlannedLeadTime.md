### Planned lead time list
The list is loaded and displayed based on the selection and settings you have chosen. As in all lists in Monitor ERP, you can drag and drop in order to change the column to group by.
This list shows how the order row has been planned in relation to desired delivery time and the part’s lead time.

#### Desired lead time
Desired lead time
Desired lead time = Desired delivery date – Print date

#### Planned lead time
Planned lead time is a calculated column that shows the amount of days that are planned for delivery.
Planned lead time = Planned delivery date – Print date

#### Difference
The difference between what you have desired and what you have planned for will be shown in the calculated column Difference. If the value is positive, you have planned for more days than you have desired. If the value is negative, you have planned for fewer days than we have desired.
Difference = Planned delivery date – Desired delivery date

#### Comparison
If the setting Part’s lead time is checked, the calculated column Comparison is shown. This column shows the comparison between the part's lead time and the planned lead time. If the difference is a positive value, it means that the order row is planned to be delivered on a delivery date after the part’s lead time. If the difference is a negative value, it means that the order row is planned to be delivered on a delivery date before the part’s lead time.
Comparison = Planned lead time – Part’s lead time
