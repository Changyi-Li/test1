### Settings
The settings in the Stock value between warehouses procedure are nearly the same as the [settings](../StockValue/bSettingsDetailedTotal.htm) in the Stock value procedure. The information that differs is described below.
By using the price alternative Customer order/Invoice, you valuate purchased and manufactured parts based on the parts' price on the customer order.
You cannot display any historical price or calculation information in this procedure. In this procedure there are no balance alternatives or alternatives to show different "last dates" (last arrival date, last consumption date, and last stock count date) for the parts.
