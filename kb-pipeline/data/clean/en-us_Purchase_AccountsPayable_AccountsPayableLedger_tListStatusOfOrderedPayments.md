### List type Status of ordered payments
This list is only available if you have activated the option Bank integration.
Here you can see the status of your ordered payments, meaning the payments that have been sent to the bank via the bank integration.
The list is grouped by status and you can see, among other things, sending date, consecutive number, transaction list, supplier, supplier’s invoice number, and amount.
The following status options are shown in the list:
-   
Failed sending to bank (i.e. if reference numbers have been entered incorrectly, or there is some other type of error that the bank will not accept)
-   
Not signed
-   
Rejected
-   
Partially signed (if countersigning is used and only the first signing has been made)
-   
Pending payment (the payment has been sent to the bank correctly, and is waiting to be payed out from the bank account)
-   
Paid
-   
Canceled
Using the button Load payment status ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_run.png) lets you update the statuses of the payments automatically. If the status has been retroactively changed at the bank, you need to use the button to see the current status of the payment. If you load the list without loading payment status, the status that the payment received at signing of the payment will be shown.
