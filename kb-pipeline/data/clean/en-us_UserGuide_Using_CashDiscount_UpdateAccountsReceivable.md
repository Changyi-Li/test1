### Update accounts receivable
In the Update accounts receivable procedure you can use the button ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_more_info.png) next to the due date to see if cash discount has been given on the invoice.
[![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/TrainingMaterial/UpdateAccountsReceivable.png)](../../../../Resources/Images/TrainingMaterial/UpdateAccountsReceivable.png)
Here you also have the opportunity to adjust the cash discount date and the cash discount (%) on the invoice.
If you create new invoices via this procedure you can select cash discount on the invoice. To do so you select a payment term which is linked to cash discounts.
