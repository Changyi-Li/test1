### The list type Material – Detailed
The list is loaded and displayed based on the selection and settings you have chosen. As in all lists in Monitor ERP, you can drag and drop in order to change the column to group by.
This list displays information about reporting of material in detailed form and shows planned, reported remaining and rejected quantity.
The list is grouped according to selected presentation. Grouped by date, Grouped by material's part number, and Grouped by manufacturing order, are the presentations available as standard. When grouping by date you can also select to group dates by day, week, month, quarter, or year.
