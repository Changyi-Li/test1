### Posting
In the Posting box you will see the posting of the invoice row selected in the box Incoming payments box. It is possible to make changes in the Posting box but you cannot add/delete or change accounts that in the Chart of accounts procedure have the setting Block for manual postings activated. In other respects, the Posting box is designed in the same way as other posting procedures in the system.
The account for incoming payments for the customer in question is loaded from the customer register.
When invoices are paid and on account payments are made, the accounts receivable account is credited and the bank account is charged. When credit invoices are paid, the situation is reversed.
If the setting Specification is activated for the accounts receivable account in the Chart of accounts procedure, the invoice number will automatically be used as specification text. For on account payments, the specification text will instead be "On account On account is a partial payment (advance payment) which you have made to a supplier or received from a customer. + [consecutive number]". The consecutive number is loaded from the number series for On account invoice (Sales).
For payments of advance invoices, the order number is saved for the posting items which concern recording of the change between unpaid and paid advances (if the account has been configured for this).

#### Total accounting order
At the top of the box you will see the setting Total accounting order. If you check this checkbox, you will see a total posting for the postings of all the invoices. This allows you to easier check that everything is correctly recorded before you save the incoming payment batch. When you activate the setting Total accounting order, the postings will be automatically total and it is no longer possible to change these postings in the box. If there are different payment dates among the registered payments of the batch, the total posting will be divided by payment date.
