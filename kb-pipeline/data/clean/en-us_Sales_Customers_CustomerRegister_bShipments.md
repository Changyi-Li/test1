### Shipments
In this box you can see all shipments (transports) of goods to the customer.
Using the button ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_link.png) you can go to the Register shipment procedure for the selected shipment. In the G column, it is also possible to perform goods tracking of the shipment. Goods tracking works for shipments sent via nShift Delivery and Web-TA.
You can also see the total number of records.
