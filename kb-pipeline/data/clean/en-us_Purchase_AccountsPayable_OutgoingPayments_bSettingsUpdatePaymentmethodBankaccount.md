### Settings

#### Include
Determines whether invoices with the status Registered, Preliminary recorded, or Final recorded should be shown in the list. By default, all statuses are selected.

#### Show blocked
With this setting you determine whether or not blocked invoices should be shown in the list.

#### Payment method
Determines whether the payment method on the invoice should be synchronized with the payment method in the Supplier register.

#### Recipient account
Determines whether the recipient account on the invoice should be synchronized with the recipient account in the Supplier register.

#### Only show invoices with a difference
Determines whether only invoices where payment method/bank account has been updated should be shown in the list.

#### Pre-select "Include"
This setting determines whether or not the “Include” box should be checked by default for all rows in the list/result.
