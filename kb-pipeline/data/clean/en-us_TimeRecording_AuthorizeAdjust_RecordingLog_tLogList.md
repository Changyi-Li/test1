### The list type Log list – Attendance
This list shows all attendance recording items (In/Out), absence recording and overtime recording items in chronological order, day by day. The list is grouped by employee and shows day, date, and time of recording, if the recording item concerns clocking in or clocking out, absence code, overtime type, which terminal was used for the recording, what action created the recording item, and if the recording was performed in the mobile client.
You select which recording actions to include in list by configuring settings under the Selection tab.
