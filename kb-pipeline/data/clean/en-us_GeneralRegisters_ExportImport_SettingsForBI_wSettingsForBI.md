## Settings for BI
> This procedure is no longer being developed or maintained and will be removed in future updates. We recommend that you instead use our new, web-based Monitor BI. Read more about [Monitor BI](../../../UserGuide/GettingStarted/MonitorBI.htm).
> The user rights for this procedure are by default limited to users assigned the ERP manager role or System administrator.
In this procedure you can configure general settings regarding data mining to the BI functions in Monitor ERP. For example, which data should be extracted and how often.
The settings in this procedures apply to the entire system and not only to the company you are working in. Regardless of which company you are working in, you find the same settings. The settings are saved in a system database. These settings do NOT apply to the external application Monitor BI.
Under the Scheduling tab you create one or several scheduled data mining and configure settings to each data mining.
If you use one or several companies in Monitor G4 systems, you can also create data mining from these company databases. This is made under the External companies tab.
Under the Currency tab you can enter the company currency for each respective company in the system including any external companies. For each company you enter the exchange rate that applies from different dates in relation to the company currency in the main company.
Under the Access settings tab you can deny other companies in the system access to your company’s data in the business intelligence. If your company has been denied access to another company's data, you can make exceptions for individual users of yours, so that only those persons get access to the other company's data in the business intelligence.
