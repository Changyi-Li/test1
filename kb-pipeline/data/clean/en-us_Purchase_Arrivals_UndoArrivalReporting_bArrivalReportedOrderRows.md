### Arrival reported order rows
A list of arrival reporting made for purchase order rows.

#### Undo
Select the checkbox in this column for the arrival reporting of the order row you wish to undo.

#### Arrival reported quantity
Here you see the quantity that has been arrival reported for the order row and that will be undone.

#### Arrived quantity
In this column you see the quantity that has arrived to the receiving inspection and that will be undone.

#### Actual delivery date
This is available if the Check open accounting period during order reporting system setting is activated in systems where stock accounting and management accounting are applied. This check is made against the actual date when reporting arrival of purchase order or when undoing arrival reporting of purchase order.

#### Log date
Here you see when transaction was made.
