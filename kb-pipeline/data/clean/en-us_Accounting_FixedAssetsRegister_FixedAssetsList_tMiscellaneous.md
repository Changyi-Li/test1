### The Miscellaneous list
In this list you see information from the boxes in the Register fixed assets object procedure. The information on the rows can be updated in this list.
The general grouping displays information from the boxes Miscellaneous, Posting, Description, and Comment/Files.
The presentation Acquisition/Insurance displays information from the boxes Acquisition and Insurance/Warranty.
The presentation Depreciation information display information from the Depreciation box.
