### The Stock driven M-parts tab
Under this tab you find information about planned and reported quantities, times and reported costs, and SO/SC mark-ups for stock driven manufactured parts that are incorporated in the selected order and main part.
If there is a manufacturing order log for the part, you can see it under the button Manufacturing order log ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_log.png) on the row. Using the Go to the level list button ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_link.png) on each row you can link to the part under the Level list tab and see where it is located in the structure.
The tab is similar to the corresponding tab in the Post-calculation procedure.
