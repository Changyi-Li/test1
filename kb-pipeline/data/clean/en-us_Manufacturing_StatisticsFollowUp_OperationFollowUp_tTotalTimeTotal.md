### The Total time – Total
The list is loaded and displayed based on the selection and settings you have chosen. As in all lists in Monitor ERP, you can drag and drop in order to change the column to group by.
This list type loads planned and reported quantity, planned and reported total time, and efficiency factor (E-factor) for the total time. The list is total by work center or by actual finish date. In the list you can use the drilldown function on the rows to see the detailed information.
At the bottom you see a total of quantity, times, and E-factor. Below the totals, you also see the efficiency factor in chart form.
