### Settings
General

#### Annual volume
If you have activated the system setting called Show annual budget, annual volume, and order quantity with current pace, it is possible use Annual volume or Annual volume, current pace when calculating volume value.

#### Classification
Applies to the list types: Classification and Classification grouped by ABCABC codes are used to classify the range of parts by the volumes you sell. The codes are used as a scale for the parts that turn over the most money. The turnover is calculated by multiplying the price of the part by the annual volume. Parts that turn over the most money are called "A-parts", and after that, "B-parts", etc. code. Here you decide how to classify the parts. You can choose from the following classifications:
- None, only show – Only existing ABC codes are shown.
- According to volume value % – The parts are classified with ABC codes. These are based on the volume value (in percent of the total value) of the selected parts.
- According to number of parts % – The parts are classified with ABC codes. These are based on the number of parts (in percent of the total value) for the selected parts.
- According to amount limits on volume value – The parts are classified according to amount limits on the volume value.

#### Total from all warehouses
With this setting you determine whether or not all available warehouses registered on the company, should be total in the list. The ABC code is saved in the warehouse you are working in. This setting is not activated by default. Warehouse is an option.
Price type
These settings determine which prices should be used for purchased and manufactured parts,. If you select the price list option for any of the settings, the field below it will become available. There it is possible to select which price list to apply. If you have selected a supplier price or a price in another currency, the field where you can select rate type will also become available.

#### Include SO mark-up
With this checkbox you determine if SO mark-up (storage overhead mark-up) should be included in the calculation for purchased parts. Any exceptions entered for the parts will be considered.
ABC codes
Applies to the list types: Classification and Classification grouped by ABC code. In the ABC codes tables, you see default values for the registered ABC codes. These can be modified for separate runs. However, modifications made here will not affect the basic data. All columns found under the ABC codes tab in the Basic dataWith "basic data" we refer to the static records in a database, for example parts, customers, users, work centers, etc. – Part procedure are also shown here.
