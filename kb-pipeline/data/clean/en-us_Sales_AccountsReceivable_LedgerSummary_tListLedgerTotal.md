### The Ledger – Total list
This list displays total information for invoices with invoice amounts and remaining amounts per a certain ledger date. By activating a setting under the Selection tab, you can also choose to include fully paid invoices. You can total the list by VAT registration number and currency. Regardless what you total the list by, you can see the number of invoices and you can also expand each row to show detailed information. In the list you find the Ledger summary column which displays the net amount between unpaid customer invoices and supplier invoices. A positive amount means that that company has a debt to you , and a negative amount means that you have a debt to the other company.
