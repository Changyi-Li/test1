### Statistics
In this box you see statistics for the customer in the shape of a bar chart.
You can select to view sales statistics*, order inflow, delivery reliability, quote statistics, or cases.
You can select a time limit in number of months. It determines for how far back in time data should be included in the statistics.
For statistics regarding delivery reliability there are two options:
- Delivery reliability – Planned – Measures the delivery reliability taking the actual delivery date and the planned delivery date into account.
- Delivery reliability – Initial – Measures the delivery reliability taking the actual delivery date and the initial delivery date into account.
> * The sales statistics shown is for when the customer is the order customer on the invoices.
