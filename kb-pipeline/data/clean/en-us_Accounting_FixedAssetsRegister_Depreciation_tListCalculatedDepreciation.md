### The Calculated depreciation list
When you use calculated depreciation, the depreciating is only done via list.
The method for the calculations of calculated depreciation differs from the scheduled depreciation in several ways. The scheduled depreciation is based on remaining residual values and distributes depreciation over the remaining depreciation period. The calculated depreciation completely takes place based on the depreciation basis, not taking the remaining depreciation period into account.
The calculated depreciation differs if the asset is fully depreciated by schedule or if it is not fully depreciated. This is governed via the fixed assets group.
