### Buffer location to move from
In this box the Quantity to move on the buffer location will be automatically filled in with the same quantity as in the Quantity field for the part in the upper box called Pick location to refill. If the quantity on one buffer location is not sufficient, the remaining quantity will be taken from more than one of the available buffer locations. The withdrawal from buffer locations is made using the same rules as for withdrawal from stock locations. That is, primarily from the location with the highest priority, and secondarily based on age analysis.
If the pre-filled quantity on the buffer location does not match the quantity that has been physically moved, you can adjust the pre-filled quantity directly on the buffer location. Then the Quantity to move field will be automatically adjusted and locked. The document Refill pick location is used as basis for the reporting.
