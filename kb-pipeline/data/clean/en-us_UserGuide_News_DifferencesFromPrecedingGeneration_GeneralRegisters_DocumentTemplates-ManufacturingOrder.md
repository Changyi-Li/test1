### Document templates – Manufacturing order
- The manufacturing order documents are a part of the new document concept which means that you yourself can make changes in the layout and you do not have to generate the document after the changes.
- The system provides three basic types of documents and for these you can then create your own variants and edit with a built-in tool called DevExpress Report Designer.
- There are quite a few new settings which you use to decide what to be shown on the manufacturing order.
