### Search stock accounting/Search management accounting
In the procedure Search stock accounting/Search management accounting, you can search for transactions in the stock accounting and/or management accounting and get the result presented in a detailed or a total list. You can find which transactions have been made during a specific time period for a selection of orders, accounts, parts, etc. It is also possible to include transactions which have not yet been approved, that is, transactions waiting for approval in the Print log journal procedure.
![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/TrainingMaterial/ManagementAcc57.png)
Transactions which have not yet been approved and transferred to the accounting, are shown in italics in the list.
[![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/TrainingMaterial/ManagementAcc58.png)](../../../../Resources/Images/TrainingMaterial/ManagementAcc58.png)
> Please note! This procedure gathers data from many logs. The list might take time to load if you do not make any selections.
