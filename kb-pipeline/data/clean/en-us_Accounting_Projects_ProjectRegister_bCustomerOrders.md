### Customer order
In this box you see the customer orders that exist for the project in question. The customer orders are shown regardless of the status. Deleted customer orders are not shown.
By using the button Show orders without remaining amount ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_goto_critical_op.png) you can choose to also show orders that do not have a remaining amount.
From the row in the table you can go to the Register customer order procedure via a link or by double-clicking the record.
Using the Show document button ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_form_viewing.png), you open the document in a separate window. There you can also reprint the document or attach it to an e-mail message.
