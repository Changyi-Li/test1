### Settings

#### Show part rows without affect on balance
This only applies to the Detailed list type. With this setting you determine if delivered order rows containing parts that are not stock updated (row type 2), should also be included in the list.

#### Show delivery notes not yet received
This setting is activated by default. This means that all delivery notes are displayed by default in the list; both the ones marked as received and the ones not marked as received.

#### Exclude packaging parts
With this setting you decide if the packaging parts should be excluded in the list.
