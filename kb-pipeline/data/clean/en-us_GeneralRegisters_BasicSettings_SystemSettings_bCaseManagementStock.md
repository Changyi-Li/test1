### Case management

#### Affect balance for case
This setting determines if it should be possible to perform stock reporting of the number of rejections in cases in the Register case procedure. When the system setting is activated, you can link ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_link.png) to the Direct stock reporting procedure from the case in question. You can perform stock reporting for all case types.

#### Log outgoing e-mail
This system setting determines that an e-mail log will be created under the Logging tab in the Register case procedure, when someone sends the following documents via e-mail from the procedure: 1) Confirmation of or response to customer nonconformity. 2) Claim report for supplier nonconformity.
