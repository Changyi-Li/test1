### Change period/accounting year
- The procedure has been renamed and was previously called Period Management.
- The system setting Advanced period management? does not exist in G5. For each period there are the status options Open, Closed, and Locked.
- In G4 a warning was shown when changing period. In G5 you will see warnings directly when you open the procedure and in front of each period containing warnings.
- You change accounting year and change year in the same procedure. When the final period is the current period, a button becomes activated where you can change year.
- If the next year has not been created in advance, it is possible to enter what the new year should be called in connection with the changing of year.
