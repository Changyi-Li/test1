## Register delivery schedule – Sales
In this procedure you register and update delivery schedules.
There are three different ways to register delivery schedules:
- Register the delivery schedules manually in this procedure.
- Import delivery schedules from an EDI EDI is the acronym of Electronic Data Interchange. EDI is about exchanging electronic business documents with your business partners, e.g. customers and suppliers. The EDI concept can be wide and a bit unclear, and can many times be used about all types of documents which are sent electronically, even if it might be PDF files sent via e-mail or publishing business documents on a website. What we refer to as EDI – and what is traditionally meant by EDI – is structured business documents following given standards, electronically sent or received and which are compiled and interpreted automatically and that is integrated with the customer's/supplier's ERP system. source.
- Import delivery schedules via Monitor-to-Monitor.
In this procedure you can also load the delivery schedules which have been imported via EDI and via Monitor-to-Monitor.
A delivery schedule mainly consists of these hierarchic levels: 1) The delivery schedule's header with the receiver of expected deliveries and overall information, for example buyer, date, and type. 2) One or multiple scheduled parts with corresponding information such as dock, storage, and the customer's order number. 3) The calls belonging to a certain receiver and part, with information about for example date, quantity, and type of call.
In the procedure you see this information, and it can also be updated at the same time in the different levels in a delivery schedule.
Save as
