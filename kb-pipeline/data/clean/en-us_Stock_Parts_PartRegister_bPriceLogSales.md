### Price log
In this box you can see information about sales prices from the price log. The prices are shown in the company currency and the unit is the unit entered on the main row. Read more about the different [price alternatives](../PartPrices.htm).

#### Last sales price
In this field you can see the part's last sales price. The Last sales price and the Average sales price below are only updated if the option Create sales statistics has been activated for the order type in the Order types procedure.

#### Average sales price
Here you can see an average value for the last ten sales prices (that have been invoiced).

#### Customer price log
By clicking this button you can display a log of customer prices from the customer links.

#### Sales price log
By clicking this button you can display a log of sales prices from the price lists.
