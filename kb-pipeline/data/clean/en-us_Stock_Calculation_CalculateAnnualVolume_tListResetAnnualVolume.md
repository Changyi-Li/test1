### The Reset annual volume list
This list type is used to set the annual volume to zero for the selected parts.

#### Save
By marking the Save checkbox and then saving in the procedure, the annual volume is reset to zero.

#### Annual volume
Here you see the part's annual volume.

#### Last reset
Here you see the date and time of the most recent reset of the annual volume.

#### Last update
Here you see the date and time of the most recent update of the annual volume.
