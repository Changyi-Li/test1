### The Total list
This list type shows a total list containing one row for each record. It can be presented as total by part or by part code. The list shows the row's positive and negative stock count difference, stock count difference, price, positive and negative value change, and net value.
Using the expand function on the rows you can load the detailed information for each row.
At the bottom of the list you see a total of the entire value change in the list, of which positive difference and negative difference.
