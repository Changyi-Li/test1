### The Customer links list
This list contains the parts' customer links. The list can be grouped by part number or customer.
By clicking the Updateable button ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_edit_list.png) (Ctrl + U), you can load the list and update/modify information in the customer links on multiple parts. This corresponds to what you can modify per part in the Customer links box in the Part register procedure.
> If you load the list without making it updateable, you can see which parts are missing customer links. Then the customer name will be "Customer link is missing". Use the presentation called Group by customer to show the parts without customer links in the first group in the list.
