### Reported material
Here you can see information about the reported material that is being undone for the reporting selected in the Reporting box.

#### Part node
Here you can see the part number of the part node to which the material belongs.

#### For operation
Here you can see the operation number for which the material is used.

#### Reported quantity
In this column you can see the reported quantity of the material that is being undone.

#### Rejected quantity
Here you can see the rejected quantity of the material, if any, that is being undone.
