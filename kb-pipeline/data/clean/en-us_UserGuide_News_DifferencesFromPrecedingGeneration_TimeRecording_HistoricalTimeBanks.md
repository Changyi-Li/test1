### Historical time banks
- In G5 it is possible to show the balance an employee had for example in his/her comp time (compensatory time) bank at a certain date. That balance will then also include the late adjustments made which affect the balance.
- Via the procedure Update time balances it is also possible to enter which Balance brought forward an employee would have had on a historical date. This makes it easier to reconcile balances between the payroll program and G5.
