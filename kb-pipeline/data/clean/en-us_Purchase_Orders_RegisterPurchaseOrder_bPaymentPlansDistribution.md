### Distribution of the payment plan
On the rows here you see the selected payment plan's distribution.

#### Row
Here you see the row number.

#### Status
In this column you see the status of the rows.

#### Partial invoice type
The partial invoice types available you can select here are Advance, Delivery, and In arrears.
- Advance – This invoice is sent before the order is delivered. For each partial invoice number of the advance type, an invoice basis is created.
- Delivery – This invoice is sent at the delivery of the order. An invoice basis is created in connection with arrival reporting of order.
- In arrears – This invoice is sent separately in arrears, for example, when the delivery has been approved on final inspection. For each partial invoice number of the "In arrears" type, an invoice basis is created.

#### Part
For the partial invoice types "Advance" and "In arrears" you will see the service registered for this payment plan. It is possible to select another service for the row.

#### Name
Here you see the name of the service for advance invoices and in arrears invoices. For delivery invoices you see the name Delivery invoice.

#### Invoicing period
Here you see the invoicing period. Today's date is set by default for all partial invoice types, but it can be changed for the respective partial invoice type.

#### Percent (%)
The Percent column indicates how large a portion of the order value that will be invoiced on each occasion.

#### Amount
Here you see amount in the currency of the order. The amount will be recalculated depending on the percentage.

#### Consecutive number
Here you see the consecutive number of this partial invoice.

#### Invoiced amount
In this column you see the invoiced amount.

#### Left to invoice
In this column you can see the amount left to invoice.

#### Account
Here you see the account for the row.

#### Posting
In this column you see the posting of the row.

#### VAT code
Here you see/select a default VAT code. VAT codes must first be registered and activated in the VAT settings procedure.
