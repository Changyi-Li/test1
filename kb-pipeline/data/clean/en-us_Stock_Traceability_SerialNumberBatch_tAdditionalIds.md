### Additional IDs
The list is only visible if the Additional IDs system setting is activated under the Stock tab in the System settings procedure.
Under this tab you can enter information about, for example, DDS and the EU Deforestation Regulation (EUDR). You can manually add, import, or export additional IDs linked to a batch or a serial number
To obtain an increased traceability for the DDS number, the information from the supplier is entered in this list, and in cases where multiple DDS number have been entered in the same delivery.
It is possible to use additional IDs for other purposes if your company is not required to provide traceability according to EUDR.
