### The list Internal operations
In this list you can update information for internal (own) operations. The information shown in this list is the same as the data on operation rows in the BOM and routing procedure. For more information about each column, please [see the help function](../BOMAndRouting/bOperations.htm) for that procedure.
> Please note! If you have changed Operation number and the operation number is not associated with any material, you will not receive a warning as you do in the BOM and routing procedure.
