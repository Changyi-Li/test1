### The Recording items list
This list displays records with attendance recording items grouped by person.
The C column (Changed via adjustment) shows an exclamation mark for records that have been adjusted. The NA column (Not authorized) shows an asterisk for records which have not been authorized (days). The M column (Manual) displays a question mark for records generated by a forgotten clock out or generated by a recording item with the recording terminal in training mode. Read more about settings for users who forget to clock out and training mode in the help function for the System settings procedure.
The records are primarily sorted by clock in time, and secondarily by clock out time.
On the grouping row for each employee you see a total of worked time (attendance + overtime + flex), attendance, overtime before and overtime after the scheduled time, absence, and flex. The flex time is not saved per recording item but is instead loaded from the total of flex of the day in question.
Below the list you see a total of these times for all employees in the list.
