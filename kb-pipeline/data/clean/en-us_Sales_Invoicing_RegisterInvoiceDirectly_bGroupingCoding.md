### Grouping/Posting
Here you group and post the invoice basis. The information entered here corresponds to the information in the [Grouping/Posting](../../Orders/RegisterCustomerOrder/bGroupingCoding.htm) box for a customer order. However, a difference is that you cannot create a project for an invoice basis.

#### Agreement basis
If an agreement is linked to the invoice, you will here see the agreement number. This field can not be edited.
