### Log for part
In this box you see a log after the check is made of the marked parts. If a part cannot be deleted or it cannot be deactivated, you will see row with information about in which registers the part occurs.
By using the Go to procedure button ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_link.png) you can via the link go to related procedures for the log item on the row in question.
