### Training, preparations, and actual startup
When information and settings have been registered and controlled in Monitor ERP it is time for a training phase.
Prior to the training you will be given access to the e-learning platform [Monitor Academy](https://monitorerp.com/sv/academy/) where you find Monitor ERP courses.
During the training phase, consultants/instructors from Monitor ERP System AB work through basic data and order flows with the affected personnel.
After the training phase a preparation phase begins. Then for example a balance import is performed to give parts current balances on locations. If the company has converted from Monitor G4, personnel from Monitor ERP System AB will perform a final control of the data migration and final migration of the database to a Monitor ERP database. For documented settings of user rights, system settings, and other information, a synchronization is made. The affected personnel in the company will then perform a control and approve the migration/conversion.
After the preparation phase you are ready to go live in Monitor ERP for the company's operations.
