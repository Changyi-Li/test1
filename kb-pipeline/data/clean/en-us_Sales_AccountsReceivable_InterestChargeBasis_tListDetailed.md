### The Detailed list
This list shows detailed bases for interest charge, grouped by customer. The list contains partially paid invoices and fully paid invoices, which have interest that should be charged the customer. Based on this list you can release bases for interest charge.
You release the bases in the list using the button Release interest invoices ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_run.png) on the toolbar. You will get a control question regarding the releasing of interest invoices. After the control question, the list will be emptied. After you release the interest charge bases, the following will take place:
- The bases with Interest invoice selected as interest charge in the list will automatically be loaded in the Review/Approve invoice procedure for approval and printing of interest invoices.
- The bases with Next regular invoice selected as interest charge in the list will be put in queue for interest charge in the Review/Approve invoice procedure. There you will see a button which lets you know that there is interest to be charged. Using that button you then get to mark the customers for which you want to charge interest on regular invoices.

#### Include
This setting is checked for the rows with Interest invoice selected in the Interest charge column. With this checkbox you determine which invoices will be released.

#### Interest days
In this column you can modify the number of days for which the interest is calculated for the respective invoice. The difference between due date and payment date is shown by default.

#### Interest charge
Here you can select how the interest should be charged after releasing interest invoicing, or if it should not be charged at all.
- Interest invoice – This means that the interest is charged on a separate interest invoice to the customer.
- Next regular invoice – This means that the interest will be charged as a separate invoice row on the next regular invoice to the customer.
- No charge – This means that the interest for this invoice should not be charged.
