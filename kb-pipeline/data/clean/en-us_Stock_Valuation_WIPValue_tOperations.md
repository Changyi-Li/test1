### The Operations tab
Under this tab you find information about planned and reported quantities, times and reported costs, and SC mark-ups for operations incorporated in the selected order and main part.
If there is a manufacturing order log for the part, you can see it under the button Manufacturing order log ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_log.png) on the row.
The tab is similar to the corresponding tab in the Post-calculation procedure.
