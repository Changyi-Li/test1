### Communication
Here you can add communication information. You can modify, delete, and add rows containing communication information for the record in question. The information is loaded from the customer. See an explanation of the different information in the help chapter for [Communication](../../Customers/CustomerRegister/bCommunication.htm) in the Customer register procedure.

#### Printout via
With this setting you determine if the printout by default should be made using a Printer or via E-mail. This setting is also loaded from the customer.
