### The List tab
Here you find a short description of the list types Calculate safety stock and Calculate reorder point.

#### Save (S)
In this column you decide which parts you want to save.

#### Annual volume
Here you see the part's annual volume.

#### Daily pace
Here you see the part's daily pace.

#### Lead time
Here you see the part's lead time.

#### Safety stock
Here you can see the part's current safety stock.

#### New safety stock
Applies to the Calculate safety stock list type. Here you see the new safety stock for the part in the default unit. The column is activated if you check the box Save.

#### Reorder point
Applies to the Calculate reorder point list type. Here you see the part's current reorder point.

#### New reorder point
Applies to the Calculate reorder point list type. Here you see the new reorder point for the part in the default unit. The column is activated if you check the box Save.

#### Diff. %
Here you see the difference between the existing stock level and the new stock level.

#### More info
Under the More info ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_more_info.png) button you find additional information about the part. This information is displayed in the columns Part type, Part code, Part category, Product group, ABC ABC codes are used to classify the range of parts by the volumes you sell. The codes are used as a scale for the parts that turn over the most money. The turnover is calculated by multiplying the price of the part by the annual volume. Parts that turn over the most money are called "A-parts", and after that, "B-parts", etc. code, Rounding quantity, and Safety time.
