### Settings and Result – Documents (list type Saved emission calculations)
The emission calculation can be printed or sent by e-mail.
Settings
- Document variant – Here you select which document variant you want to print. At present, the only available variant is the Emission calculation – Total.
Result
Here you select which part numbers you want to include in the printout. If the calculation is linked to an quote row or a customer order row, you can see information about it both in the document footer and here.
