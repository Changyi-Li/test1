### Preview
In the Preview box you can preview the document in question before printing it. If the printout consists of several pages, it is possible to scroll in the box. You can zoom the document by using Ctrl + the scroll wheel or the scroll percent in the lower right part of the box.
You you print or e-mail the document using the Print ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_print.png) and Send e-mail ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_email.png) buttons at the top of the box.
