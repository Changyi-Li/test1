### Result
In this box you see the delivery schedules you selected under the Selection tab. At the bottom of the window you see a total of the delivery schedules.
When you have selected the rows you want to include, and then printed or e-mailed the rows in question, you can approve the printouts. Mark the printouts you wish to approve in the Approve column. You execute by using the Approve button ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/StatusFinished.png).
If you need to redo the printouts, you cancel by clicking the Cancel button ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_cancel.png). The delivery schedules can then be reprinted, using the same method or a different method (printer or e-mail).

#### Include
The delivery schedules selected in this column are the ones that will be printed/sent.

#### Supplier
In this column you see the supplier number and the name of the supplier on the delivery schedule.

#### Warehouse
This column is available if the Warehouse option is installed in your system. Here you see the warehouse to which all purchase orders/purchase order suggestions on the delivery schedule belongs. If there are purchase orders/purchase order suggestions for different warehouses, these are grouped on separate delivery schedules.

#### Printing method
With this setting you decide if the delivery schedules should be printed or sent by e-mail.

#### Print log
For the list type called Existing delivery schedules you can view a print log where you see when the delivery schedule was printed and what the printout looked like.

#### EDI export
If the supplier on the delivery schedule is connected to EDI EDI is the acronym of Electronic Data Interchange. EDI is about exchanging electronic business documents with your business partners, e.g. customers and suppliers. The EDI concept can be wide and a bit unclear, and can many times be used about all types of documents which are sent electronically, even if it might be PDF files sent via e-mail or publishing business documents on a website. What we refer to as EDI – and what is traditionally meant by EDI – is structured business documents following given standards, electronically sent or received and which are compiled and interpreted automatically and that is integrated with the customer's/supplier's ERP system., it is set by default that the delivery schedules should be exported and sent via EDI, but this is possible to change for a delivery schedule in the list. There is also an option in this field to export the delivery schedule via EDI plus print/e-mail (based on the printing method in the previous field). The delivery schedule becomes exported when the printout is approved with the Approve button ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/StatusFinished.png).

#### Approve
This column appears after you have printed the delivery schedule. With the checkbox in this column you decide which of the delivery schedules should be approved, meaning the delivery schedules change status from 1 to 2.
