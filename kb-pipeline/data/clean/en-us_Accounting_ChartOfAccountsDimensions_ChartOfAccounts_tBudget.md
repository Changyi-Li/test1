### Budget
Under this tab you handle the budget information for the account in question. You can see the budget registered for the account and compare it with the result. Under this tab it is possible to create new budgets, edit or delete existing budgets. You normally register budgets using the procedure Register budget or by importing budgets.
> If you have the Fixed assets register option, you can also register a budget for a depreciation account and a value reduction account from the [Budgetary depreciation](../../FixedAssetsRegister/Depreciation/tListBudgetaryDepreciation.htm) list in the Depreciation procedure.
For the account you can handle budgets for all combinations of account strings which the account setting allows. For example, CC and CU might be allowed on the account, but not Project. For the row you are on, period details are shown in the table in the bottom part of the window.

#### Budget number
Here you select the budget number which will be handled. Next to the field you see the total annual budget for all budgets in the table below.
Budgets

#### Annual budget
Here you enter the annual budget for the account string. This is enter in whole numbers. If you enter or modify a period amount in the bottom table, this field will be updated.

#### Annual quantity
The same applies for this field as for the field above.

#### Budget chart
The budget chart you selected under the tab General is suggested for a new budget row. If you change budget chart, the period distribution of amount and quantity in the lower table of periods will also be affected.

#### Dimensions (CC, CU, Project)
One column per dimension is shown. It is only possible to enter one account string for a row. A dimension column is gray if the account has No configured in the Applied column for that dimension under the General tab. You receive a warning if a budget has been registered on both account level and on any of the dimension levels. The reason for this is to prevent the total budget being registered both on the account and divided per dimension.

#### Created/Modified by
Here you see which user has created or modified the budget for the account.

#### Date
Here you see date and time when the budget was created or modified.
Periods

#### Budget
In this column you can in the sub-columns Quantity and Amount enter or modify the distribution of the quantity and amount in the budget by period. If you make changes in these columns it means that the fields for annual budget and annual quantity in the upper table will also become updated. You can also see the accumulated amount.

#### Result
In the sub-columns in this column you can compare the budget with the result.
