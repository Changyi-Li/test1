### The Statistics list
This list is used for the purpose of following up on certain statistics in the EIM flow. For example you can follow-up on handling time per invoice for different signers and suppliers. By making a selection by date (action date) you can choose a period of time to access statistic which is more specific.

#### Number of invoices
In this column you can see a total of the number of invoices that have been handled by the signer on the respective row. The same consecutive number will not be counted more than once (if, for instance, an invoice has been managed several times by the same signer).

#### Handling time/invoice
Here you see the average handling time per invoice for each signer. At the very bottom you will see the total average time of what is displayed in the list. The number of days divided by the number of invoices shows the total average time.
