### The Detailed list
The Detailed list shows information in detail about the quotes that you have selected. It can be presented as grouped by quote or grouped by customer. The information shown here is for example delivery date, quantity, amount, and customer information. The column Excluded from statistics is marked if the quote row is not included in the statistics.
The list is loaded and displayed based on the selection and settings you have chosen. As in all lists in Monitor ERP, you can drag and drop in order to change the column to group by.
