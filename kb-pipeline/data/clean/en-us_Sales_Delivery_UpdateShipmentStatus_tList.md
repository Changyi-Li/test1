### The list in Update shipment status
In this list you see the shipments you selected.
If the shipments in the list are missing the shipping agent's shipment number and shipping agent's package number, at the same time as the status is not 5 (Booked/Printed), this will in most cases mean that the shipments have not yet been complemented/printed via nShift's web interface.
To be able to update the shipments in Monitor ERP with shipment number and package number, and get the status updated to 5 from nShift, you must have the schedule activated under Scheduling under the Selection tab.
When there is a shipment number or a package number for a shipment, you can use the Goods tracking button ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_url.png) on the function menu in order to find the shipment.
