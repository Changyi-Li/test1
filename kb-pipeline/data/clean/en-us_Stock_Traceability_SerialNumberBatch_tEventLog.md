### Event log
Under this tab an event log is shown containing log records for all that has happened with the loaded serial number or batch number, as of when the number was created up to the present. Planned events are also displayed.
There are different types of historical events:
Manufacturing
- Manufacturing, transfer to stock
- Manufacturing, material withdrawal.
Purchase
- Arrival
- Register supplier invoice – displays the monetary values. The amount is displayed as cost.
Sales
- Sales, aftermarket (quote) – also includes quotes created using Save as.
- Sales, aftermarket (customer order) – also includes orders created using Save as.
- Sales (delivery)
- Sales, aftermarket (delivery)
- Sales (invoicing) – displays the monetary values. Both cost and income are displayed. The cost is made up from the standard price on the invoice row. The invoicing is not displayed until the invoice is approved.
- Sales, aftermarket (invoicing) – the same as the above.
- Sales, customer agreement – also includes customer agreements created using Save as.
- Sales, customer agreement (agreement basis)
- Sales, customer agreement (invoicing) – displays the monetary values. Both cost and income are displayed. The cost is made up from the standard price on the invoice row. The invoicing is not displayed until the invoice is approved.
Stock
- Stock count (only shown if the balance was changed during the stock count).
Miscellaneous
- Change of owner
- Case (with status 4 or 9) – displays the monetary values The actual costs are displayed.
The planned events shown are:
- Purchase order
- Purchase (receiving inspection).
- Manufacturing
- Sales.
At the bottom of the tab you see a summary of the total amounts for cost, income, and contribution margin (CMThe contribution margin (CM) is the difference between the standard price and the sales price.) and a total percentage for contribution ration (CRThe contribution ratio (CR) is the portion of the invoice amount (sales price) that the contribution margin represents. CR is entered as a percentage.).
