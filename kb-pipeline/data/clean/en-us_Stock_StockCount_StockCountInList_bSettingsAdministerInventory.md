### Settings – Manage stock count
Here you can configure settings regarding how the reporting should be accounted.

#### Stock count date
Here you select the date when the stock count was performed. The date is saved in the stock count log. If a stock count has been previously made on the locations for the selected parts, you cannot select a stock count date that is earlier than the location's Last stock count date on each respective location.

#### Suggest saved/current balance as stock counted quantity
With this setting you decide that the saved location balance or the current balance should be suggested as stock counted quantity in the list. This applies for each location. If you do not activate this setting, zero will be suggested as stock counted quantity.

#### Show already reported rows
With this setting you determine if already reported rows (that is, history rows) should be shown for the stock count number.

#### Stock counted by
Here you see/select the person who performed the stock count. The name is shown as a signature on the stock count basis.
