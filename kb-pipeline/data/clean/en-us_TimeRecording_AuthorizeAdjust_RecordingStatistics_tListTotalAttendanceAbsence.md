### List type Total – Attendance/Work
This list is used to compare attendance between persons and how much work that has been recorded.
The list shows recorded attendance, work and absence, summarized by employee number. The list also shows overtime and flex time as well as recorded work (divided by direct and indirect work). The pauses shown are the paid breaks. The shown difference is the one between worked hours and direct+indirect work. At the bottom, you will see a total of each column in the list.
