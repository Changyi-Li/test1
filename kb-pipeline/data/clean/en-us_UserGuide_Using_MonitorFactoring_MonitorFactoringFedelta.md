## Monitor Factoring
With factoring integration you can strengthen your company’s cash flow by quickly and easily selling your customer invoices to a factoring company (Fedelta Finance). In addition to faster liquidity, this option also reduces the administrative burden of managing customer invoices. Monitor Factoring is based on modern API technology, which eliminates the manual file handling – such as invoices and payments. Fedelta can also assist with administration of for example reminders and debt collection.
