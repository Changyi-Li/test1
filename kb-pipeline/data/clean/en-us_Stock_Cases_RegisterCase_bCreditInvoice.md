### Credit invoice
If the customer should be credited or if the supplier will send you a credit invoice, you use this box.

#### Customer nonconformity
If the customer should be credited for the costs that the rejected parts have caused, this is handled in this box. You can link an existing credit invoice by entering the order number.
Using the More info button you will see the order header. There you can see information about the customer and the delivery address.
By using the preview ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_form_viewing.png), you can print or send the invoice by e-mail. A link between the case and the credit invoice is created when saving.

#### Supplier nonconformity
If the supplier is to send you a credit invoice for the rejected parts, you check Request credit. After this you enter the consecutive number the supplier credit invoice got at the registration. A validation will be made in order to check that the invoice is of type credit and belongs to the supplier registered on the case. The consecutive number is filled in automatically, if the invoice is linked to the arrival. If the order has been created separately, you can enter the consecutive number manually.
