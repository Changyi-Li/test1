### Measuring points
In this table you see the measuring points to be reported for the part on the order row. Which measuring points should be reported depends on the entered quantity to report and the measuring plan's construction on the part. The measuring points are also grouped according to the measuring plan.
Here you find the same functions as in the [Measuring points](../../../Manufacturing/Reporting/ReportMeasuringData/bMeasuringPoints.htm) table in the Report measuring data procedure in the Manufacturing module.
