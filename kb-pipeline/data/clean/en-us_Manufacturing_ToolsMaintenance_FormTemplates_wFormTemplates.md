## Form templates
This procedure is used if you have installed the Tools & Maintenance option.
In this procedure you create form templates under the Templates tab. These will then be used during calibration and maintenance of tools and machines.
A form template describes the protocol that is used to enter measuring data when calibrating tools. A form template can also be a checklist or a protocol that is used during maintenance of machines.
The form template can consist of several sections with main rows and sub-rows. Main rows work as a heading. Sub-rows can be of the types decimals, checkboxes, date fields, and text fields.
Under the Document tab you can preview what the form template will look like as a document if you print it. Under this tab you can also print the document or send it as a PDF attached to an e-mail.
You link a form template to one or multiple maintenance templates. These are created in the Basic data With "basic data" we refer to the static records in a database, for example parts, customers, users, work centers, etc. – Maintenance procedure. The maintenance templates should then be linked to maintenance plans that are registered for each tool and machine in the Maintenance plans procedure.
