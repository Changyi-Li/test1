## Maintenance plans
This procedure is part of the Tools & Maintenance option. Here you register maintenance plans for your tools and machines.
A maintenance plan describes the maintenance required for a tool or a machine, and how often it should be performed. In the maintenance plan you add one or multiple maintenance templates and the parts (tools and machines) to which the maintenance plan applies.
Read more about [tools & Maintenance](../../../UserGuide/Using/ToolsMaintenance/ToolsMaintenance.htm) under Using Monitor in the online help function.
