### The Price adjustment – Agreement list
In this list you can update certain information under the Rows tab in the Register customer agreement procedure. The columns of the list corresponds to the columns under the [Rows](../RegisterAgreementSales/tRows.htm) tab.

#### New price
Here you see the New price. The value in this field can be updated.

#### Difference – Price
The Difference – Price column is calculated as New price/Inv. interval - Price/Inv. interval.

#### Difference (%) – Price
The Difference (%) – Price column is calculated as (Future price - Price/Inv. interval)/( Price/Inv. interval).

#### New amount/Inv. interval
Here you see the new amount per invoicing interval.

#### New future quantity
The value in this field can be updated.

#### New future discount
The value in this field can be updated.

#### New future price
The value in this field can be updated.

#### New "Future valid from"
This valus is loaded from the "Future valid from" setting.
