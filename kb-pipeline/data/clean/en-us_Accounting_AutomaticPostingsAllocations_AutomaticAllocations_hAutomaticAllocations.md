### Header row

#### Number
Here you enter/select the number of the automatic allocation. The numbers are linked to a number series.

#### Name
Here you can write a descriptive text as a name. You enter name texts in the company language and they are displayed in the user’s language.

#### Type
In this field you see/select the type of the automatic allocation.
-   
Direct – This option means that the automatic allocation takes place in real time in connection with you registering the postings in the system.
-   
Period balance – This option means that the automatic allocation takes place per month based on the month's result on the account. You do the release in the Release automatic postings/allocations procedure.
-   
Accumulated balance – This option means that the automatic allocation takes place per month based on the accumulated result on the account. The release is made in the above mentioned procedure.

#### Active
With this checkbox you determine if the automatic allocation in question should be active.
