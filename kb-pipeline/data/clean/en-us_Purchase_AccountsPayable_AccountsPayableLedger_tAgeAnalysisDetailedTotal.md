### The list type Age analysis – Detailed/Total
This list type shows an age accounts payable list, detailed or total. The list includes values of overdue supplier invoices for three different time intervals. The time intervals (in days) are entered under Settings under the Selection tab. Total overdue, Not overdue, and Total accounts payable, are shown in separate columns. For each time interval you find totals for total overdue, not overdue, and total accounts payable, at the bottom of the list.
In the total list, you can also see the number of invoices. Each row in the list can be expanded to show detailed information.
The lists are grouped/total by supplier.
