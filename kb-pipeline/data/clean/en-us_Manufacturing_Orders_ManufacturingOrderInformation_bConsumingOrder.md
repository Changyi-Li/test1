### The Consuming order tab
Under this tab you can see which customer orders or manufacturing orders that, according to the net requirement calculation, will be the ones consuming the manufactured details. You can for example see information about finish date and requirement date.
By clicking the button Only show end-consumer ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_show_endconsumer.png) you will only see the end-consumer, the material requirement, manufacturing order or customer order row, which started the chain of requirements.
