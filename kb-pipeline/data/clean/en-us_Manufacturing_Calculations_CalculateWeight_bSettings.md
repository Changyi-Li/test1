### Settings

#### Calculation
With the Calculate underlying levels setting you decide if the net weight for parts in underlying levels in the part structure should be recalculated before their net weights are added together to create the total net weight for the structure part. By default the list is set to save new net weights in the part structure, but this is optional.
> Please note! Purchase parts in underlying levels will also be recalculated, but they are not displayed in the list. If a purchase part does not have a fixed weight entered (see below), it will be recalculated to zero (0) in net weight since it is not a structure part and does not have a material list.
With the Include parts with fixed weight setting you can decide to also include parts with fixed net weight in the total (default). Parts with fixed net weight will not be recalculated by the above setting.

#### Terms
With the setting called Quantity if order qty is missing you can assign an order quantity to the calculation for the parts which are missing an order quantity.
You can enter terms for Customer, Finish date, Variant code, Order number, and Warehouse (available if the Warehouse option has been installed). If you have entered a term, only the underlying parts in material lists that has that term selected on the material row, will be included in the calculation
