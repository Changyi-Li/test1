### List type Level list
This list shows supplier relationships according to the setting Parent company (supplier).
This list has two windows: Consists of and Included in.
In the Consists of window, the suppliers you have selected are displayed. If you select a supplier in this list, the suppliers that the selected supplier is included in are displayed in theIncluded in window.
The toolbar in the Included in window has a button: Load the selected supplier to “Consists of”. If you have selected a supplier in the Included in window and then click this button, the selected supplier will be highlighted in the Consists of window.
