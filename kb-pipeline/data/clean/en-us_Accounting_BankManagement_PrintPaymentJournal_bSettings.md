### Settings

#### Enter voucher date manually
The purpose of entering a voucher date manually is to direct the voucher(s) to a specific date, e.g. if they are old entries that belong to a locked period. If you activate this setting, the field Voucher date will become available, and you can enter the voucher date you wish to use. Today's date is shown by default.
> If the voucher date is entered manually, the voucher date is displayed in red in the journal to indicate that the payments will be recorded on another date than the payment date. Please note! This function should be used with caution, since it can cause differences between the accounts receivable and the general ledger!
You can also see information about the integration selected for Payments in the Voucher number series/Journals procedure. The integration options are Direct per payment date or Via journal – Total per payment day.

#### Payment types
You can choose among the following payment types: Customer invoice, Supplier invoice, and Other payment.
