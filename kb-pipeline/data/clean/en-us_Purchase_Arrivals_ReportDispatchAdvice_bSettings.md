### Settings
Here you find settings and input fields that are used when reporting dispatch advises. The information you enter in the fields is saved in the header of the dispatch advice during reporting. Before you can load the list and report dispatch advice, you must select a Dispatch date and enter a Delivery note number. The fields Delivery note number and Dispatch date, together with supplier, form a unique key used for a dispatch advice. This means that you cannot report two dispatch advices with the same supplier, delivery note number, and dispatch date.

#### Dispatch date
Here you enter the dispatch date of the received dispatch advice. This field is mandatory.

#### Delivery note number
Here you enter the delivery note number of the received dispatch advice. This field is mandatory.

#### Shipment number
Here you enter the shipment number of the received dispatch advice.

#### Link to goods tracking
Here you enter the address or link (URL) that is used during goods tracking of the received dispatch advice.

#### Suggest quantity
Here you determine which suggested quantity on the order rows in the received dispatch advice should be reported as dispatched.
- Zero (0) – With this alternative, the suggested quantity will be zero (0,00).
- Remaining on order row – With this alternative, the suggested quantity will be the remaining quantity on the order row.
- Remaining on order row minus previously dispatched quantity – With this alternative, the suggested quantity will be the remaining quantity on the order row minus the previously dispatched quantity

#### Show only main rows
With this setting you determine if only main rows should be displayed in the list. Related sub-rows in form of additional texts, alloy costs, etc. are not shown. This setting does not apply to sub-rows on fictitious parts. They are always shown.

#### Only include order rows with remaining quantity
With this setting you decide if only order rows which have a remaining quantity should be shown in the list.

#### Do not include fully adviced rows
With this setting you decide if fully adviced order rows should be excluded from the list.
