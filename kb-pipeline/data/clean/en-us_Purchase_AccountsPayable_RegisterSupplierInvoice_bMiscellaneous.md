### Miscellaneous

#### Country
Here you can see the country from which the delivery is made. By default, the country is loaded from the delivery address of the supplier. The field is used for reporting of Intrastat Intrastat is the system which gathers statistics relating to trade in products within the European Union. Gathering of Intrastat statistics is handled in the same way by all EU member states. import. It is possible to change country regardless of the status of the invoice.

#### Supplier group
Here you can see the supplier group of the invoice. It is loaded from the supplier. If you have activated the system setting Load invoice information from purchase order, the supplier group will be loaded from the purchase order selected in the Order number field on the main row. Supplier group is used as a selection term in lists. When a purchase order link has been made on a supplier invoice, this field becomes deactivated and a button appears which you can use to change supplier group (see below).

#### Change supplier group
For an order linked supplier invoice it is possible to change supplier group by using the button Change supplier group ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_change_record.png). This button is then shown to the right of the Supplier group field. It is possible to change supplier group as long as the invoice has not been final coded in the system. When you click the button, a dialog box will open where you select a new supplier group and also decide if the posting should be changed on the linked purchase order rows. By default, the setting is checked which means that the posting will be modified.

#### VAT registration number
Here you see the supplier's VAT registration number. This information is loaded from the supplier register.

#### Event log
The Event log button is only available if option EFH is activated. By clicking this button you access a log where you can see how the invoice has been handled; from registration to final recording. The items logged here are time, event, user, procedure, and a description.

#### Bookkeeping list
Under this button you can see information about the bookkeeping transactions of the invoice in question, not only the invoice’s preliminary and final recording but also the payments and rectification vouchers made for the invoice, if any. At the bottom of the list you see totals of the debit and credit columns. The information displayed is mainly the same as in the bookkeeping list in the Accounts payable ledger procedure.
The malaysian supplier must include the reference number of the customs form (e.g., Customs Form No.1 or No.9) in a self-billed e-invoice. Here you can enter information from the declaration form you received from customs when importing goods.
