### Comment/Files
Here you can add comments and link files to the invoice basis. Information entered and files linked in this box correspond to those in the [Comment/Documents](../../Orders/RegisterCustomerOrder/bCommentFiles.htm) box on a customer order, apart from the internal order text and its linked files which do not exist on invoice bases.
Please note! You have to check the Linked files setting in the Print invoice procedure in order for the file to be printed together with the invoice. This applies even though you have checked the Automatic printout setting when linking the file.
