### Behavior
In this table you add the EDI EDI is the acronym of Electronic Data Interchange. EDI is about exchanging electronic business documents with your business partners, e.g. customers and suppliers. The EDI concept can be wide and a bit unclear, and can many times be used about all types of documents which are sent electronically, even if it might be PDF files sent via e-mail or publishing business documents on a website. What we refer to as EDI – and what is traditionally meant by EDI – is structured business documents following given standards, electronically sent or received and which are compiled and interpreted automatically and that is integrated with the customer's/supplier's ERP system. behaviors that you need.
By using the buttons in the function menu you can add, insert, and delete behaviors row by row. You can also copy behaviors. If you delete a behavior, a check is made to see if it is used in any EDI configuration. If so, a dialog box appears where you can see the configurations where the behavior is used.

#### Name
Here you enter the name of the behavior. You see the name of the behavior in the EDI configurations procedure.

#### Module
Here you select to which module you want to link the behavior – Sales or Purchase.
The module determines which settings and rules will apply to the behavior as well as if you can link customers or suppliers to the behavior.

#### Included in configuration
By clicking the Included in configuration button ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_more_info.png) you see in which EDI configurations the behavior is used. You also see if the configurations are active.

#### Log
By using the button Log ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_more_info.png) you find a changelog for the behavior. Here you see who modified the information as well as when. You also see what has been changed, with the value before and after the change.
