### Budget
Under this tab you can create a budget for the part for several years. You can also see the result of the budgets.
In the upper part you add rows with budget years. In the lower part you can modify or enter the allocation by period. In the Type field you select if you are handling budgets regarding sales or purchase.
Budget can also be registered via the Calculate annual budget procedure. There you can select a year to which to save the calculated budget.
In the Invoicing log procedure you can see and follow up on sales budgets by using the list types for budget deviation analysis.

#### Type
Here you select if you want to handle budgets regarding sales or purchase.

#### Year
On the first row you add here, the current year will be suggested but it is possible to change. This means that you can create next year’s budget in advance. You can also enter a past year. The current year is shown in bold font.

#### Quantity
The quantity you enter here is the planned volume of the part which should be sold or purchased during the year in question. The default value here is 0.00. The quantity is displayed in the unit selected on the part's main row.

#### Price alternative
This field is only displayed if Sales has been selected in the Type field. In the price alternative field you select the price list that applies for the budget year in question. You can choose among the price lists registered in the company currency in the Price lists procedure.

#### Price
In this column you enter the part's price per unit. It should be entered in the company currency. You can enter the price manually, or the sales price from the selected price list will be loaded automatically.

#### Amount
The amount you see here is calculated as Quantity x Price.

#### Budget chart
Here you decide if the entered amount for annual budget should be distributed evenly over all periods or if they should be distributed based on a budget chart. Budget charts are managed in the Budget charts procedure.

#### Budget
Below the Budget column in the lower part of the box you can in the columns Quantity and Amount enter or modify the distribution of the quantity and amount in the budget by period. If you modify the quantities here, the quantity will be updated in the Quantity field in the upper section as well. If you modify the amounts here, the amount will be updated in the Amount field in the upper section as well. You also see the distribution in percent, accumulated quantity, and accumulated amount.

#### Result
Below the Result column in the right section you see the sub-columns Quantity, Amount, Accumulated quantity, Accumulated amount, and Accumulated difference (amount). The final column is used to show the result per period for the part.
If it is a sales budget you will see data from the invoicing log, total per month.
If it is a purchase budget you will see data either from the supplier invoice log or from the purchase statistics, total per month. This is determined by the system setting Create invoice basis at arrival of. If Purchase order or Purchase order (subc.), or both have been configured for the setting in question, then data from the supplier invoice log will be displayed. If you have not activated any alternative for the system setting, you will see data from the purchase statistics.
