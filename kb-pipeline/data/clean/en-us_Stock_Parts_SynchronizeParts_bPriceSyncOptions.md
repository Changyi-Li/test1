### Price sync options
Here you can choose to synchronize prices on parts. Monitor ERP will convert the currency if it differs between the companies. This conversion will be based in the exchange rate in the sending company.

#### Sync from
The following prices can be synchronized from the sending company:
- Supplier price
- Future supplier price
- Standard price
- Future standard price
- Price list
- Future price list
- Supplier price
- Future supplier price

#### Price list
If you have selected price list, you should here choose which price list to synchronize from. If you choose a price list in another currency, you will also be able to select Rate type.

#### Rate type
Rate type based on a price list with a different currency.

#### Sync to
The following prices can be synchronized to the receiving company:
- Supplier price
- Future supplier price
- Standard price
- Future standard price
- Price list
- Future price list

#### Price adjustment
Here you can enter an adjustment in percent for the price in the receiving company. For example, if the price in the receiving company should be increased with 10 percent, you should enter 10,00%. It is also possible to do a negative price adjustment by entering a negative percentage.

#### New
Here you decide if data for new parts should be synchronized.

#### Existing
Here you decide if data for existing parts should be updated in the synchronization.
