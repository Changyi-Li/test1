### Delivery info in Monitor
In this box you see the deliveries sent to the customer for the marked delivery schedule or for the marked part in delivery schedule.
You use the information in this box and in the Reconciliation info from delivery schedule box, to form an opinion regarding what you have delivered, compared to what the customer regards as received. This is information which makes it possible for you as a user to reconcile the delivery schedule.
In the field Number of deliveries to show you can enter how many performed deliveries you want to see in the box. If a large amount of deliveries have been made to the customer, then it is good to limit how many deliveries to show here due to performance issues. The default value here is 50 deliveries.
