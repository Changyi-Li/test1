### Costs
Under this tab you handle the gross list of costs which apply to all case types. There are two costs included as standard that you can use or delete. You can also register own costs. Please note that costs for cases also include costs for activities and manufacturing, but these are not included as costs under this tab.
Enter Cost and check the D box if the cost should be charged. To charge the cost means that the cost will not be included in the total cost for the case.
