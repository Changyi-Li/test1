### The Standard list
This list is available in different presentations showing information about the personnel which can be updated.
Presentations:
- General – In the list you can update: name, initials, department, category, and language.
- Contact information – In the list you can update: phone, mobile phone, internal phone, e-mail, private mobile, and private phone.
- Next of kin information – In the list you can update: next of kin, phone to next of kin, mobile phone to next of kin, and e-mail to next of kin.
- Home address – In the list you can update: address rows, zip code, city, region, and country.
