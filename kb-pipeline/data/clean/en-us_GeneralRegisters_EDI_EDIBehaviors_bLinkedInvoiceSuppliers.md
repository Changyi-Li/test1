### Linked supplier numbers on invoice
In this box you find EDI EDI is the acronym of Electronic Data Interchange. EDI is about exchanging electronic business documents with your business partners, e.g. customers and suppliers. The EDI concept can be wide and a bit unclear, and can many times be used about all types of documents which are sent electronically, even if it might be PDF files sent via e-mail or publishing business documents on a website. What we refer to as EDI – and what is traditionally meant by EDI – is structured business documents following given standards, electronically sent or received and which are compiled and interpreted automatically and that is integrated with the customer's/supplier's ERP system. behaviors for the Purchase module.
Sometimes the supplier on the purchase order is not the supplier that sends the invoice. To be able to handle this in a good way during export of purchase orders and import of supplier invoices, you must separate the supplier on order and supplier number on invoice in two different boxes.
You add the supplier on the order in the box Linked suppliers. If the supplier on the order is not the same as the invoice supplier set as Supplier code invoice on the order, you add the invoice supplier in the box Linked supplier numbers on invoice. A supplier being linked as invoice supplier does not mean it is automatically configured for EDI invoices. It only means that it is the supplier who will be set as invoice sender on the order. Linked invoice suppliers are only used in connection with the import.
If the supplier on the order and the invoice supplier are one and the same, you only have to add the supplier in the Linked suppliers box.
At the bottom you find a total of rows with suppliers added to the table. You can also use the navigation buttons to move between the rows.
