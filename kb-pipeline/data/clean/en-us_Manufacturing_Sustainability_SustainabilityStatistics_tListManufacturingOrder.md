### The Manufacturing order list

#### Order number
Here you can see the manufacturing order number.

#### Warnings (W)
If warnings exist, you can click the button to view the warnings. The warnings you have not clicked on are displayed with a warning sign ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/warning.png) on the button. The warnings that you have clicked are displayed with an exclamation mark ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/warning_validation.png). Warnings are not saved in the statistics.

#### Part number
Here you can see the part number of the main part in the order, or the selected part (depending on the list type).

#### Name
Here you see/enter the name of the part.

#### Part type (T)
Here you see a symbol indicating which type of part the part is. When you hover over the symbol, a tooltip will display the part type in text form.

#### Finish date
Here you can see the actual finish date of the order which it received when reporting.

#### Emissions
Here you see the part's current emissions.

#### Include
By default, all records are included in the statistics calculation. Here you can deselect individual records by unchecking the respective box, or deselect all underlying records by unchecking the box in the column heading.

#### Status
Here you can see the status of the order.

#### Warehouse (WH)
Applies if you have installed the Warehouse option). If you change to another warehouse you will see the WH column. On the rows which belong to another warehouse than the selected warehouse you will see a symbol ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_warehouses_alt.png) in the column. A tooltip for the symbol informs you of which warehouse the row belongs to. Values and texts in all columns for these rows are displayed in italics.
In many of the procedures you can change the warehouse which you will be working in by using the Companies/Warehouses button ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_warehouses.png) in the toolbar of the procedure. It is also possible to generally change in which warehouse to work. This is applied to all procedures.This is done in the desktop backstage.. In registration procedures for quotes, inquiries, different orders, and invoice bases, you can in a field select to which warehouse the record belongs.
Columns that are displayed also after the statistics calculation

#### Finish date
Here you see the part's finish date for the order.

#### Emissions
The part’s saved CO2e value.

#### Planned quantity
Here you can see the planned quantity of the part on the order.

#### Reported quantity
Here you can see the reported quantity of the part on the order.

#### Planned emissions, part
The part’s CO2e value x Planned quantity.

#### Planned emissions, order quantity
The order’s planned emissions in kg CO2e calculated based on the order’s material structure and operations.

#### Planned total emission for reported quantity
The planned emissions shown in kg CO2e for the reported quantity. It is calculated based on the order’s material structure and operations.

#### Emissions based on reporting
Emissions based on reported time and quantity recalculated in kg CO2e.

#### Total emissions diff.
Here you see the difference between planned and reported emissions. It is shown in kg CO2e.

#### Total emission difference per unit
Here you see the total emission difference per unit. This is displayed in CO2e kg/unit.
