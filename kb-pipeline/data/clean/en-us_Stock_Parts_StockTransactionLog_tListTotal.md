### The Total list
This list shows total information about stock transactions in the stock transaction log for the selected parts. The list can be total by log date, actual date, part, and part/period. In the list you can expand the rows in order to see detailed information corresponding to the detailed list.

#### Withdrawn quantity
The calculation of Withdrawn quantity is based on the following transaction types: Delivery, Direct withdrawal, Case/posterior rejection, Register invoice directly, Material withdrawal for manufacturing order, Delivery (stock order), Undone reporting – Material, Undone delivery, Withdrawal (tools), Credit supplier invoice, and Return order.
