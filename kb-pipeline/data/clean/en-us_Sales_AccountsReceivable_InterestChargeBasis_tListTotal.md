### The Total list
This list shows the same information as the detailed list, but it is shown as total per customer. It is not possible to update any information or release any invoice bases in this total list. You can expand the rows to show the detailed information.
In the lower part of the window you see the interest amounts per customer in a bar chart. You can configure what should be shown under the button Chart ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_chart.png).
