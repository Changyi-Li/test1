## Basic data – Part
In this procedure you add and handle basic data for your parts.
Basic dataWith "basic data" we refer to the static records in a database, for example parts, customers, users, work centers, etc. is:
- Activities – for a structured way of working with parts.
- Activity templates – for a structured way of working with parts.
- Units – the units that should be used for parts in the system.
- Part codes – used to group parts in the part register, e.g. in order to divide parts into different qualities, types, variants, etc.
- Other part identities – e.g. GTIN8, GTIN12, GTIN13 and GTIN14 (Global Trade Item Number) and SGTIN (Serial Global Trade Item Number).
- CN codes – (goods codes used within EU) and determine how they should be reported.
- ABCABC codes are used to classify the range of parts by the volumes you sell. The codes are used as a scale for the parts that turn over the most money. The turnover is calculated by multiplying the price of the part by the annual volume. Parts that turn over the most money are called "A-parts", and after that, "B-parts", etc. codes – used to determine how the parts will be classified according to their annual volumes and determine the stock count intervals.
- Alloy codes and Mark-ups – make it possible to update the additional costs of parts.
- Package typePackage type describes what kind of package that is used, such as "pallet" for EU pallets, "box" for cardboard boxes etc. – here you register the package types that should be used in the system. The package type describes what kind of package is concerned.
- Goods typeGoods type describes what kind of part it is, for example machine parts, electronics, etc. Goods type is printed on shipping documents. – used to declare/present the goods type in connection to transport.
- Inspection templates – used when variable receiving inspection for parts is applied. Here you register inspection templates which you then link to purchased parts in the Part register.
- Forecast deduction – interval settings regarding forecast deduction for sales forecasts, which can then be selected for a part when the deduction method Periodic intervals has been selected on the part in the Part register.
- ISO codes for units – ISO codes for units which can be linked to the units under the Units tab in the procedure.
- Exposure time – exposure times for parts, e.g., Moisture sensitivity level (MSL).
