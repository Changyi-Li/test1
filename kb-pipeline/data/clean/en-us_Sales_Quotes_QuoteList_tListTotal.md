### The Total list
The Total list shows total information about the quotes that you have selected. It can be presented as total by quote or total by customer. In the lower part of the tab you will see the amount in chart form. You can expand the rows to show the detailed information.
The list is loaded and displayed based on the selection and settings you have chosen. As in all lists in Monitor ERP, you can drag and drop in order to change the column to group by.
