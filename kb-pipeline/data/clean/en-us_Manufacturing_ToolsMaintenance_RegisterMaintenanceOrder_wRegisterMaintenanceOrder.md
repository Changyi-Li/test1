## Register maintenance order
This procedure is part of the Tools & Maintenance option. Here you register maintenance orders for your tools and machines.
Maintenance orders are used to plan and report time and material for different types of maintenance.
A maintenance order is always linked to the serial number on which the maintenance is carried out, and to the maintenance template which describes the maintenance operation.
When a maintenance order is registered you can choose to use operations and material from any part, as well as utilize variant codes and configured parts. This enables you to register maintenance orders seamlessly. When the maintenance order has been saved, you can supplement and modify operations and material, and instructions can be added.
Maintenance orders are a type of manufacturing order, and are based on the basic type Maintenance. If needed, you can create multiple order types for that basic type. This is done under the Manufacturing order tab in the Order types procedure. Since maintenance orders are a type of manufacturing order, you report time and material in the regular reporting procedures for manufacturing orders.
The form for maintenance is reported in the Report maintenance procedure.
For purchase of maintenance – for example, calibration of measuring tools – the maintenance order must include a subcontract for the calibration.
