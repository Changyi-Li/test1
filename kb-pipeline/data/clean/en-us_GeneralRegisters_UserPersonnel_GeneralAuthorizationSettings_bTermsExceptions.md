### Terms (exceptions)
In this box you add the terms that should apply to the selected authorization limit in the exception.

#### Based on
Here you determine on what the term should be based. You can choose Supplier group or Supplier.

#### From, To
Here you enter or choose the selection on which authorization limit exceptions should be based. You can enter an interval for Supplier group and Supplier in this field.

#### Active
Here you determine whether the term should be active and used.
