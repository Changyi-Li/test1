### The Detailed list
This list shows detailed information about the inquiries that you have selected. The list can be grouped by inquiry or supplier.

#### Delivery date
In this field you can enter the delivery date. This date always refers to the date when the delivery should arrive to you.

#### Price each
Here you can see the part’s price each. If you make changes in this field and save, the amount will also be updated. In order to see the updated amount, you must reload the list.

#### Excluded
In this column all inquiry rows set as Exclude from statistics are marked.
