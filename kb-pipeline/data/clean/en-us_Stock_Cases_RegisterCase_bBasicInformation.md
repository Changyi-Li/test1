### Basic information
In this box you fill in basic information for the case type.

#### Status
Here you select the status of the case. Automatic status changes are always updated upwards. The status of a case is, e.g., never changed back to Printed if it already has a higher status. The different status steps are:
1 – Registered
2 – Printed
3 – Started
4 – Closed
9 – Historical
99 – Rejected.

#### Case category
By clicking the Category selection button ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_more_info.png), you can select a category if categories have been registered in the Categories procedure. If no categories are registered, you can type as you please in this field. Categories can be used as a selection term in different lists. Read more about how categories can be created/constructed in the online help function for the [Categories](../../../GeneralRegisters/Categories/Categories/wCategories.htm) procedure.

#### Customer's/Supplier's Reference
You can select the person who is the reference for the customer or supplier on the case.

#### Customer's/Supplier's case number
It is possible to enter the customer's or the supplier's case number for the following case types: Customer nonconformity and Supplier nonconformity. For the customer's case number you can enter 40 characters. For the supplier's case number you can enter 20 characters.

#### Our Reference
Here you can select the person to be your own reference for the case.

#### Case date
Here you enter the date that will be the registration date or start date of the case. You can enter a date which is either earlier or later than the date when the case is created.

#### Finish date
Here you enter the date that will be the finish date of the case.
Initial delivery
Under the Initial delivery button, you enter the information that applies to the initial delivery to which the nonconformity part belongs. The fields are filled in automatically if there is data available. The fields are available for the case types customer nonconformity and supplier nonconformity.

#### Order number
Here you enter the order number of the initial delivery to which the part belongs. The number you enter is validated against the customer/supplier.

#### Arrival reported/Delivery quantity
Here you enter the quantity the initial arrival/delivery consisted of.

#### Delivery date
Enter the date when the initial delivery was made.

#### Project
Here you enter the project to which the initial delivery belongs.

#### Invoice/consecutive number
Here you enter the invoice number that was used when the delivery was invoiced, alternatively, you enter the consecutive number the invoice of the arrival was assigned.
Initial manufacturing
By clicking the Initial manufacturing button you can enter information about the initial purchase.

#### Manufacturing order
If the part is manufactured by you, you can link the manufacturing order information to the case. If the part is delivered to you, you can enter the manufacturing order of the initial manufacturing of the nonconformity part.

#### Operation
Here you can enter the operation number, if the nonconformity can be traced to a certain operation.

#### Work center
Here you can enter the work center, if the nonconformity can be traced to a certain operation.

#### Manufactured quantity
Here you can enter the manufactured quantity where the initial production of the claimed part took place. This is a numerical field with two decimals. If the part number has been entered you will see the part's standard unit in the field. By default, here is no value in the field. The field becomes automatically filled in with the finished quantity of manufacturing order if it is finished.

#### Manufacturing date
Here you can enter the finish date when the initial production of the claimed part took place. This is empty by default. If there is a finish date from the manufacturing order, this is filled in automatically.
Initial purchase
By clicking the Initial purchase button you can enter information about the initial purchase order.

#### Purchase order
If the part is purchased, you can link the purchase order to the case. If the order contains multiple part numbers, you select which part number/numbers the case concerns.

#### Arrival reported qty
The arrival reported quantity of the part which the claim concerns. Enter the quantity with two decimals. If the part number has been entered you will see the part's standard unit in the field. By default, here is no value in the field.

#### Delivery date
Here the delivery date for the initial arrival is shown.
