### Title bar
At the top of the desktop in Monitor ERP you in find the title bar.
![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/UserGuide/TitleBar1.png)
From the left you’ll find the functions [Window](../GeneralFeatures/Window.htm), [Message center](../GeneralFeatures/MessageCenter.htm), [Clipboard](../GeneralFeatures/ClipBoard.htm) and [Find procedure](../GeneralFeatures/ProcedureSearch.htm) (Alt + D).
In the middle there is a title displaying information about company, database number, warehouse, accounting year, period, and logged-in user.
To the right of the title there is a button for [user settings](../GeneralFeatures/UserSettings.htm), a button which takes you to the home page for this online help, and finally, the function [Monitor search](../GeneralFeatures/MonitorSearch.htm) (Ctrl + F).
