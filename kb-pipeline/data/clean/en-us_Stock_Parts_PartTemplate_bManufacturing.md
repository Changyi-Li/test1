### Manufacturing
In this box you configure settings regarding manufacturing of parts in this template.

#### Production engineer
Here you select a person from the personnel records which should be used by default as responsible for BOM and routing for parts in this template.

#### Sales overhead
Here you enter a default AFFO Sales OH is a percentage mark-up of the manufacturing cost that describes the overhead expenses in addition to the manufacturing itself. These are administrative costs and selling expenses. Manufacturing cost + Sales OH = Cost price (the limit where the company starts making money). mark-up on the parts in this template. This applies to the Manufactured part type.

#### Profit
Here you enter a default Profit The profit mark-up is a percentage mark-up on the cost price which will generate a suggested quote price. mark-up on the parts in this template. This applies to the Manufactured part type.

#### Default transport label
With this setting you determine the default type of transport label to print for parts in this template when they are to be manufactured. Does not apply to the Fictitious part type.
