### Invoicing/Payment plans
In the Invoicing/Payment plans box, you register the invoicing plans and payment plans that you want to be able to use in the system.

#### Number
Enter a number for the plan.

#### Name
Here you can write a descriptive text as a name. You enter name texts in the company language and they are displayed in the user’s language.

#### Type
Here you decide if the plan should be possible to use as an Invoicing plan, a Payment plan, or Both.

#### Active
Here you determine if the invoicing plan should be active or not. If you deactivate this setting, it means you cannot select the plan in question in, for example, the Register customer order or Register purchase order procedures.
