### The Detailed list
This list displays a detailed stock count log. It can be presented as grouped by part or by part code. The list shows the stock counted balance and stock count differences in the part's unit that you have selected under the Selection tab.
If the stock count basis was created with stock count status Under stock count with saved balance, you will see the saved balance and when the balance was saved (when the basis was created).
The value per piece for the part is shown in the price column and in the value column you will see value change of the stock count.
At the bottom of the list you see a total of the entire value change in the list, of which positive difference and negative difference.
