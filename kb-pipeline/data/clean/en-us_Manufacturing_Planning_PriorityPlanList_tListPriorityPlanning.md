### The Priority plan list
The list is loaded and displayed based on the selection and settings you have chosen. As in all lists in Monitor ERP, you can drag and drop in order to change the column to group by.
This list consists of the same columns as the [priority plan](../PriorityPlanning/bPriorityPlan.htm) box in the Priority planning procedure. The list is always grouped by work center.
You cannot update any information in this list.
