### Invoice basis
In this box you can see all invoice bases for the customer.
Using the button ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_link.png) you can go to the procedure Register invoice directly for the selected invoice basis in the box.
By using the Show document button ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_form_viewing.png) you can view the actual document for the selected row in the box.
You can also see the total number of records, total invoice amount, CM The contribution margin (CM) is the difference between the standard price and the sales price., and CR The contribution ratio (CR) is the portion of the invoice amount (sales price) that the contribution margin represents. CR is entered as a percentage.. The CM and CR calculation is based on Standard price.
