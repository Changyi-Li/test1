### Price comparison
Settings only available when you have chosen the Existing calculation list type.

#### Comparative price
To compare prices for parts in existing calculations with another price, you can here select a price alternative to compare with. The default option here is standard price.

#### Price list
In this field you can choose a price list if you selected the alternative Price list in the setting above.

#### Rate type
Here you enter the rate type that should apply for the price.
