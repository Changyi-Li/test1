### The Standard list
In this list you find information about the personnel. It can for example be names, addresses, and comments. The list is sorted by employee number. If you make the list possible to update ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_edit_list.png) (Ctrl + U) you can change the information described below for each presentation of the list.
Presentations:
- General – This presentations contains general information about the personnel. In the list you can update: name, initials, department, category, and language.
- Contact information – This presentation contains contact information of the personnel. In the list you can update: name, phone number, mobile phone number, and e-mail address.
- Next of kin information – This presentation contains information about next of kin to members of the personnel. In the list you can update: name, phone number, mobile phone number, and e-mail address to the next of kin.
- Home address – This presentation contains information about the home addresses of the personnel. In the list you can update: address rows, zip code, city, region, and country. "Country" determines the address format that the person uses.
