### The list Operations – Detailed
The list is loaded and displayed based on the selection and settings you have chosen. As in all lists in Monitor ERP, you can drag and drop in order to change the column to group by.
This list shows information in detail about reporting made for operations. The information displayed is reported quantity, reported setup time and unit time, rejected quantity, reported setup cost and unit cost. In the User column you see which logged in user who performed the reporting. If the module Time recording is used, you will in the Signing employee number column see the operator who performed the reporting and in Reporting employee number you will see the employee number of the machine. To the far right you can see comments, if any, entered for the operation during reporting. In the column Type of reporting you see if it is reporting, recording, or adjustment.
The list is grouped according to selected presentation. Grouped by date, Grouped by work center, and Grouped by manufacturing order, are the presentations available as standard. When grouping by date you can also select to group dates by day, week, month, quarter, or year.
