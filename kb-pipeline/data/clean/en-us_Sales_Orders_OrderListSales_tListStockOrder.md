### The Stock order list
This list is available if you have the option Warehouse. In the list you find detailed information about the stock orders for sales that you have selected. Here you for example see ordered quantity, delivered quantity, remaining quantity, quantity in transit (from the sending warehouse), and arrival reported quantity (in the receiving warehouse).
You cannot update any information in this list.
The list is loaded and displayed based on the selection and settings you have chosen. As in all lists in Monitor ERP, you can drag and drop in order to change the column to group by.
