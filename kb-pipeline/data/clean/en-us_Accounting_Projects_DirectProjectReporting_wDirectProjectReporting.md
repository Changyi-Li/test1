## Direct project reporting
In this procedure you can register costs/income and/or activities on a project. This is useful in different contexts, for example to report hours or costs on a project, where is has not yet arrived in the shape of supplier invoices, customer invoices, etc. Project activities recorded in the Recording terminal and records modified in the procedure Authorize/Adjust recording, will create records in the procedure.
In this procedure you can load reporting items previously made and adjust certain information in them. A consecutive number is saved together with the information, for each reporting item you save. When you have entered the information and saved, the procedure will empty of all info and the cursor is placed in the record selector.
There is a number series for the reporting items in the Number series procedure.
If you report on a project with status 1 (Registered) the status becomes upgraded to 3 (In progress).
> If dependencies between activities/phases are used, then there might be limitations deciding that phases or activities must be performed in a specific order.
