### Settings – Check standard price
View

#### Show only records where standard price is missing
With this setting you decide if only parts that have no standard price should be displayed in the list. This setting is activated by default.
Price alternative

#### Standard price
In this setting you can choose between Standard price and Future standard price. This makes it possible for you to see how a changed standard price would affect the contribution ratio of the order backlog. The default option is Standard price.
