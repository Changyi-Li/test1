### The List tab
This list shows the price development for the selected parts. It also shows the price development in a [chart](bCharts.htm).

#### Include
This setting determines the parts for which prices should be included in the chart.

#### Category
Here you see the category of the price.

#### First price in period
Here you see the part's first price in the category in the selected period. The price does not include the setup price.

#### Last price in period
Here you see the part's last price in the category in the selected period. The price does not include the setup price.

#### Price difference (PD %)
Here you see the price difference in percent between the first and last price in the period.

#### Total amount, using first price
Here you see the total amount when using the first price. It is calculated as: First price in period x Actual quantity.

#### Total amount, using last price
Here you see the total amount when using the last price. It is calculated as: Last price in period x Actual quantity.

#### Actual quantity
Here you see the total quantity of the part on all invoices in the period.

#### Actual amount
Here you see the actual amount. It is calculated as: The part's price on all invoices x Actual quantity.
The actual amount includes both setup prices and discounts.
