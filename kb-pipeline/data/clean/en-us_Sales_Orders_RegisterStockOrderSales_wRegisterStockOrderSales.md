## Register stock order – Sales
This procedure is part of the option Warehouse. Here you can create stock orders for sales (customer order). In connection with this, a corresponding stock order for purchase (purchase order) is also created which is linked to this stock order. Stock orders for sales can be also be created in the Register stock order – Purchase procedure in the corresponding way.
Here you can also change or delete existing stock orders for sales. If you delete a stock order, it means that the linked stock order for purchase will be deleted as well. Any linked (normal) purchase orders or manufacturing orders will not be deleted.
If you add/delete or change a stock order row, then the corresponding will also be made on the linked stock order for purchase. If there is no linked (normal) purchase order or manufacturing order to the row, you can change it as long as it has not been delivered or printed in a pick list.
You can add extra fields for this procedure in the Extra fields procedure. If such fields already have been created, these will be available under the Extra fields tab.
In the Extra fields procedure you can to add extra fields for order rows. If such fields already have been created, these will be available under the Extra fields button on order rows.
