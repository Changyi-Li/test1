### Accounting order
Under this tab you see a voucher for resetting the VAT for the VAT period. All accounts linked to VAT rows for output or input VAT are compiled. On the voucher a posting is made for accounts for output VAT and input VAT belonging to the VAT report in question. This posting will reset the balance for these accounts.
If the total VAT becomes a debit balance this will be recorded against the standard account for VAT recoverable. Otherwise the difference between the accounts will be recorded on the standard account for tax deduction.
