### Linked invoice customers
In this box you find EDI EDI is the acronym of Electronic Data Interchange. EDI is about exchanging electronic business documents with your business partners, e.g. customers and suppliers. The EDI concept can be wide and a bit unclear, and can many times be used about all types of documents which are sent electronically, even if it might be PDF files sent via e-mail or publishing business documents on a website. What we refer to as EDI – and what is traditionally meant by EDI – is structured business documents following given standards, electronically sent or received and which are compiled and interpreted automatically and that is integrated with the customer's/supplier's ERP system. behaviors for the Sales module.
It is rather common that an invoice should not be sent to the customer on the order. To be able to handle this in a good way during import of customer orders, you must separate customer on order and customer number on invoice in two different boxes.
You add the customer on the order in the box Linked customers. If the customer on the order is not the same as the customer set as Customer code invoice on the order, you add the invoice customer (customer code invoice) in the box Linked invoice customers. A customer being linked as invoice customer does not mean it is automatically configured for EDI invoices. It only means that it is the customer who will be set as invoice recipient on the order. Linked invoice customers are only used in connection with the import.
If the customer on the order and the invoice customer are one and the same, you only have to add the customer in the Linked customers box.
A customer that is not the customer on the order but is both invoice customer during import of EDI orders and recipient of invoices during export of EDI invoices, must be added in the box Linked invoice customers as well as in the box Linked customers.
At the bottom you find a total of rows with customers added to the table. You can also use the navigation buttons to move between the rows.
