### Result
In the Result box, you will see one row per journal number. A new journal number is generated for each new accounting period. This applies to both detailed and total integration. When you mark a journal, it will be displayed in the box to the right. In the Period column you see the accounting period to which the journal belongs. When you print, all journals will be included.
It is possible to cancel the revenue calculation journal. This will create a reversal voucher, and any accrual record will be removed.
If you have released the accrual record, you cannot cancel the journal. The period/voucher number series must also be open.
