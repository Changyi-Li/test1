### The list Part's future delivery reliability – Total
This list type displays detailed information about the future internal delivery reliability for parts when a new New finish date has been calculated via the Net requirement calculation You use the net requirement calculation to perform requirements planning based on the customer order backlog, as well as any existing sales forecasts.. You can group the list by work center or by actual (reporting) date. The list can also be presented as a chart in the lower part of the window.

#### Calculated columns:
Total count = On time (count) + Late (count)
On time (%) = (On time (count) / Total count) x 100
Late (%) = (Late (count) / Total count) x 100
