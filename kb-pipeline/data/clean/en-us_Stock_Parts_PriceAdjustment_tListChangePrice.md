### The Change price list
This list type is used to change a certain price for a part, supplier, or customer. Parts, customers, and suppliers are managed based on the selections made and how the settings are configured. After having made the selection you load the data and you will see a simple list for each part and price under the List tab.

#### New price
In this column you see the value calculated based on the adjustment. You can manually enter a new price for each part.

#### Difference
Here you see the difference between the new price and the current price.

#### Save
Check this box if you wish to save the part row. A price change log will be created if you save the row.
