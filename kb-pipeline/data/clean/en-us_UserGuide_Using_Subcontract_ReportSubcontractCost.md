### Report subcontracting cost
You can report or adjust actual costs for subcontracts on manufacturing orders after you have received the supplier invoices. For example, when the invoice does not specify the setup cost and the unit cost in detail. Then you use the Report subcontract cost procedure. In that procedure you can load a list where you can update Setup price, Total unit price, or Price per unit.
