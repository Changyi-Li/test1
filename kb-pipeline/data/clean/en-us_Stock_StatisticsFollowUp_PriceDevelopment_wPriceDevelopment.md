## Price development
In this procedure you can see the price development for a selected period.
You can compare to see if a price has gone up or down between two dates, based on an actual number of sold or purchased parts.
The prices that can be compared are:
- Supplier price
- Future supplier price
- Customer price
- Future customer price
- Standard price
- Future standard price
- Mean price
There is also a chart displaying trends for the sales prices, purchase prices, and total sales amount.
