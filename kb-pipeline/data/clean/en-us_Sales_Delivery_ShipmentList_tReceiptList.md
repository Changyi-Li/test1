### List type Receipt list
This list type displays information about shipments to be picked up and you can use the list to print the shipment receipt document. You leave the shipment receipt document to the driver. The driver can then check which shipments they are expected to pick up, tick them off and then sign the document with their name and the vehicle’s license number.
You can also update the pickup information in the Register Shipment procedure.
The list can be updated making it possible to mark the shipments as picked up in the system (and not only on the printed document).
These fields are updateable to enable registering the shipments as picked up:

#### Picked up
Here you see if the shipment has been picked up. The following options are available:
- Yes
- No

#### Picked up date
Here you can enter the date when the shipment was picked up.

#### Picked up by
Here you can enter who picked up the shipment, that is, the name of the driver.

#### License plate
Here you can enter the license plate number for the vehicle that picked up and transported the shipment.
