### The list type Delivery reliability of subcontract – Detailed
This list shows detailed information about arrival reported order rows for subcontract and whether they were delivered on time or not. The list also shows the number of days that differs between the selected delivery date and the actual delivery date. The list shows a sub-total in percent, the quantity per grouping alternative, and as a total for the entire list, of order rows that have been arrival reported too early, on time, or too late.
