### The Total time – Detailed list
The list is loaded and displayed based on the selection and settings you have chosen. As in all lists in Monitor ERP, you can drag and drop in order to change the column to group by.
The list shows planned and reported quantity, planned and reported total time, and efficiency factor (E-factor) for the total time. The list is grouped by work center or by actual finish date.
At the bottom you see a total of quantity, times, and E-factor.
