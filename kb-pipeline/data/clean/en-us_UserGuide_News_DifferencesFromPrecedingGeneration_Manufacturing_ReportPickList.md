### Report pick list
- This procedure is a reporting procedure for material. It requires that you have created a pick list with a certain number in an earlier step. The pick list number is the only thing you need to enter to load material rows, and the reporting takes place one pick list at a time.
- The sorting of the material rows is the same as on the pick list.
- It is also possible to enter which person who performed the reporting and on which date (if the physical withdrawal took place on another day than today).
