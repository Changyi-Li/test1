### The Level list tab
Under this tab you find information in all levels in the structure of the selected order and main part. Information is displayed about planned and reported quantity, reported costs, and value of transfer to stock for each included part.
By clicking the button ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_link.png) on each row, you can link to the part under the respective tabs Direct material, Order oriented M-parts, or Stock driven M-parts.
The tab is similar to the corresponding tab in the Post-calculation procedure.
