### The Report remaining list
In this list you always report manufacturing orders with remaining quantity on all levels. One row per manufacturing order will be displayed in the list. The remaining quantity can differ between different nodes, operations, and material.
Only the manufacturing orders for which the Include checkbox is marked, will be reported when you save. It is not possible to include orders that have a pool work center as planned work center for any of the operations. The reporting cannot be made before the operation is delegated to some of the pool work center's related work centers.
