### Sales
Under this tab you will see standard accounts related to the Sales module.

#### Type of account
Here you will see the standard account type. You can expand the row to see information about customer groups. There you can add an exception account as well as see the dimensions for the customer groups.

#### Standard account
Here the standard account is entered/displayed.

#### Dimensions
The account can be configured to handle dimensions. If the row in question has a mandatory dimension in the chart of accounts, a warning will appear if no dimension has been entered. However, you can save without having entered a dimension. Dimensions Dimensions are used by large companies in their accounting in order to divide up activities and make it easier to track internal results. An account is a dimension, although large companies usually use the dimensions cost center (CC), cost unit (CU) and project. In addition to these you can create other dimensions in Monitor ERP based on your own operational follow-up. are handled in the Dimensions procedure.
