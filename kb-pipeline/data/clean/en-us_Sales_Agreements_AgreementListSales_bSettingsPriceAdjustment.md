### Settings – Price adjustment – Agreement
General

#### Pre-select "Save"
Here you decide if the Save checkbox on the row should be marked by default.
Adjustment – Price

#### Suggest future price as new price/interval
This setting is activated by default. If this checkbox is marked, the other price adjustment settings are deactivated and the Future price on the agreement row will be suggested as New price/interval when the list is loaded.

#### Adjust with
In this field you select how the price should be adjusted: by percent or amount. The default option is percent. A value field is shown to the right where you can enter a percentage or an amount. If this field is left with the default value 0.22, no adjustment will be made, and the price in the list must be manually adjusted part by part.
The formula for copying new price will be: price to copy from × adjust with × exchange rate.

#### Number of decimals in new price
Here you enter how many decimals should be saved/managed in the new price. The default option here is 2 decimals. It is possible to use a maximum of 6 decimals.
You can also enter negative decimals to round-off the new price to the nearest immediate 10 or higher. For example, -1 will be rounded to the nearest ten, -2 will round-off to the nearest hundred, etc.

#### Number of significant digits in new price
Enter the number of significant digits according to which new prices should be rounded. This field is left empty by default, which means that no round-off will take place for integers. For example:
- One significant digit will round off the integer 1,453 to 1,000.
- Two significant digit will round off the integer 1,453 to 1,500, etc.

#### Apply on future price
If you check this setting, the settings configured under the Adjustment – Price heading will also apply in the New future price column.
For example, if you want to adjust the agreement by a 2% in and activate Apply on future price, the previously entered future price will be adjusted upward by 2%.
Valid from

#### Include agreements with "Future valid from" date up to
Agreements with a "future valid from" date up to (and including) the date you enter here, will be loaded in the list. This field is empty by default.

#### New "Future valid from"
The date you enter in this field will be suggested in the "Future valid from" column on the agreement row. This field is empty by default.
