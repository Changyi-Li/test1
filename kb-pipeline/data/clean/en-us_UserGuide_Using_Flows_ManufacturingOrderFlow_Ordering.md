### Ordering
- Order documents – Order documents (such as operation documents or travelers) often represent the actual ordering of work which tell the operator what he/she should do.
- Priority planning – A list that shows all orders on hand per work center. The priority of operations is based on planned finish date, but it can also prioritize per operation.
[![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/TrainingMaterial/PriorityPlanning.png)](../../../../../Resources/Images/TrainingMaterial/PriorityPlanning.png)
- Pick List – In this procedure you can create either a pick list or a list to use for reporting of material. The pick list is used for material withdrawal from stock and it is also possible to clear material directly when printing the pick list. The procedure is mainly intended for you who report the material before the manufacturing is started.
