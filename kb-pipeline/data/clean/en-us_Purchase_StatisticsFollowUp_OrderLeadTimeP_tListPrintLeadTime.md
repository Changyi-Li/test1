### Lead time to printing list
The list is loaded and displayed based on the selection and settings you have chosen. As in all lists in Monitor ERP, you can drag and drop in order to change the column to group by.
This list shows how many days have passed between purchase order registration and the order being sent to the supplier.

#### Example of calculating lead time to printing
Order date_ 2024-05-15 (a Wednesday)
Print date: 2024-05-21 (a Tuesday)
| Calendar | Wed. 15 | Thur. 16 | Fri. 17 | Sat. 18 | Sun. 19 | Mon. 20 | Tues. 21 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Standard calendar | 0 | 1 | 2 | Not a work day | Not a work day | 3 | Lead time Number of days between ordering date and delivery date. Normally used for purchased parts. to printing 4 days |
| All calendar days | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | Lead time to printing 6 days |
If you are using the standard calendar, your country’s holidays will not be included in the calculation.
