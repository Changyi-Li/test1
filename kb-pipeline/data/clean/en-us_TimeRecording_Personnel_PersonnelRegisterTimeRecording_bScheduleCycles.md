### Schedule cycles
This box is only available if you for the setting Schedule management have selected the option According to schedule cycle.
Here you add the schedule cycles which applies for the person and you also enter the dates when they cycles will start.
You can select to show inactive records (schedule cycles in past time) using the button Show inactive records ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_approve_document.png).

#### Number
In this field you select the schedule cycle's number. You register the schedule cycles in the Schedule cycles procedure. You will see the name of the schedule cycle to the right.

#### From
Here you select the start date from when the schedule cycle will apply.

#### Day in cycle
Here you see which day in the schedule cycle which applies for the start date. By clicking the button ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_more_info.png) in the Days column, you see which work schedule that applies for each day in the schedule cycle.
