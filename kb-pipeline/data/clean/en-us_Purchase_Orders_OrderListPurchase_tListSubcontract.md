### The Subcontract list
In this list you will find information about the subcontracts. The purchase order rows are linked to a specific operation on the manufacturing orders. Purchase orders of the subcontract type are also shown in the regular lists if you have made such selections, but this list type only show subcontracts.
The list is loaded and displayed based on the selection and settings you have chosen. As in all lists in Monitor ERP, you can drag and drop in order to change the column to group by.
