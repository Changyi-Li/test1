### Logging of e-mail
Both incoming and outgoing e-mails are logged here. You can double-click a row to open and read the e-mail in a separate window. By using the buttons at the top of this window you can reply to, reply to all, or forward, the e-mail. If there is an attached file in the e-mail, you can also use one of the buttons to download the file.
By using the Show e-mail message button ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_preview.png) on the function menu you can open and read the e-mail.
By using the Send a new e-mail message button ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_email.png) you can create a new e-mail message in your regular e-mail program, and at the same time a new record is created in the log.
By using the Import from inbox button ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_import.png) you can import e-mails from the inbox in question. This function requires you to configure setting in the procedure called Settings for incoming e-mail.

#### Dir. (Direction)
Here you see if it is an incoming (In) or outgoing (Out) e-mail.

#### Time
Here you see the date and time when the e-mail message was logged.

#### From
Here you see by whom the e-mail message was sent.

#### To
Here you see to whom (name or e-mail address) the e-mail was sent.

#### Copy (Cc)
Here you see to whom (name or e-mail address) the copy was sent.

#### Bcc
Here you see to whom (name or e-mail address) the secret copy (Blind carbon copy) was sent.

#### Subject
Here you see the subject row text of the e-mail.
