### Accounts receivable
In this box all unpaid customer invoices from the accounts receivable. These are invoices with some type of remaining amount: debit, credit, or interest invoices. On account On account is a partial payment (advance payment) which you have made to a supplier or received from a customer. payments are not shown here. The matching of the file contents is made according to how the payment format has been configured.
You can choose to delete all matchings or the matching for the row you have selected. You can preview the invoices
Matched invoices are displayed with green rows. The posting of the row selected, is shown in the box below.
