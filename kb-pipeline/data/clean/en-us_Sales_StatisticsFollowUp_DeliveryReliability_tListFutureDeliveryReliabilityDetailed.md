### The list Future delivery reliability – Detailed
This list shows in detailed our future delivery reliability to customer within the entered horizon (in number of days). This is done by having the planned delivery date compare to New finish which is calculated via the net requirement calculation. It is possible to group the list by customer, part, new finish, and delivery date.
The list is loaded and displayed based on the selection and settings you have chosen. As in all lists in Monitor ERP, you can drag and drop in order to change the column to group by.
