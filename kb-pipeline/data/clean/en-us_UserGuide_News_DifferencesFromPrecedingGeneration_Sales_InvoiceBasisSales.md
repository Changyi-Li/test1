### Invoice basis – Sales
- It is possible to enter price alternatives for CMThe contribution margin (CM) is the difference between the standard price and the sales price. and CRThe contribution ratio (CR) is the portion of the invoice amount (sales price) that the contribution margin represents. CR is entered as a percentage. under the Selection tab.
- From the list you can link to more procedures.
- It is possible to show records which have been canceled in the procedure Register invoice directly. This is done by selection by status 0 = Canceled.
