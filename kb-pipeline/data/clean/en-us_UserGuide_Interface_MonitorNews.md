# Monitor News
The news component Monitor News ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/UserGuide/MonitorNews.png) displays short news posts which contain important information and inspiration for users of Monitor ERP. The posts include notifications from our Support Center and the development department, tips and advice about how you get the most out of Monitor ERP, as well as information and links to training resources such as Monitor Academy, our webinars, our options, integrations, and more.
The news component is set to visible by default, however by clicking the ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/UserGuide/MonitorNews.png) button, you can choose whether you want to see the component or not. To let you know when there is an unread news post, an orange symbol is shown on the ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/UserGuide/MonitorNewsNewNotification.png) button.
In User settings you can select areas of interest to see the most relevant news in Monitor News. You choose areas of interest with the Roles/Areas and Countries settings. You will then get a new feed with content aimed at the country and role/roles you selected.
> The news feed shown in Monitor News is loaded by the Monitor server from the following endpoint: https://monitornews-prod.monitorerp.com.
