### Outgoing payment

#### Payment method
Here you select the payment method for the invoice. The payment method determines how the invoice will be paid. The default payment method is loaded from the supplier but it is possible to select among the payment methods registered in the Bank settings procedure.
> For electronic payment methods, you can configure that a warning or block should be shown if bank account information is missing for the invoice. This is configured for each respective payment method in the Bank settings procedure.

#### Direct payment amount
If it is a cash payment where cash book is used, you should here enter the amount paid out.

#### Cash report
If it is a cash payment (out) where cash book is used, you should here select the cash book where the outgoing payment should be recorded.

#### Pay via
Here you select the supplier to which the payment will be made. The default value is loaded from the supplier. If there is another supplier selected to be paid via, the supplier code will be displayed in red.

#### Bank account
The bank account of the payment recipient. It is loaded from the supplier selected in the field Pay via. You can change the bank account as long as there are no payments registered for the invoice. If the supplier has several registered bank accounts, the account in the same currency as the invoice will be suggested.
> If you import e-invoices via CrossState (Crediflow), the bank account on the e-invoice will be suggested, provided said bank account is registered for the supplier in theSupplier register.

#### Outgoing payments
Under this button you can see information about outgoing payments registered for the invoice in question.
