### Calculate annual budget
- It is possible to save a price list from the calculation.
- You can create the distribution per month automatically for the parts when you save.
- The parts can only be valued to price lists, not to standard price, customer price, etc. since these cannot be saved under the budget tab of the part.
- The list type Reset annual budget is a brand new list type that has been developed with the Agent in mind. This list enables you to reset the annual budget in an Agent task.
