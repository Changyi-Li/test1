### Loading plan
Here you see loading plans for the number of planned deliveries of customer orders and order rows for each delivery date. You can decide to only include orders ready for delivery. This is configured using a setting under the Selection tab.
In the upper part of the tab you will divided per date see: number of orders and order rows, net weight oa parts, volume, and number of packages. The pick time shown at the end of the list is the time it is calculated it will take to physically pick the parts on the pick list from stock. This time is calculated based on entered general values in the system settings Setup time for picking per order row and Unit time for picking per package.
In the bottom part of the tab you see net weight and pick time as a loading chart (in a line chart). You can configure the chart type and what should be shown in the chart under the Chart button ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_chart.png) on the function menu.
