### Update accounts receivable
- It is possible to load an accounts receivable record via order number.
- Shows more information about the accounts receivable record, for example which payments exist on the invoice and all posting items made, from invoice to payment.
- You can enter number of payment reminders and penalty interests.
- Viewing of invoice documents.
- When deleting an accounts receivable record, the invoice will remain in the accounts receivable but with the status Deleted.
- There is a new button called Incoming payments. It is used to see which payments are registered for the invoice in question.
- The new button Bookkeeping list is used to see an overall picture of all coding/posting items made for the invoice in question, from invoicing to payment.
