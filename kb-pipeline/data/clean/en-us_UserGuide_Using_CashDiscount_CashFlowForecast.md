### Cash flow forecast
When you load a cash flow forecast, you can take cash discount into consideration, as shown in the illustration below. If you for example choose the alternative Yes, according to discount limit 1, you simulate that everything will be paid on the first cash discount day for the records affected by this. In this case the amounts will be lowered and the cash flow is advanced for the records with cash discounts.
[![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/TrainingMaterial/CashFlowForecast.png)](../../../../Resources/Images/TrainingMaterial/CashFlowForecast.png)
This parameter affects all records except for other payments, that is, quotes, orders, invoice bases, ledgers.
