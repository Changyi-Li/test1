### The List tab
The list is displayed in two modes. One mode before calculation of net weight, and one mode for after the calculation.
Before calculation
Here you see the structure parts you have selected to calculated net weight for, The list is grouped by the initial selection. It is possible to deselect parts you do not want to include. It is also possible to – per part – change the terms you configured in the settings.
You start the calculation by using the ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_run.png) button on the toolbar of the procedure.
After calculation
Here you see the result of the calculation. The list is grouped by the initial selection of structure parts. If you have selected to also calculate underlying levels, the underlying parts are shown in a grouping on the recalculated parts.
In the list you see the old net weights and the new calculated net weights per piece for each part. You can also change the new net weights manually. The difference between old and new net weights is shown as a percentage. You can configure net weight as fixed weight. The default is to save new net weights for the parts in the list, unless they did not have any net weight prior to the calculation. It is optional – per part – to save the new net weight. To the far right in the list a warning ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/warning.png) is displayed if net weight is missing on any of the underlying parts.
New net weights are saved for the parts where Save is marked in the list. These will be saved when you save ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_save.png) in the procedure.
> If you have activated calculation of underlying levels, please note the following: A structure parts can contain more than one of the same underlying part. The net weight displayed in the list is the weight per piece of each part. If the new net weight has been changed for an underlying part, you must first save the weight for that part in the list, and then reloade the list in order to get a correct new net weight calculated for the structure part.
