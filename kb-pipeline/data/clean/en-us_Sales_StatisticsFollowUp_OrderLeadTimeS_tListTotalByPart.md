### The Total by part list
The list is loaded and displayed based on the selection and settings you have chosen. As in all lists in Monitor ERP, you can drag and drop in order to change the column to group by.
In the list called Total by part, the average lead times for order rows are compared to the part’s lead time.
Only order rows that have been partially delivery reported or fully delivery reported, will be included in the list.

#### Safety stock
Shows the part’s Safety stock from the Planning tab in the Part register.

#### Safety stock in work days
Shows the part’s Safety stock in work days from the Planning tab in the Part register.

#### Safety time
Shows the part’s Safety time from the Planning tab in the Part register.

#### Delivered order rows
In this column you see the number of delivered order rows (partially delivered or fully delivered rows depending on how Status was set in the selection).

#### Desired lead time
Calculated average desired lead time for the delivered order rows. Desired lead time = Desired delivery date – Order date.

#### Desired comparison
Here you see the calculated difference between the average desired lead time and the part’s lead time.

#### Initial lead time
Here you see the calculated average initial lead time for the delivered order rows. Initial lead time = Initial delivery date – Print date.

#### Initial comparison
Here you see the calculated difference between the average initial lead time and the part’s lead time.

#### Actual lead time
Here you see the calculated average actual lead time for the delivered order rows. Actual lead time = Actual delivery date – Print date.

#### Actual comparison
Here you see the calculated difference between the average actual lead time and the part’s lead time.
