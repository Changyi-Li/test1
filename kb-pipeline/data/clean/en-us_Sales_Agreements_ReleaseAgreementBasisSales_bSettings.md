### Settings

#### Show invoiced rows
With this setting you decide if invoiced agreement bases (invoices with status 9) should also be shown in the list.

#### Show foreign currency
With this setting you determine if all values should be displayed in foreign currency. If the setting is activated, you will see a total per currency at the bottom of the list (total value per currency code). For the total lists, CM The contribution margin (CM) is the difference between the standard price and the sales price. and CR The contribution ratio (CR) is the portion of the invoice amount (sales price) that the contribution margin represents. CR is entered as a percentage. are not shown. Instead you will see amount and currency. The amount is displayed in the company currency.

#### Pre-select "Release" of agreement basis
Here you decide if the Release checkbox should be marked by default in the list.
