### Deliveries
In this box you see a list of customer orders which have reported deliveries.
The list shows order number, partial delivery number, information (see more below), customer number, and name, order type, pick list number (if pick list has been created), and status of the invoice basis.

#### Undo
Check this checkbox if you wish to undo all the delivery reporting items on the order.

#### Information
An error symbol ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/error.png) will be shown if the delivery reporting cannot be undone due to different reasons. When you hover over the symbol, a tooltip will display the cause in text form. For example, you cannot undo a delivery reporting if a pick list has been created or if the delivery has been invoiced.
