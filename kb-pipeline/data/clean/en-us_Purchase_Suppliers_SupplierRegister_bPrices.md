### Prices
In this box you configure price settings for the supplier.

#### Discount
In this field you can enter a discount, if any. This discount will be selected by default e.g. on new order rows.

#### Discount category
Here you select a discount category. Discount categories must first be registered in the Discount categories procedure.

#### Supplier prices
Here you can see all parts to which the supplier is linked/connected. You can add/delete parts with prices and update other part information. If you copy a supplier using the Save as function, you can choose to copy the information found under the Prices button. You can go to the Part register procedure by using a link.
