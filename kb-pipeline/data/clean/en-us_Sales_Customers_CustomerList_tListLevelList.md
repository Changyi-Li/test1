### List type Level list
This list shows customer relationships according to the setting Parent company (customer).
This list has two windows: Consists of and Included in.
In the Consists of window, the customers you have selected are displayed. If you select a customer in this window, the customers that the selected customer is included in are displayed in theIncluded in window.
The toolbar in the Included in window has a button: Load the selected customer to “Consists of”. If you have selected a customer in the Included in window and then click this button, the selected customer will be highlighted in the Consists of window.
