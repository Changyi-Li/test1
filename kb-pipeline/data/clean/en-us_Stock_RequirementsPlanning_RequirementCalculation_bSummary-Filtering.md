### Summary/Filtering
In this box you can filter in the list above after the calculation has been made. You filter out/take away information in the list by deactivating the checkbox for the respective alternatives in the sections.
In the left section you can filter the list by: manufacturing order suggestion, rescheduling – in, rescheduling – out, and unnecessary orders for manufacturing.
In the center section you can filter the list by: purchase order suggestion, rescheduling – in, rescheduling – out, and unnecessary orders for purchase.
In the section to the right you can see blocked suggestions or warnings, if any. For example, that a supplier is missing, how many suggestions this affects, and how many parts or suppliers that are affected by this.
