### Configurations
In this box you add the EDI EDI is the acronym of Electronic Data Interchange. EDI is about exchanging electronic business documents with your business partners, e.g. customers and suppliers. The EDI concept can be wide and a bit unclear, and can many times be used about all types of documents which are sent electronically, even if it might be PDF files sent via e-mail or publishing business documents on a website. What we refer to as EDI – and what is traditionally meant by EDI – is structured business documents following given standards, electronically sent or received and which are compiled and interpreted automatically and that is integrated with the customer's/supplier's ERP system. configurations that you need.
By using the buttons in the function menu you can add, insert, and delete configurations row by row.

#### Name
Here you enter the name of the configuration.

#### Active
Here you determine if the configuration should be active or not. You can set a configuration as active if it has an active channel that in turn has at least one linked behavior.

#### Log
By using the button Log ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_more_info.png) you find a changelog for the configuration. Here you see who modified the information as well as when. You also see what has been changed, with the value before and after the change.
