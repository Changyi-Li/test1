### Settings – Electronic outgoing payment
Bank information

#### Payment format
Here you determine which payment format should apply to this payment method.
The formats available to select among depend on which bank has been activated under the Bank settings tab.
In the Settings for export/import procedure, you configure additional settings for the payment. You can access these settings via the link to the right of the field.

#### Confirmation format
Here you determine which confirmation format for outgoing payments should apply to this payment method.
The formats available to select among depend on which bank has been activated under the Bank settings tab.
In the Settings for export/import procedure, you configure additional settings for the payment. You can access these settings via the link to the right of the field.

#### Internet bank website
Here you enter the website to the Internet bank to which the payment is made. When the payments have been exported, the Internet bank's website will open automatically in your browser.
Transaction list

#### Document
Here you select which transaction list should apply to the outgoing payment.
Check the recipient of payment

#### Check if bank account is missing
Here you determine if a check should be made to see if bank account is missing. Then you can choose if the system should warn or block if bank account is missing.
For electronic payment methods, a warning or block can be shown if bank account information is missing for the invoice. This is configured for each respective payment method in the Bank settings procedure.

#### Link to bank account/bookkeeping account
Here you link currencies to bank accounts. If the company has different currency accounts at the bank, you can determine which account should be charged depending on the currency on the supplier invoice. The link is also used when posting outgoing payments in connection with the confirmation to make sure that the correct bookkeeping account is used depending on the currency in which the payment was made. If you leave this table empty, the system links to the accounts that are registered under the Bank accounts tab, based on their currency.
