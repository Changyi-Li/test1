## Monitor diagnostics
This procedure is used to see which scheduled tasks/jobs that have taken long to perform and may be the cause of why other tasks/jobs in the scheduler did not start. You can see when the server was started, how many active threads there are, and how many jobs have run since the start. And most importantly, you see which jobs are in progress right now, when they where started, and for how long they have been running. This means you can quickly identify the problem instead of troubleshooting the procedure where there are scheduled task not starting, which may not be the actual root of the problem.
