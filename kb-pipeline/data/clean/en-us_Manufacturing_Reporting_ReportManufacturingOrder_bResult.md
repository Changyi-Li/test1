### Result
In this box you see the parts from the saved reporting. You can preview the transport labels in the box to the right by marking the parts's rows.
The purpose of the transport labels is to use them to mark the pallet between the operations and after the last operation for the transfer to stock.

#### Include
In this column you determine if the transport label for the part should be printed or not by using the checkbox Include. By default, all parts are selected for printing of transport label.

#### Reported quantity
Here you see the reported quantity but it can be edited, when needed.

#### Quantity/package
Here you can enter quantity per package. This affects how many transport labels will be printed. In this column the reported quantity is shown by default. If you have entered a quantity per package in the Miscellaneous box under the Manufacturing tab in the Part register, this quantity will be shown by default.

#### Number of copies
Here you select the number of copies that should be printed. The default number of copies is determined in the Document settings procedure.
