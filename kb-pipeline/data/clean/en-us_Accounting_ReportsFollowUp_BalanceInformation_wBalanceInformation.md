## Balance information
In this procedure you can see balance information for one or more accounts. You can also see transactions and journals, as well as dimensions, that is, what forms the basis of the balance information.
The data in the procedure is loaded from account balances, transactions and related tables and data, and it is by default loaded from the active accounting year. You can see balances and transactions in another accounting year by first changing accounting year using the button Change accounting year ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_calendar.png) on the toolbar. If you change accounting year you then need to load data again to the list.
To see balance information from a part of a period (selected by date) you must use the Print accounting report procedure. In that procedure you can via reports load approximately the corresponding data as in the Balance information. This procedure is mainly used to quickly see information about separate accounts, for example by linking from other procedures or by opening it manually.
