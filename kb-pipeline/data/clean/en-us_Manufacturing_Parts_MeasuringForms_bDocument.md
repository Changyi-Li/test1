### Documents
Under this tab you can preview what the measuring form will look like as a document if you print it.
At the top of the tab you find buttons used to print ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_print.png) the document or send it via e-mail ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_email.png) as PDF in an attachment. It is possible to enter values manually on the printed document from this procedure.
At the bottom of the tab you can zoom using the lever or by using Ctrl + the scroll wheel.
> In the Document templates procedure you can create your own variants of this document. You find the document under Common and it is called Form.
