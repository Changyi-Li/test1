### Edit posting
Here you can adjust errors in postings of payments, if any. You can e.g. add or delete account rows in the table. However, there are some restrictions regarding what you can modify in a posting. For example, you cannot modify rows containing accounts which are configured as blocked for manual postings in the Chart of accounts procedure.
At the bottom of the table you will see two amount fields, one for the Debit column and another for the Credit column.

#### Account
Here you see the debit and credit accounts used in the existing posting.

#### Dimensions (CC and CU and Project)
Here you can enter dimension codes for dimensions. You handle these in the [Dimensions](../../ChartOfAccountsDimensions/Dimensions/wDimensions.htm) procedure.

#### Specification
This column shows a specification for the posting row in question. Posting rows can contain the invoice number in the specification column to simplify the traceability in the accounting. In order to see a specification here, you need to check Specification for the account in question in the Chart of accounts procedure.

#### Amount
Here you see/enter the posted amount. It is entered in the invoice's currency. An amount is only shown if the account in question is a currency account in the chart of accounts, e.g. a bank account in a foreign currency.

#### Debit and Credit
These column shows the debit amount and the credit amount that have been posted (in the company currency).
