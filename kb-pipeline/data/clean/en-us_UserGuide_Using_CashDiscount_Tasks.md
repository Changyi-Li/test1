### Information
The following applies if the option EIM is activated in the system. In the Tasks window you find the EIM tab where you can see the invoices for you to authorize. By clicking the button More info you find the column Cash discount date. If it is possible to use cash discount on the invoice you will in this field see the cash discount date. If there are multiple cash discount limits for the same invoice, you will here see the earliest possible cash discount date on which you can pay. When this date has passed, you will instead see the next cash discount date. When all cash discount dates have passed, the field will be empty. The cash discount date is displayed in red when the cash discount date is five days from now, or less.
