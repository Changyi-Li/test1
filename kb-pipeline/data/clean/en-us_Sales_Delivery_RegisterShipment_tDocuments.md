### The Documents tab
Under the Documents tab you handle document printouts from the procedure. Use the menu to the right to select which section of the tab you wish to read about.
In the Document texts procedure, you can configure settings for "Additional text in e-mail message" if you send waybills via e-mail. There you can, for example, add tags show our reference, phone number, your reference, etc.
> Please note! The Documents tab is only available if you have selected Other as plugin for export in the Supplier register procedure.
