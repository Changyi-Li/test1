### The Ledger – Detailed list
This list is a detailed list of invoices with invoice amount, and remaining amount per a certain ledger date. These amounts are also shown in foreign currency. By activating a setting under the Selection tab, you can also choose to include fully paid invoices. The list can be grouped by VAT registration number and due date. In the list you find the Ledger summary column which displays the net amount between unpaid customer invoices and supplier invoices. A positive amount means that that company has a debt to you , and a negative amount means that you have a debt to the other company.
