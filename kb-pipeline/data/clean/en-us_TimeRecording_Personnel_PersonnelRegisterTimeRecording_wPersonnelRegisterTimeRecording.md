## Personnel records – Time recording
In this procedure you register different information connected to attendance recording and work recording for an employee.
If the option Machine integration is installed, you also use the procedure to register information about machines and line.
However, you can also register other information in the procedure, such as addresses and phone numbers for the employee. If the module Time recording is not included in your system, you register this type of information in the procedure Personnel records – General, which you find in the module General registers.
This means it is possible to modify and enter the same information in this procedure since it is the same register. The online help for the boxes under the General and Activities tabs lead to the personnel records in the General registers module.
You can add extra fields for this procedure in the Extra fields procedure. If such fields already have been created, these will be available under the Extra fields tab.
