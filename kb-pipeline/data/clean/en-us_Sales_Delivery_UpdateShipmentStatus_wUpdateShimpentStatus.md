## Update shipment status
In this procedure you can activate a scheduled loading of information from nShift and Logtrade.
The purpose is to load complementing information for shipments to Monitor ERP, which earlier was exported to nShift Delivery or Logtrade from the Register shipment procedure with the setting Complement/Print in TA system activated.
The exported shipments should then be complemented and printed via the TA system's web interface. In nShift, these shipments are referred to as "stored printings". In nShirt Web-TA, these shipments have status "OK", and in Logtrade these shipments have status "New".
The scheduled loading updates the shipments in Monitor ERP with for example the shipping agent's shipment number, shipping agent's package number, and gets the status updated from 4 (Exported) to 5 (Booked/Printed).
In the procedure you can also load a list of shipments to check that they have had the above mentioned information complemented and that they have been printed. You can also perform goods tracking for these shipments in that list.
