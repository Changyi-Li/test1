### Print payment reminder
- A new layout of the window with document preview on the same level as the result list.
- It is possible to see distribution method in the result list.
- The days of grace can be adjusted in the selection.
- It is possible to go to the procedure from the list in Payment reminder basis and then bring a selection of records to the reminder list.
- When e-mailing reminders you can also print a copy to printer.
- A copy of the original invoice can then be attached to the reminder.
