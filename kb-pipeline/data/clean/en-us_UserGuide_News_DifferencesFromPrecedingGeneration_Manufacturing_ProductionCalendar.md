### Production calendar
- In the procedure you enter exceptions from the basic time (day planning) and exceptions from schedule (hourly planning, capacity via schedule) for individual days. This is done in a calendar view.
- It is possible to change the schedule for multiple days at a time.
- It is possible to change for several work centers at a time.
- The exceptions can also be seen and updated in the Work centerA work center is a part of the factory. It can be a single machine or a group of machines, a single workstation or a group of workstations. register procedure.
- When you add an exception for capacity, it is now possible both to make exceptions from which basic time and the number of machines/persons which are available at a certain date. This can also be entered directly in the work center register.
