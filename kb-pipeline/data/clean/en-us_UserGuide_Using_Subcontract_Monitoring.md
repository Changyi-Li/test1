### Monitoring
Throughout the period from registration of the manufacturing order to when the subcontract is fully completed, the order can be monitored in Order list – Purchase → list type Subcontract. This list shows all subcontract operations as a remaining quantity. Under Settings, you can choose for the list to show fully completed operations as well.
