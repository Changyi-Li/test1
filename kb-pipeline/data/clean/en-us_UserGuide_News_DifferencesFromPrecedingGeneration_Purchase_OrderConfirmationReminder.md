### Order confirmation reminder
- In G4 the procedure is called Order Confirmation Reminders but in Swedish there was another name. In G5, the procedure is therefore renamed in Swedish so it matches the procedure name Order confirmation reminder.
- The printing of the reminder is logged on the purchase order.
- Days of graceThe term days of grace (or grace period) is used at requirements planning in order to calculate rescheduling of actual orders that cover the requirement but that are too late in time, instead of suggesting a new order. for order confirmation (on supplier) is a new setting.
- Separate days of grace for time since the most recent reminder regarding the order.
- If the price has been shown on the order it means that the price will also be shown on the reminder and vice versa.
- The procedures for regular purchases and for subcontracts are now one and the same procedure.
- Support for stray/occasional supplier (gives you a document which is grouped by order instead of by supplier).
