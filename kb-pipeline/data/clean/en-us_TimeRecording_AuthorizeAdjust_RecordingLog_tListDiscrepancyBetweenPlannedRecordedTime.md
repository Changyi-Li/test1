### The list type Discrepancy between planned/recorded time
This list shows if there are time differences between planned and recorded time, that is, difference between attendance/absence and the scheduled time. The list is grouped by person.
If a person on one day has recorded more or recorded less time than the scheduled time, this list will show that day with information about date, schedule number, schedule time, attendance time, absence time, and the difference between recorded time and scheduled time (in hours).
All discrepancies must be adjusted before the say can be authorized.
