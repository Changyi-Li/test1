### The Detailed list
The detailed list shows extensive information about the selected invoice bases. In this list you can enter comments for each invoice basis. It is also possible to delete invoice bases that have not been linked or preliminary linked to supplier invoices.
> If you have reported subcontract costs in the Report subcontracting costs list type in the Report subcontracting cost procedure, the supplier invoice will be shown with status Final recorded, but without a Consecutive number.
