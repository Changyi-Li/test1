### Terms
In this box you configure different terms for the quote. When registering new quotes, the terms are by default loaded from the customer selected in the procedure.
Please note! Delivery terms and delivery method are affected by the selected delivery address. This means that if you change the delivery address, the delivery terms and delivery method will also be changed to the one entered for the selected delivery address.

#### Payment terms
The payment terms describe the credit time, that is, within which time frame the payment should be made. It is also possible to enter a payment term that is not previously registered. The field Credit period will then open where you must enter the credit period in days.

#### Delivery terms
The delivery terms describe who is responsible for the freight and when/at what point the responsibility for the transport passes from the selling party to the buying party. This will in its turn determine who will pay for the freight, unless the term has been set to freight free/free shipping. It is also possible to enter a delivery term that is not previously registered.

#### Delivery methods
The delivery method describes how or in what way the delivery should be made. It is also possible to enter a delivery method that is not previously registered.

#### Invoicing plan
This field is available if the system setting Handle invoicing plans on quotes/customer orders is activated.
In this box it is then possible to select an invoicing plan for a quote. This is selected from the templates registered in the Invoicing/Payment plans procedure in General registers. If you select an invoicing plan, the Invoicing plan tab opens where you can adjust the invoicing plan if needed. If you leave this field empty, the invoicing of the customer order – that was created from the quote – will be made as usual.
> An invoicing plan entered for a quote will be included when the quote is turned into a customer order. Please note! Adjustments, if any, made on the invoicing plan on the customer order will not update the invoicing plan on the quote. The invoicing plan on a quote works as a template for the order that will be registered.
