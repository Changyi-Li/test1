### Settings – Electronic incoming payment
Bank information

#### Confirmation format
Here you determine which confirmation format for incoming payments should apply to this payment method.
The formats available to select among depend on which bank has been activated under the Bank settings tab.
In the Settings for export/import procedure, you configure additional settings for the payment. You can access these settings via the link to the right of the field.

#### Link to bank account/bookkeeping account
Here you link currencies to bank accounts. If the company has different currency accounts at the bank, you can determine which account should be charged depending on the currency on the supplier invoice. The link is also used when posting outgoing payments in connection with the confirmation to make sure that the correct bookkeeping account is used depending on the currency in which the payment was made. If you leave this table empty, the system will link to the accounts that are registered under the Bank accounts tab, based on their currency.
