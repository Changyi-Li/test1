### The General list
In this list you can see and update different settings for the user. The list has three different presentations: General, E-mail, and Printers.
In the General presentation you can modify some general user settings. In the list you see the settings that are found under the heading [General](../Users/bGeneral.htm) in the Users procedure.
In the E-mail presentation you can change the e-mail address, and if a copy should be sent to own e-mail address of the user. In the list you see the settings that are found under the heading [E-mail](../Users/bE-mail.htm) in the Users procedure.
In the Printers presentation you can change printout format for shipping label. In the list you see the settings that are found under the heading [Printers](../Users/bPrinters.htm) in the Users procedure.
To be able to change the settings in the list, you click the Updateable button ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_edit_list.png) (Ctrl + U) on the toolbar of the procedure and reload the list.
