### Supplier links
The management of subcontract part's supplier links is similar to the one in the Part register. You can change price, unit, staggered prices, enter quantity/Package, enter material type and emissions, etc. For specific information about each column, please see the help function for [Supplier links in the Part register](../PartRegister/bSupplierLinks.htm).
> Please note! Supplier links are only shown, cannot be edited. The supplier link can only be changed by switching the work center and thereby the supplier in the BOM and routing procedure.
