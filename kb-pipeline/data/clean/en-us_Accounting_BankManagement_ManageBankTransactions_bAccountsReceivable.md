### Accounts receivable
Under this tab you see all unpaid invoices from the accounts receivable. These are invoices with some type of remaining amount: debit, credit, or interest invoices. The matching of the file contents is made according to how the payment format has been configured.
You can choose to delete all matchings or the matching for the row you have selected. You can preview the invoices
Matched invoices are displayed with green rows. The posting of the row selected, is shown in the box below.
