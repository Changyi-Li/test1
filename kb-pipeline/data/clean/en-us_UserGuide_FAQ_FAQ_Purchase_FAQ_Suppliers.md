### FAQ – Suppliers
Can I delete a supplier?
You cannot delete a supplier by using the Delete button ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_delete.png) (the eraser icon) in the Supplier register procedure if the supplier is registered/used in any context, for example, if the supplier is used on a purchase order, supplier link, etc. In cases where you are not allowed to delete a supplier, you can instead deactivate or block the supplier.
