### Requirement list
This list shows the net requirement on the main row, and the sub-rows show more information about whether the requirement comes from customer or purchase orders. If there is a purchase order, it will be shown on a separate row.
You can choose to view the balance as quantity or Standard price in company currency.
Columns that you have loaded from More info ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_more_info.png) will disappear if you reload the list.
