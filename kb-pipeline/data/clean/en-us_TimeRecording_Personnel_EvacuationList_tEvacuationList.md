### The Evacuation list
This list is used as a so-called "fire list" and is printed as a basis to use calling over of names of personnel present if an evacuation situation should occur. Only persons who are clocked in, and persons who are clocked out on break, will be included in the list. The list shows everyone who is clocked in, those who are clocked out for a break, and if location recording is activated, also employees who have made a location recording even though they are clocked out. This can be useful, for example, when using company premises outside of working hours. The list is grouped by department.
The list shows the recording terminal on which a person most recently recorded, as well as the recording status Clocked in or Clocked out on break.
If site recording is activated (via the system setting called Activate site recording), you can also see on which site each person choose when recording. Based on this you can tell which persons are in the building and which are not.
