### Revenue calculation method
This box becomes available when you apply the Percentage of completion method to the project.

#### Revenue calculation method
Here you see the revenue calculation method for the project type. The options are None and Percentage of completion method.

#### Pricing
Here you the pricing selected for projects with revenue calculation: Continuous price, Fixed price, or Continuous price with profit mark-up.

#### Profit mark-up
Here you enter the general profit mark-up in percent.

#### Calculation basis stage of completion
With this setting you determine if the result of Costs or Hours should be the basis of the calculation. The default option here is Costs.

#### Level for revenue calculation
This setting will affect on which level the revenue calculation should be performed. The Main project option will total all sub-projects together with the main project and perform the calculation on main project level. When selecting the Sub-project option it means all projects which belong to the main project will be handled as separate projects with individual revenue calculations.
The Level for revenue calculation field is not displayed for sub-projects because this is determined by a setting on the main project, and for the individual project the field is deactivated since it does not perform a function.
