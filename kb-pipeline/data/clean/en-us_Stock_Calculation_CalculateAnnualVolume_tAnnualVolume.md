### The Annual volume tab
After calculation in the Explosion or Simulation lists, this tab will become available showing all selected main parts and the incorporated parts found at the explosion. You can modify New quantity and you can also select for which parts the new quantity should be saved to the annual volume. If you have performed a simulation it is not possible to save.

#### New quantity
In this column you see the quantity you get from the total of all structure explosions. A part can get its quantity from multiple products in which it is included. In the column you can change the new quantity.

#### Select
Here you determine if the new quantity of the part should be saved to the annual volume when you save in the procedure.
