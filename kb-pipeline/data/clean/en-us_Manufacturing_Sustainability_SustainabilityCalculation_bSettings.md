### Settings – Part contents calculation

#### Calculation
- Calculate underlying levels – With this checkbox you decide if the underlying levels in the structure should be calculated.
- Pre-select "Include" – With this setting you decide if the parts "Include" checkbox should be marked by default, that is, that they are marked to be included in the part contents calculation.
- Quantity if order qty is missing – the lot size the calculation will be based on for parts where order qty is missing.
