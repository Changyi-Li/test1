### The Update annual volume list
This list type loads the calculated quantity for the annual volume for main parts. Data is loaded from the date interval you selected to load annual volume from and is then multiplied by the factor. If you are loading the consumption for three months, you take that value and multiply by four (the factor = 4.00) to achieve an annual volume.
The list is used either to manually update the annual volume or to calculate a new quantity by selecting something in the table Load annual volume from in the Settings box.

#### New quantity
In this column you can update the quantity of the part. The differing quantity in the next column shows New quantity − Old quantity. When you update this column, the New value column will also become updated. This value is formed as the part's quantity multiplied by the part's price.

#### Save
In this column you decide which rows you want to save.
