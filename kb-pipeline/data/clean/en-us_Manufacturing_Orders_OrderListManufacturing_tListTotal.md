### The Total list
This list shows total information per manufacturing order. It is possible to expand a row to see the detailed information for each operation in the order.

#### Include
You can check this checkbox for the orders you wish to load to another procedure via Go to procedure ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_link.png) on the function menu. The procedures possible to open with the same order numbers loaded are Register manufacturing order, Print manufacturing order, and Synchronize with BOM and routing.
