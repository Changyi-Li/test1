### Traceability
This box is available if the part of the dispatch advised order row has traceability. If the part has traceability at batch level, you will see Batches as a sub-heading. If the part has traceability at serial number level, you will see Serial number A serial number is a number that is used for traceability for parts on entity level. as a sub-heading. In this box you can add rows for batches as well as for serial numbers.
You can expand/minimize the Traceability Traceability in Monitor ERP is all about being able to trace a specific serial number or a batch in each step it is being processed, as of when a part or a material arrives with you from a supplier. Traceability is also about stating what is withdrawn from and what is added to stock, so it is then possible to trace from customer order, via manufacturing order to purchase order. But it is also about being able to trace the other way around; from purchase order via manufacturing order to customer order. box by using the button to the right of the box.
Batches

#### Quantity
Here you can enter the number of parts in the batch.

#### Batch number
Here you can enter a batch number for the batch or you can leave it empty if you want the batch number to be loaded from the number series.

#### Charge number
Here you can enter a charge number for the batch.

#### Country of origin
Here you can edit/enter enter a country of origin for the batch.

#### Certificate (C)
Here you can add/show linked certificates for the batch.
Serial number

#### From
Here you enter the first serial number for the parts.

#### Quantity
Here you enter how many parts should have serial numbers.

#### To
Here the last serial number will automatically be entered. This number is assigned as a consecutive number based on the first serial number and the number of parts with serial numbers.

#### Charge number
Here you can enter a charge number for the serial numbers.

#### Country of origin
Here you can edit/enter a country of origin for the serial number.

#### Certificate (C)
Here you can add/show certificates for the serial number.
