### Linked suppliers
In this box you find EDI EDI is the acronym of Electronic Data Interchange. EDI is about exchanging electronic business documents with your business partners, e.g. customers and suppliers. The EDI concept can be wide and a bit unclear, and can many times be used about all types of documents which are sent electronically, even if it might be PDF files sent via e-mail or publishing business documents on a website. What we refer to as EDI – and what is traditionally meant by EDI – is structured business documents following given standards, electronically sent or received and which are compiled and interpreted automatically and that is integrated with the customer's/supplier's ERP system. behaviors for the Purchase module. Here you add the suppliers that should be linked to the selected behavior. You can add separate suppliers row by row or you can add several suppliers at a time from the Clipboard.
In the Supplier column you select a supplier from the supplier register. You then see the name of the supplier in the column to the right. In the column More information ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_more_info.png) you find address information as well as other information about the supplier.
If Search order, supplier identity is configured to Alternative supplier number, a warning appears ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/warning.png). This applies if the supplier you have added has no alternative supplier number in the supplier register. If two suppliers happen to have the same alternative supplier number, an error message appears ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/error.png).
At the bottom you find a total of rows with suppliers added to the table. You can also use the navigation buttons to move between the rows.
