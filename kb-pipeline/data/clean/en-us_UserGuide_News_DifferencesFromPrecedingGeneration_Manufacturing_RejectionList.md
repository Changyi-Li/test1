### Rejection list
- The list shows the actual date (not the planned date) for the rejection of the material.
- The list of material rejections is now possible to be grouped by rejection date.
- The system always makes a cost calculation per operation and the alternative Distribute according to std. price has been removed in G5.
- It is possible to select if the material's SO or the subcontract's SC should be included in the cost calculation.
