### Synchronize customer order

#### Include
In this column you determine if the customer order should be included.

#### Order number
In this column you see the order number.

#### Customer number
In this column you see the customer number.

#### Name
Here you see the customer name.

#### Status
In this column you see the status of the order.

#### Information
Here you see information and warnings such as for example "There are invoice bases which might also need to be updated".
