### Settings – Total by date

#### Date
With this setting you determine if the parts should be valued to the price that applied on the Log date or at the Actual date. The price for that date is registered in the standard price log in the part register.

#### Price alternative for purchased part
This is always the standard price. You cannot change the price alternative for purchased parts in this list.

#### Price alternative for manufactured part
This is always the standard price. You cannot change the price alternative for manufactured parts in this list.

#### Historical price
Here you determine if the parts should be valued using the historical price. If you do not activate this setting, the parts will instead be valuated according to the current standard price.

#### Include SO mark-up
It determines if SO mark-up (storage overhead) should be added to the parts in the list.

#### Group dates by
Here you decide how the dates should be grouped. The available options are: Day, Week, Month, Quarter, and Year.
