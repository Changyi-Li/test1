### Settings

#### Status
Here you select which status options of the outgoing payments that should be included in the list for cancellation. The available alternatives are:
- Ordered, not sent – outgoing payments that exist in transaction lists but have not been sent to the bank.
- Sent, not confirmed – outgoing payments that have been sent to the bank.
- Paid/Confirmed – outgoing payments that have been paid manually or have been confirmed.

#### Voucher text
Here you select the text that will be used as voucher text for outgoing payments that will be canceled. The default text is “Canceled outgoing payments”. In the Voucher texts procedure you can create texts to use as pre-defined voucher texts.
