### Sanctions lists
Under each tab of sanction lists you find a list of matches made against the sanctions list. Below you find explanations of the list columns:
ID is the ID number of the entity in the sanctions list.
Name of the person or organization for the entity in the sanctions list.
Sub-name is an alias for a person or an organization if it occurs in the sanctions list.
VKZ is the employee number which has been entered as Seller on the customer in Monitor ERP.
HO-id is the customer number, supplier number, or employee number in Monitor ERP.
Asp is the row number of the reference in Monitor ERP if the match is made on reference.
Matched text is the information in Monitor ERP which has been matched.
Sanctions text is the information in the sanctions list which has been matched.
