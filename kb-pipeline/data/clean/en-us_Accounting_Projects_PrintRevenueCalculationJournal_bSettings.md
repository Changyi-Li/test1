### Settings

#### Date
Here you select a date for the period which the revenue calculation journal concerns.

#### Enter voucher date manually
With this setting you decide if the voucher date should be manually entered.

#### Voucher date
In this field you enter the voucher date manually. This way you can direct the journal to a specific date if it for example contains old records belonging to a locked period.

#### Create reversal voucher
Here you decide if an accrual record of the type called reversal voucher should be created at the time of approving the journal.

#### Voucher date
Here you select a date for the reversal voucher.
