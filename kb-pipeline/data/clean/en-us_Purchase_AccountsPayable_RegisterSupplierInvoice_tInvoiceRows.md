### The Invoice rows tab
The Invoice rows tab is available when the EIM Workflow option is installed.
Here you can check interpreted invoice rows that are included on the imported invoice. You can change all information on the invoice rows, for example, if a piece of information has been misinterpreted. It is also possible to add and delete invoice rows.
The invoice rows shown here will also be shown in the Invoice rows box under the Order link tab. There you can manually link purchase order rows to invoice rows if no match and automatic linking has taken place.

#### Bank account
Here you select the bank account to link to the invoice rows.

#### Costs
By clicking the button ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_more_info.png), you can see costs that are in the invoice header and not on the invoice rows on the imported invoice. You can also add and delete such costs.

#### Include in matching
With this setting you decide if the invoice row should be included when Workflow is matching invoice rows with purchase order rows.
