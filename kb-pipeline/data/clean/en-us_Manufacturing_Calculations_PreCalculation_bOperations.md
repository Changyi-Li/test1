### Operations
Here you find settings for operations in the calculation. The settings exist for the list type New calculation.

#### Cost factors
The cost factors for operations that will be included in the calculation. The costs that are selected in the system setting Default cost factors will here be shown as default.

#### Price alternative
With this setting you decide if current or future prices should be loaded from the cost factors.

#### Staggered subcontract prices
Uses the staggered price for the operation quantity included in the calculation. If you uncheck this checkbox, the basic price will be applied. Staggered subcontract prices affect only the unit price for subcontracts.
