### Settings – View

#### Show only main rows
Detailed. This setting determines if only main rows will be loaded to the list. Associated sub-rows such as additional texts and included parts in fictitious part, will not be displayed.

#### Show alloy cost
With this setting you decide whether or not rows with alloy costs should be displayed in the list.

#### Show foreign currency
If you activate this setting, the list will show price each, setup price, and amount also in the foreign currency. A currency total is also displayed at the bottom of the window (total value is shown per currency code).

#### Use unit for statistics
This setting is used to determine if the invoice rows in the log should be shown in the unit selected for Statistics for the parts. This is entered under the [Unit usage](../../../Stock/Parts/PartRegister/bStandardpriceUnit.htm) for the parts in the Part register. Quantity and price are recalculated via the conversion factor of the statistics unit. If the setting is not activated, the log will show the quantity and unit which applied for the invoices.

#### Debit/Credit
With this setting you determine if all invoices should be included, or alternatively, only debit invoices or credit invoices.
