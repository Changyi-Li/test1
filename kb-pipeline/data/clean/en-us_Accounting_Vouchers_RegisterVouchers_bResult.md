### Result
In this box you can see the result for the current period and year. These results also include the postings you are working on right now in a new voucher.
The results in this box are calculated as the sum of all transactions of account types Income and account type Costs. What has been recorded on debit counts as minus. What has been recorded on credit counts as plus.
For example:
Total of recorded income is credit 500 000. Total of recorded costs is debit 200 000. The result is 300 000.
Total of recorded income is debit 18 000. Total of recorded costs is debit 25 000. The result is -43 000.

#### For the period
Here you see the result of the current period in the accounting year you are working in.

#### Total
Here you see the result of the current year in the accounting year you are working in.
