### Personnel records – Time recording
- Since it in G5 also is possible to add sellers, administrators, references, etc. in a joint personnel record together with employees who record attendance/work, the register has now been renamed Personnel records. This means you can here link a seller to that persons employee number so you do not have to register the same person in two procedures.
- There are fields where you can enter date of employment. It is possible to enter both start and end date.
- It is possible to enter planned absence in this procedure instead of having to go to a separate procedure for planned absence.
- You can enter schedule exceptions in the procedure or go to the separate procedure for schedule exceptions.
- It is possible to add an optional number of persons who authorize/adjust per employee number. Furthermore it is possible to separate user rights for work and attendance.
