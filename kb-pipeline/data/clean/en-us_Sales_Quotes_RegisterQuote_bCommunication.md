### Communication
For a new quote you will here, by default, see the e-mail address that is registered for the customer as recipient of the quote. You can modify, delete, and add rows containing communication information for the quote in question. See the explanation of the different columns in the help chapter for [Communication](../../Customers/CustomerRegister/bCommunication.htm) in the Customer register procedure.

#### Printout via
With this checkbox you determine whether the printout of the quote by default will be made via printer or e-mail. The setting is loaded from the customer.

#### Print invoice via
With this checkbox you determine if the invoice (created based on the quote via customer order) by default should be printed via printer or e-mail. The setting is loaded from the customer.
