### Accounts payable
Under this tab you find all unpaid as well as ordered but not confirmed supplier invoices in the accounts payable. When loading, a match will be made of the invoices with status Ordered in the accounts payable and the entries/records in the file. It is not possible to deselect invoices from confirmation. This is only possible when performing manual confirmation.
Matched invoices are displayed with green rows. The posting of the row selected, is shown in the box below.
