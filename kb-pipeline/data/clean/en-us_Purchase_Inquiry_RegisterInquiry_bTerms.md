### Terms
In this box you configure different terms for the inquiry. When registering new inquiries, the terms are by default loaded from the supplier selected in the procedure, but it is possible to change the terms, if needed.

#### Payment terms
The payment terms describe the credit time, that is, within which time frame the payment should be made. It is also possible to enter a payment term that is not previously registered. The field Credit period will then open where you must enter the credit period in days.

#### Delivery terms
The delivery terms describe who is responsible for the freight and when/at what point the responsibility for the transport passes from the selling party to the buying party. This will in its turn determine who will pay for the freight, unless the term has been set to freight free/free shipping. It is also possible to enter a delivery term that is not previously registered.

#### Delivery methods
The delivery method describes how or in what way the delivery should be made. It is also possible to enter a delivery method that is not previously registered.
