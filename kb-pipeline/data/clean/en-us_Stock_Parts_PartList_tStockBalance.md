### The Stock balance list
In this list you see stock balance per warehouse, balance per location with traceability, or stock balance per part. Each location is displayed on a separate row. Current balance Current balance is the part balance at this moment on the locations., unit, and warehouse are displayed o the row. The checkbox Exclude balance, determines if the balance should be excluded during net requirement calculation, requirement calculation, and check delivery times. By using the button Warehouse ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_warehouses.png) on the toolbar, you can select which warehouses you want to include in the list.
