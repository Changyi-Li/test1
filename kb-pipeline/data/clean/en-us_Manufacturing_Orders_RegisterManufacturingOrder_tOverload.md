### The Overload tab
If the setting Check overload is activated when a new order is registered by clicking Save, and a work center has or will have an overload, this tab will become activated.
Here you will see the work centers with overload. You can see if a work center already was overloaded prior to the last order registration.
By expanding a work center, you will see the orders, parts and operations on the work center that you have to fix, in case you do not replan the order you just registered. By clicking the button Go to procedure ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_link.png) you can open the procedure Loading plan where you can see the overload. The overload is displayed at week level.
