## Basic data – Fixed assets register
In this procedure you register different basic data for the fixed assets register.
It is mainly fixed assets groups. The fixed assets group determine if depreciating should be made or not for the fixed assets objects linked to the group. The fixed assets group contains depreciation settings and postings for scheduled, calculated, depreciation of fixed assets.
For scheduled depreciation, you can choose from the following methods:
-   
Straight-line
-   
Declining balance
-   
Declining balance + Straight-line.
Here you can also register fixed assets types and locations (places) of the fixed assets. This information is optional. Fixed assets types and locations can for example be used as selections in lists.
There are two system settings regarding the fixed assets register that are good to know. It is the setting Depreciation is based on where you can choose between days or months. It is the setting Depreciation start for new acquisition where you can choose between the options First day of acquisition month, Acquisition date, Month after acquisition date, or First day of acquisition year.
