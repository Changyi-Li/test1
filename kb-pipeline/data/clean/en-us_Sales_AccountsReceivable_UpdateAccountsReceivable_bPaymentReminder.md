### Payment reminder
In this box you handle payment reminders on accounts receivable records. All the information here can be modified for existing accounts receivable records.

#### Payment reminder
With this setting you decide if payment reminders can be sent to the customer for the invoice in question. The setting is loaded from the customer for a new accounts receivable record.

#### Number of payment reminders
In this field it is indicated how many payment reminders have been sent for the invoice in question. The field works the same way as Number of payment reminders in the [list](../ReminderBasis/tListReminderBasisDetailed.htm) in the Payment reminder basis procedure. For a new accounts receivable record, the value is 0 (zero).

#### Last reminder date
Here you can see the date when the last (most recent) payment reminder was sent. For a new accounts receivable record you can here enter a date.
