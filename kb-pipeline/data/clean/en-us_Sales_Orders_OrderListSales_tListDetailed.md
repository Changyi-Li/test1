### The Detailed list
This list shows detailed information about the customer orders that you have selected. If you have activated the updateable mode of the list, you can make changes to the Delivery date and Discount of the orders. If the delivery date is changed this is shown in italics.
> Please note! The linked manufacturing order/purchase order will not be updated when you change the delivery date on the customer order. Instead you need to handle this in the Manufacturing order suggestion or Purchase order suggestion procedure.
The list is loaded and displayed based on the selection and settings you have chosen. As in all lists in Monitor ERP, you can drag and drop in order to change the column to group by.
