### The Write-down of costs list
This list type is used to write down material costs for projects. The price is calculated as per the settings under the Selection tab.
Example of when a write-down of material costs can be useful: 
A purchase order contains 100 pcs of Part A which has been purchased on Project P1.

The standard price is 10 SEK for part A.

The invoice is recorded on project P1 with the price 12 SEK per piece, which results in the material costs for the project being 1200 SEK

As per the manufacturing order that is linked to the project, only 80 pcs of Part A are used. The remaining 20 pcs can be/have been used in another order/project.

To get the correct material cost in project P1, the price is written down by moving 20 pcs from project P1 and storing them unlinked to a project. By using the Write-down of costs list type, material cost is calculated with the price alternative Standard price. When the calculation is saved, a voucher is created in the journal with project P2 on the credit side and no project on the debit side. When the journal is approved in the Print material cost calculation journal procedure, the voucher becomes visible in the project and the material cost for part A in project P1 is reduced by 200 SEK (quantity x standard price) to 1000 SEK.
