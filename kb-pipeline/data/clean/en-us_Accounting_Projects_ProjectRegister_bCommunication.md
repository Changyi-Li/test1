### Communication
In this box you can add internal and external contact information regarding a project.

#### Type
Here you enter the type of communication. It can be phone, fax, e-mail, or mobile phone.

#### E-mail/Phone
Here you enter the e-mail address, phone number, fax number, or mobile phone number.

#### Remark
Here you can enter a remark or description regarding the address/number. An example of an remark here can be that the entered phone number goes to a switchboard operator.

#### Recipient of
If you have selected e-mail as type of communication method then you can in this column choose External project report or Internal project report. If you enter a customer for the project, a check will be made against the customer register to see if a person has been registered as recipient of external project reports in the communication box in the Customer register procedure. If this is the case the row will automatically be added in the communication box in the Project register procedure.
> One row in the table can be the recipient of several of the e-mail alternatives.
