### Invoicing
This tab is only available if there are any linked invoice basis or invoicing logs on the customer order.
In the Invoice basis box you see the invoice bases that exist on the customer order (row type 1 and 2). That is, the deliveries which have not yet been invoiced. Linked credit invoices are also shown.
In the Invoicing log box you see the invoices that exist for the customer order (row type 1 and 2). Linked credit invoices are also shown.
In the Incoming payments box you see information about the incoming payments which have been made for the invoices linked to the customer order. This information is loaded from the list type [Payments](../../AccountsReceivable/AccountsReceivableLedger/tListPayments.htm) in the Accounts receivable list procedure.
