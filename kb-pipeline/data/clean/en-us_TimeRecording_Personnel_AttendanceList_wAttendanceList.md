## Attendance list
In this procedure you can load a list over the personnel who are clocked in, who have clocked out on break, who have clocked in as absent, or who are not clocked in (within or outside the scheduled time).
Only employees with the setting Time recording set to Attendance/Work in the personnel records will be included in the list. Blocked personnel are not included in the list.
In the list you can see the work item a person has in progress and also contact information from the personnel records. There are also functions where you can report absence, enter schedule exceptions or planned absence, and view schedule and schedule exceptions, last recordings, and time balances for the employees.
Under the List tab you can use the Evacuation list... button to create an evacuation. This button will open the Evacuation list procedure.
