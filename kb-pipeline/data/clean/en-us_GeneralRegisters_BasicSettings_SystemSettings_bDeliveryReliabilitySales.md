### Delivery reliability
Here you enter the allowed allowances in calendar days for delivery reliability in the Delivery reliability procedure. In that procedure you can temporarily change the allowances. In that procedure you can also select calendar, that is, if the allowances should apply only to work days (national calendar) or to holidays as well (all calendar days).

#### Default allowance for own delivery reliability – Too early
Here you enter the number of calendar days (not work days) allowed prior to the delivery date before the own delivery will be considered to be too early.

#### Default allowance for own delivery reliability – Too late
Here you enter the number of calendar days (not work days) allowed after the delivery date before the own delivery will be considered to be too late. The default option here is 0 days.
