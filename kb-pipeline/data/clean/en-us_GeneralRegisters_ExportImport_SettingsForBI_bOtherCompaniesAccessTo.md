### Other companies' access to
Here you can deny other companies in the system access to your company’s data in the business intelligence.

#### Deny access
Mark the checkbox for the companies in the system which you want to deny access to your company's data. Please remember that the other company can make exceptions for some of their users to gain access to your company's data.

#### Access granted by other company
If this checkbox is activated it means your company has access to the other company. If the checkbox is not marked it means that the other company has denied your company access. In that case the User's access exemptions for box is made available, regarding the other company. In that box you can make exceptions for specific users of yours, making it possible for the user in question to get explicit access to the other company's data.
