### EDI
In G5 EDIEDI is the acronym of Electronic Data Interchange. EDI is about exchanging electronic business documents with your business partners, e.g. customers and suppliers. The EDI concept can be wide and a bit unclear, and can many times be used about all types of documents which are sent electronically, even if it might be PDF files sent via e-mail or publishing business documents on a website. What we refer to as EDI – and what is traditionally meant by EDI – is structured business documents following given standards, electronically sent or received and which are compiled and interpreted automatically and that is integrated with the customer's/supplier's ERP system. is an option in the same way as EDI is a supplement in G4.
There are major differences between EDI in G4 and in G5. The differences are so great that it is hard to say that there are any direct corresponding functions. What you configure as EDI-links in G4, together with scattered settings for customers and as system settings, have in G5 been gathered in the three setup procedures called EDI channels, EDI behaviors, and EDI configurations. For handling of all EDI business transactions there is a completely new procedure called Manage EDI transactions.

#### EDI channels
Simplified: what should be imported or exported and where this is loaded from or saved to. Here you set up channels containing format, source, target, and a schedule when the channel should run. You select source or target in a channel depending on if it is import or export. You also set up the sources and targets and determine the type: Directory or FTP. You complement the system with the formats, such as order confirmation, customer invoice.

#### EDI behaviors
Simplified: how something should be imported or exported in relation to the business process in G5. Here you find the EDI settings which in G4 are scattered under the Extra info button on customer and as system settings. In this procedure you link the affected customers to different EDI behaviors.

#### EDI configurations
Simplified: links what, where and how into an overall configuration. Here you set up EDI configurations consisting of EDI channels and EDI behaviors.

#### Manage EDI transactions
Simplified: all import and export in EDI business transactions will go through here, it will be logged and monitored in the procedure. Detailed information is shown under separate tabs. Today, this is the tabs Customer orders and Delivery schedules, where you find detailed information about each customer order and delivery schedule you import. You can regard the Customer orders tab and the Delivery schedules tab as equivalents to what you find in the procedures called Import EDI Orders and Import EDI Delivery Schedules in G4.
What in G4 is called "MEDI2 log" and "MEDI" can in a simplified way be said to be included in the procedure called Manage EDI transactions and this is the part of EDI which in G5 we refer to as EDI engine (this is the part of EDI which you don't see, it runs and handles import and export of EDI transactions). The MEDI2 log in G4 is a text file on the Monitor server which is called Medi2.log. In G5 it is a part of the database. In the procedure Manage EDI transactions, you can easily access that log for a certain transaction by clicking the button ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_more_info.png) in the column called Log.
