### FAQ – Basic data Sustainability
Is climate data added to the existing Part register or is a separate Part register created with CO2e per part?
In the existing Part register procedure you find the Sustainability tab where you register climate data. By supplementing data already contained in the ERP system with information on energy consumption and carbon dioxide equivalents (CO2e),Monitor ERP can calculate your carbon footprint at component level and help structure emissions at company level (according to Scope 1, 2 and 3).
Is it also possible to link a comment or source reference to how the CO2e value was identified for a particular type of material? E.g., if it were to be reported when reviewing a customer or in a similar context.
There is no field specifically created for this type of information, but you can use the Extra field in the Part register procedure to enter a comment or source reference.
Can a part have multiple material types?
No. A part can only have one material type.
If I have two different suppliers of the same product. Can I choose which supplier the emission data should be loaded from?
No, the data is loaded from the active supplier. If you have alternative suppliers for the part, information about the emissions is displayed in the Purchase order suggestion procedure so you can make the best choice for the environment!
Is it possible to link the supplier's climate documents to the emission data in the Part register?
There is no specific function for handling/linking these documents in particular, but there are several ways to link files in the Part register.
Is it possible to update weight or CO2e data in a list instead of entering the info for each part?
Yes, in the Sustainability list procedure you can do mass updates of emission data.
What responsibility do I have for the input emission data from my suppliers being correct?
In cases where you buy material from a supplier and they show the CO2e value on the invoice, you should be able to trust it and they are responsible for these values being correct.
What do the different colors in Classification of part contents mean?
The colors show which class a chemical is according to REACH. The available classifications are Green, Yellow, and Red, where Green is the least harmful and Red is the most harmful.
How can I attach a REACH declaration (in form of a text) on a delivery note to a customer?
Our standard documents cannot show this information as the information our customers want on the documents varies so much. You can however make changes to the documents to show the desired information. You can order document adaptations from our Adaptations Department if no one in your company is able to make the desired changes.
