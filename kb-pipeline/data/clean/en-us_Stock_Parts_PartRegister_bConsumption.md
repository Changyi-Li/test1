### Consumption
In this box you will see information about arrival and consumption/delivery. The information is loaded from the stock transaction log for the part.

#### Last arrival date
In this field you see the last arrival date of the part.

#### Last consumption date
This field shows the last consumption date or delivery date of the part.

#### Consumption statistics
By clicking this button you access information about the consumption of the part on a monthly basis. The stock transactions that are regarded as consumption are:
- Delivery
- Material withdrawal – Manufacturing order
- Direct withdrawal
- Invoices that have been directly registered
