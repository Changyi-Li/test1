### Date
In this box you can see/enter different dates and times regarding the inquiry.

#### Inquiry date
In this field you enter the date from when the inquiry applies. Today's date is suggested by default. The inquiry date will be printed on the inquiry.

#### Response time
Here you enter the response time. It indicates how quickly you wish to get a response to the inquiry, in number of calendar days as of the inquiry date. The default response time is loaded from the supplier on the inquiry. You can select among response times registered in the Terms procedure. You can also enter a text in the Response time field that is not registered in the terms. In that case, you must enter the number of calendar days in the field below.

#### Response date
The response date is by default set to inquiry date + response time, but this can be changed. The response date is printed on the inquiry, and it is also used to monitor the inquiry, in order to see which inquiries that are about to pass their response date.

#### Valid through
The valid through field is empty by default for new inquiries. Here you can enter the valid through date that the supplier has entered in their response to the company. The valid through date can be used as a selection term in different lists.

#### Delivery time
The delivery time is a type of term that by default is empty for new inquiries, but you can enter it when you have received a response in form of a quote from the supplier. You can select among delivery times registered in the Terms procedure. You can also enter a text in the Delivery time field that is not registered in the terms. In that case, you must enter the number of calendar days in the field below.

#### Last contact date
The last contact date is empty by default for new inquiries. In this field you can enter the date when you last were in contact with the supplier regarding the inquiry.
