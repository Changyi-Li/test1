### Settings
These settings are only activated if integration to the accounting is applied for outgoing payment journals. These settings are made for the journal Outgoing payments in the procedure called Voucher number series/Journals. Next to the heading Integration under the settings at the bottom of the window, you will see a text regarding if integration is applied, and in that case, what type of integration.

#### Enter voucher date manually
With this setting you determine if you manually can enter a specific voucher date, e.g. if old records belong to a locked period for which you wish to load the journal.

#### Voucher date
If the above setting is activated, you here enter the specific voucher date.
> When a voucher date has been entered manually, the date will be displayed in red on the journal. This is done to clearly show that the payments will be recorded on another date than the payment date. Please note that this function should be used with caution, since it can cause differences between the accounts payable and the general ledger!
