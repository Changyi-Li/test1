#### Amount
Here you see the invoiced value, that is, price each × quantity (taking discounts and setup prices into consideration, if any).

#### CM
In this column you see the contribution margin. It is the invoiced value minus the cost based on the selected price alternative:
- Standard price – Using this alternative, the CM will be based on the current standard price in the part register. For invoice rows of row type 2, the CM is based on the manually entered standard price on the invoice row.
- Standard price at delivery or Standard price at invoicing – If any of these alternatives is used, the CM will primarily be based on a manually entered standard price on the invoice row. This applies to all row types. Secondarily, the CM will be based on the standard price which applied at the time of delivery or invoicing.
- Other price alternatives – If you have selected any of the other price alternatives, CM will be based on the selected price alternative, regardless of type of invoice row and whether or not a standard price has been manually entered on the invoice row.
