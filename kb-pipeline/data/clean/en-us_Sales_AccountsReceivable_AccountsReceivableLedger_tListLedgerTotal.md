### The Ledger – Total list
This list is a totaled information of invoices with invoice amount, and remaining amount per a certain ledger date. These amounts are also shown in foreign currency. By activating a setting under the Selection tab, you can also choose to include fully paid invoices. You can total the list by customer, accounts receivable account, and currency. If you total the list by customer, you can see the number of invoices and you can also expand each row to show detailed information. At the bottom of the list you see a bar chart of the remaining amount. Settings regarding the presentation in chart form is accessed with the Chart button ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_chart.png).
