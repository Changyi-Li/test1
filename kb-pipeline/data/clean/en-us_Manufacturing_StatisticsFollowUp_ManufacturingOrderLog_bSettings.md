### Settings
Here you can make different settings for the lists, except for the list type Material – Detailed. No settings are available for this list.

#### Cost factors
For the lists Operations – Detailed and Operations – Total you can choose the cost factors which should be basis for the calculation of reported cost and planned cost for reported quantity. The costs that are selected in the system setting Default cost factors will here be shown as default.

#### Only quantity from final operation
For the list Operations – Total you can choose to only include quantity from the final operation on every level in the structure.

#### Show undone reporting items
For the lists Operations – Detailed and Operations – Total you can choose to also include/show undone reporting items or to only shown undone reporting items. The default option is to not show undone reporting items. This setting affects operations which have been undone in the procedure Undo reporting (in the Manufacturing module) and in the procedure Undo work recording (in the Time recording module), and subcontracts which have had their arrivals undone in the procedure Undo arrival reporting (in the Purchase module).

#### Only material without remaining quantity
For the list Material – Total it is possible to choose to only include material which has been reported in full, that is, material with zero as remaining quantity.
