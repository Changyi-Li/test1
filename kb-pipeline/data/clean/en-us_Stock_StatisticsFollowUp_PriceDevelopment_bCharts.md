### Chart
In the chart you see the price development regarding sales prices, purchase prices, and user-defined prices, as lines. You can see trends in the price development for the selected date interval.
The bars in the chart display sales prices and purchase prices in total for the chart interval configured. Next to the value axis you see grading for purchase total and sales total.
The category axis is graded according to the configured chart interval.
If you place the cursor over the chart, you see a line for the period in question in the chart interval and the different amounts in the period are shown in a tooltip.
