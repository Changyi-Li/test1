### Purchase order
If the invoice is an order invoice this box is used. It contains the links that have been made to purchase order when registering the invoice. You who are a signer, can check the rows against the invoice. It is also possible to make adjustments of the rows, for example on prices, postings, etc. After this you authorize/approve the invoice.
New links can also be created. For example this might be done if the invoice is registered and sent for authorization before the purchase order has been arrival reported. You can then watch for the arrival and link the order to the invoice when the order has been arrival reported.
In Order rows box, you can see the order rows of the purchase orders you have selected to include.
