### Settings – Factoring

#### Payment method
In this field you select with which payment method you wish to confirm. You can choose from those of the Factoring type. Settings for the payment method are made in the Bank settings procedure.

#### Clearing account
With this setting you decide which clearing account you want to confirm the payments for.

#### Period
Determines which period you want to confirm payments for. With the Load transactions button ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_import.png), the bank statement is automatically loaded from Fedelta containing the transactions for the selected period.

#### Voucher number series
Here you see the voucher number series for the voucher. Voucher number series must first be created in the procedure Voucher number series/Journals.

#### Voucher text
Here you select the text that will be used as voucher text for incoming payments that you register. The default text is Factoring. In the Voucher texts procedure you can create texts to use as pre-defined voucher texts.
