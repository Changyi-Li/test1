### Delivery/Shipping

#### Freight payer
The freight payer is by default loaded form the selected delivery terms to this field. You can change freight payer for the record in question. See a description of the meaning of the different options below.
- Purchaser pays – the customer number at the shipping agent is loaded from the Company information procedure for the shipping agent which is linked to the selected delivery method for the record. This number cannot be changed.
- Seller pays – the customer number at the shipping agent is loaded from the Supplier register procedure for the shipping agent which is linked to the selected delivery method for the record. This number cannot be changed.
- Other payer – the customer number at the shipping agent must be manually entered in the field below.
- Incoterms – the customer number at the shipping agent is loaded from the delivery term in the Terms procedure.

#### Customer number, shipping agent
The customer number at the shipping agent is here shown based on the freight payer selected in the setting above. This means that the customer number at shipping agent is determined both by delivery terms and delivery method. If freight payer has been set to Other payer you should manually enter the customer number, shipping agent in the field. This information is printed on order documents when the setting called Show customer number at shipping agent has been activated in the Document settings procedure.

#### Place of terms of delivery
You only need to enter the place of terms of delivery if it is not the city entered in the delivery address. The place of terms of delivery is loaded from the customer register and describes the place or city where the financial responsibility for the shipped goods will pass to another party. This information is printed on order documents when the setting Show place of terms of delivery has been activated in the Document settings procedure.

#### Transport time
Here you see the transport time in number of work days. This is loaded from the supplier selected for the record, but the transport time can be changed, if needed. You might for example use another delivery method for the record than what you normally use. The transport time affects which delivery day is shown on the documents. If there is no transport time, the day for arrival at our warehouse is shown. If a transport time has been entered you will see the delivery date when the shipment is to be sent from the supplier. The label on the document is also changed/affected by this. You also see the transport time in the label.
