### Move stock balance

#### Group balance on locations with the same name
- No – With this option you decide that a quantity of a part which has been selected to be moved and to be distributed over several new locations with the same name, will be distributed to the new locations.
- Yes – With this option you decide that the quantity of a part which has been selected to be moved and to be distributed to several new locations with the same name, will be grouped together to one balance for one new location.
- Decide each time – With this option you will be shown a dialog in the Parts to move box. In that dialog you get to choose of you want to group the balances or keep them separated for locations with the same name.

#### Mandatory to enter person in Move stock balance
With this setting you determine if it should be mandatory to enter the person who performed the transfer/move in the Move stock balance procedure.

#### Allow moving of balance cleared by printed pick lists
With this setting you decide if it should be allowed to move a stock balance which has been cleared by printed pick lists.
