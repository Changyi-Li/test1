### Report activity – Project
- New field for activities: Remaining time.
- Enter a comment and link files for the reporting.
- Review the comments and linked files for the activity.
- It is possible to see earlier reporting items for the activity in question.
- Reference and Performed by will be filled in automatically by the system. Reference is loaded from the responsible for activity, and "Performed by" is taken from the user who is logged in and is performing the actual reporting.
