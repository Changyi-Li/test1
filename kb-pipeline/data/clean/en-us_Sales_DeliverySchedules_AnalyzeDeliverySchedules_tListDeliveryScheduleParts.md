### List type delivery schedule parts
The list type Delivery schedule Silf (the Swedish association for purchase and logistics) explain the term "delivery plan" in the following way: A delivery schedule is a plan/schedule for deliveries from supplier to customer. The delivery schedule is created by customer and generally contains a planning horizon of 0,5–1 year. Normally the delivery schedule quantities are assigned different statuses depending on the type of demand. It is common that for example the entered quantities in the immediate future (closest in time) actually are fixed orders. In an interval of a few months ahead of the fixed orders, the entered quantities might be considered as preliminary orders for which the customer is obliged to take financial responsibility for any material purchased by the supplier. The subsequent quantities entered are considered to be forecast only. (Translated from source https://www.silf.se/tjanster/ordlista-for-inkop-och-logistik/l/ [2018-08-29]). A delivery schedule is a way to increase the transparency and thereby make it possible to mutually take charge of the financial situation across multiple steps in the supply chain. This is done by transferring information regarding the immediate demands/requirements as well as future forecast demands. parts with the Deviations presentation gives you a heads-up regarding deviation alarm signals or warnings. The deviations are not shown in detail in this list, but you can use the context menu to go to the Handle delivery schedules procedure where you can see the details.
In the presentation called Transfer warnings and errors you can select by different types of warnings and errors to get an overview of part related problems which have occurred in the processing of delivery schedules.
Errors that can be displayed in the Transfer warnings and errors presentation:
- A customer order row was missing during the transfer.
- The transfer could not be executed due to an error.
Warnings that can be displayed in the Transfer warnings and errors presentation:
- The delivery note reconciliation failed to match any delivery notes.
- No previous delivery schedule was replaced.
- The transfer could not be executed due to an error.
- The customer order has been modified after the reconciliation and must therefore be reconciled again prior to transfer.
- The cumulative reconciliation did not find a start date in Monitor.
- The cumulative reconciliation did not find a start date for the delivery schedule part.
- Cumulative quantity, start date differs from received start date.
- "Cumulative quantity – Delivered" is less than "Cumulative quantity – Received".
