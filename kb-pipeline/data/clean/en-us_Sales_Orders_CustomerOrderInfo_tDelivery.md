### Delivery
In the Delivery log box you see a log containing the deliveries made for customer order rows (row type 1 and 2). The list is primarily sorted by the customer order's position, and secondarily by actual delivery date (ascending). The information is loaded from the [Delivery log](../../StatisticsFollowUp/DeliveryLog/wDeliveryLog.htm) procedure.
In the Delivery reliability box you see the delivery reliability for the order rows. The information is loaded from the [Delivery reliability](../../StatisticsFollowUp/DeliveryReliability/wDeliveryReliability.htm) procedure. Under this list you can configure what to show by using the [settings](../../StatisticsFollowUp/DeliveryReliability/bSettings.htm) (which are the same as you find in that procedure). However, the setting Include stray customers is not available in the Customer order info procedure.
