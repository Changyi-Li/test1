## Agreement management
The purpose of this description is to show how the agreement management works in the Sales module.
![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/UserGuide/Agreement1.png)
Agreements are intended to be used to invoice, for example, a service according a fixed invoicing interval. Unlike a customer order, for example, an agreement never results in stock transactions.
