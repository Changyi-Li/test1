### Customer order transfer
- The status from the supplier's customer order is automatically transferred to the purchase order in the sales company. This information is used to determine if changes are allowed or not on the sales company's linked customer order.
- If you in a sales company register a quote for a part in a production company via remote configuration, you will now get the calculated standard price and net weight calculated and saved on the row.
- From a sales company it is possible to run a CDTCDT is short for check delivery times and it is a function on order rows which calculates when the order row in question can be delivered, taking lead times and throughput times into consideration. CDT also checks if existing orders and suggestions can cover material shortages, if any, and affects when the order row can be delivered. in a production company also for configured parts.
- You can configure settings to make the manufacturing order become automatically replanned in the production company. The status on the manufacturing order determine if replanning is allowed or not.
- If you work with parts with serial numbers produced in the production company, it is now possible to transfer serial number to the sales company as early as when the customer order is created in the production company. This may be useful if you wish to show serial numbers on the order confirmation to the customers, and such confirmations are sent from the sales company. There is a new setting for this called "Create serial numbers". You find it in the Settings for customer order transfer procedure. In order for this to work, you need to have selected the option "At registration of order" for the parts in the production company in the setting "Create serial no. at customer order". In the sales company this setting must be "No" for the parts.
