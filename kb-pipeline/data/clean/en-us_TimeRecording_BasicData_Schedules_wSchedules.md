## Schedules
In this procedure you can create schedules. A schedule can be a work schedule or an overtime schedule and describes a work shift.
You can then add schedules together and create cycles in the procedure called Schedule cycles. The cycles describes for example a 7-day or 14-day rolling cycle. A cycle must consist of at least one week (7 days).
