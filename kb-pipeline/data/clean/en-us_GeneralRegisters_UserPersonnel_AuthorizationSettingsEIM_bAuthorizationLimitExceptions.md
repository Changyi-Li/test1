### Authorization limits (Authorization limit exceptions)
In this box you add authorization limits, head signers, and terms per authorization limit that shall be included in the exception. You can determine the order of the authorization limits in the list. This is important if you have created several authorization limits where the terms for the limits are based on different types of records.
For example: For supplier number 5000, authorization limit 1 should apply. For supplier category A, authorization limit 2 should apply. For supplier category B, authorization limit 3 should apply. Since supplier number 5000 also can belong to category A, the system follows the priority of the limits in the list. The limit at the top primarily applies. You can move the limits up and down by using the arrow buttons on the function menu in the box.

#### Name
Here you can write a descriptive text as a name. You enter name texts in the company language and they are displayed in the user’s language.

#### Translations
By using the button Translations ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_translate.png) you can translate the text to the different active languages registered in the system. Read more about [language management](../../../UserGuide/GeneralFeatures/GeneralFeatures.htm#LanguageManagement) for translatable texts.

#### Amount up to
The actual authorization limit entered in the company currency. The amount is the amount on the invoice, excluding VAT.

#### Head signer
A signer who is the current singer's superior manager.
