### Register shipment
- This procedure is simplified considerably than corresponding procedure in G4 called Print Shipping Documents. This is because G5 does not handle very much shipping agent specific information and also there are not separate documents and printout options. These are instead handled by nShift.
- Using an export function in the procedure the shipment is registered at nShift and you order printing of shipping documents. The shipment is exported from the procedure (using a web request) either to nShift Delivery or to nShift Web-TA (depending on which service you will use). nShift will in their turn generate a shipment number and send it back to together with the printout. The printout goes to the printer you have configured as printer for shipment documents.
