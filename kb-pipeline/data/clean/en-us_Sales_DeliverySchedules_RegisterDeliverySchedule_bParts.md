### Parts
In this box you can add parts which should be included in the delivery schedule and enter information as described below.
On the function menu there are buttons you can use to add and delete part rows. There is also a button you can use to go to the related procedures for the marked part.

#### Part
Here you see/enter the part number.

#### Name
Here you see/enter the name of the part.

#### Unit
Here you see the part's unit for customer order. If you change unit you will get a question if the quantity in the calls should be recalculated using the conversion factor for the selected unit.

#### Port
Here you see/enter the dock which is entered for the part and to which it should be delivered.

#### Storage
Here you see/enter the storage which is entered for the part and to which it should be delivered.

#### Customer's order number
In this column you see/enter the customer’s order number.

#### Status
Here you see the delivery schedule's status on this part level.

#### Dock description
Here you can enter a text describing the dock.

#### Handling code
Here you can enter how the information in the delivery schedule for this part, should be handled in relation to earlier delivery schedules.

#### Calculation date
Here you see/enter the date which the customer enter as the basis used when calculating this part's requirement in the delivery schedule.

#### Customer's order row position
Here you see/enter the customer's order row position for this part.

#### Customer's delivery schedule no.
Here you see/enter the customer's delivery schedule number for this part.

#### Customer's delivery schedule date
Here you see/enter the customer's date on the delivery schedule for this part.

#### Customer's previous delivery schedule no.
Here you see/enter the customer's entered preceding delivery schedule number for this part.

#### Customer's previous delivery schedule date
Here you see/enter the customer's date on the preceding delivery schedule for this part.

#### Drawing number
Here you see the entered drawing number for the part.

#### Revision
Here you see the entered revision for the part.

#### Cumulative quantity, start date
Here you enter a start date for the cumulative quantity from the delivery schedule. You can edit the start date as long as the Status on the part level of the delivery schedule is New or Activated.

#### Cumulative quantity – Received
Here you see the cumulative quantity which has been received from the deliver schedule. The quantity is displayed using two decimals. You can edit the the quantity as long as the Status on the part level of the delivery schedule is New or Activated.
