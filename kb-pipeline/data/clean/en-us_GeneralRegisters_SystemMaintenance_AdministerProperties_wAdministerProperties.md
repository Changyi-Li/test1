## Administer properties
In this procedure you centrally administer different property sets created in different procedures.
In a detailed view you can see the content of the property set. You can delete property sets. You can also remove or add them based on users, groups, and roles.
The procedure is only visible and available to users who have access to the procedure.
Read about [Property management](../../../UserGuide/GeneralFeatures/PropertyManagement.htm).
