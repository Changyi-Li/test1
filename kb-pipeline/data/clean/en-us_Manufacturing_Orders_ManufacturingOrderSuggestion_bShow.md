### Show
Here you find settings regarding how the order suggestions in the list should be shown.

#### Order suggestion
Here you determine if manufacturing order suggestions, rescheduling suggestions, and suggestions to delete unnecessary orders should be displayed in the list. The available options are:
- Suggestion – Manufacturing order (default) – This alternative will include the manufacturing order suggestions.
- Rescheduling – In (default) – This alternative will include rescheduling suggestions that advance the date of the orders.
- Rescheduling – In, within lead time (default) – This alternative will include rescheduling suggestions that advance the date of the orders within the lead time.
- Rescheduling – Out – This alternative will include rescheduling suggestions that will postpone the date of the orders.
- Unnecessary orders (orders without requirement) – This alternative will include suggestions of necessary orders to delete.
- Quantity shortage linked order – This alternative finds orders which have a linked refill where the quantity is insufficient.

#### Existing suggestions/orders
This setting is useful when you choose to show rescheduling suggestions. By selecting which order types to take into consideration, your results may vary regarding what is the causing requirement. This setting is not available if you in the Order suggestionOrder suggestions are suggestions made for manufacturing or purchase orders generated by the system in order to cover stock shortages of manufactured parts as well as purchased parts. setting select only Suggestion – Purchase order.

#### Include parts without reservations
With this setting you can decide whether or not to show parts without reservations in the list.

#### Include orders marked as Confirmed delay
With this setting you can include orders marked as Confirmed delay.

#### Consumption statistics
This setting determines whether or not the parts’ consumption statistics for the last 3, 6, and 12 months will be displayed in the list.
