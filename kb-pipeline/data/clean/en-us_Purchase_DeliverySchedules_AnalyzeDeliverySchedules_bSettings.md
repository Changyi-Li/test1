### Settings

#### Price alternative
Here you choose from where the part price should be loaded. This price is then used if you choose the Amount option under the List tab. The following options are available:
- Standard price – Will load price from the part’s current standard price. This option is selected by default.
- Future standard price – Will load price from the part’s future standard price.
- Supplier price – Will load price from the supplier link.

#### Use first period containing requirement as first shown period in the result under the List tab
This setting is not activated by default, which means that the list will always use the second period column for the current period (date/week or month).
If the setting is activated, it means that the period within the selected interval which has the earliest requirement the period column will be the one that is presented as the first visible period column, at the far left in the list.
