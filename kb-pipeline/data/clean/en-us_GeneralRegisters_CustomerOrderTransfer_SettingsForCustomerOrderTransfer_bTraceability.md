### Traceability

#### Document path
If the production company delivery reports customer orders including traceable parts and these parts have linked certificates, the certificates will be transferred to the sales company when the corresponding purchase order is arrival reported in the sales company. Here you then select in which path these linked certificates should be saved.
The paths that are the registered in the Paths procedure can be selected in this field.
You can preview the certificates in the Serial number A serial number is a number that is used for traceability for parts on entity level./Batch A batch is the set of components/products manufactured at the same time and made from the same original material. procedure in the sales company. The serial number/batch number is loaded from that procedure and the certificates are then available under the Files ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_file_linked.png) button next to the Certificate heading.

#### Create serial number
If you work with parts that have serial numbers created in the production company, this setting will determine if the serial numbers should be transferred to the sales company when Delivery reporting in production company or at Order registration in production company. The default option is at delivery reporting.
You configure that serial number is created at order registration if you want to show the serial numbers from the very start on order confirmations sent from the sales company to the customers. In the sales company this setting must be No for the parts.
