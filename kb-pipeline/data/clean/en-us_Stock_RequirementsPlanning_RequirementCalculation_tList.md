### The List tab
At the top of the list you can see the parts which are loaded based on the selection and the settings. The standard list is a simple list with one part per row. The other list types are grouped by supplier and administrator.
In the list you can see the part's planning settings which are entered in the Part register procedure. You can also see the parts’ current status regarding balances, actual orders, and reservations. That is, the prerequisites for the requirement calculation. You can trace the suggestions you get in the planning window after the requirement calculation, based on this information. A few of the columns are explained in detail below.
Warnings – Here you see a warning symbol ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/warning.png) if any warnings appeared during the requirement calculation. A tooltip over the symbol displays an explanation to the warning.
Balance – Here you see the part's total balance. It is shown in black text if the balance is the same as, or greater than, the safety stock. The balance is displayed in red if it has fallen below the safety stock.
Ordered – Here you see the ordered quantity from actual manufacturing orders, purchase orders, and incoming stock orders. Reserved – Here you see the total reserved quantity from sales such as customer orders and outgoing stock orders. In the planning window below, you can see separate orders, reservations, and type.
Disposable balanceThe disposable balance is the current part balance on the locations at a given time minus reserved quantity plus ordered quantity. –Here you see the disposable balance on the final row in the planning window. The value is shown i black text if the balance is the same as, or greater than, the safety stock. The balance is displayed in red if it has fallen below the safety stock. Please note! You might have selected by date which can make the planning window limited timewise. Disposable balance can be displayed with two to six decimals.
Lead timeNumber of days between ordering date and delivery date. Normally used for purchased parts. – Here you see the lead time in work days primarily from the default supplier link and secondarily from the general if it is a purchased part. If it is a manufactured part, the saved throughput time is shown. However, if there is a manually entered throughput time, this time will be shown instead. If the part is refilled from warehouse (via a planning setting configured for the part), you will see the general lead time plus the internal supplier's (warehouse's) transport time. In that case the lead time is displayed in italics. A tooltip on the value explains that the supplier's transport time is included in the lead time.

#### Summary/Filtering
In the middle section of the tab you find filtering columns. Here you can select if you only wish to show certain types of suggestions and orders. This section also displays summaries of how many rows exist for each type.

#### Planning window
In the bottom section you see a planning window for the part marked in the list. Here you can sort according to the requirement date or delivery date. You can also link to related procedures. You can unpin this section from the procedure window by using the button Floating ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_float_window.png) if you need more space for the planning window.
