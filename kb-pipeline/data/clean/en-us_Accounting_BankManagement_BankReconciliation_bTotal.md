### Total
Here you see the total from the bank statement and the total in the general ledger.

#### Opening balance
Here you see the balance in the bank and the balance for the account in the general ledger at the start of the date entered in the selection.

#### Left to reconcile
Here you see a total of what is left to reconcile in the Bank statement rows box.

#### Rec. balance.
Here you see the total reconciled balance in the bank statement. This is calculated as the opening balance plus reconciled amount in the bank statement.

#### Closing balance
Here you see the closing balance in the bank statement and in the general ledger at the end of the date entered in the selection.
