### The Our lead time list
The Our lead time list displays information about the quotes you selected. It is grouped per customer. The information shown here is, for example, inquiry date, printout date, and the difference between these two dates (the lead time). For quotes that have not been printed (that still have status Registered), the lead time is calculated using today’s date as printout date.
The list is loaded and displayed based on the selection and settings you have chosen. As in all lists in Monitor ERP, you can drag and drop in order to change the column to group by.
