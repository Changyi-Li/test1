### Subcontract
- Printing of subcontract purchase order – You do the printouts in the Print purchase order or Subcontract documents/Shipped procedures, where multiple orders to the same supplier can be printed using a joint collective number.
- Shimpent of subcontract – You report how many parts you have shipped in the Subcontract documents/Shipped procedure.
- Report arrival – The arrival reporting of subcontract purchase orders are made as regular material purchases in the Report arrival procedure in the Purchase module.
