### The Total list
This list shows total information about the customer orders that you have selected. Each row can be expanded using the drilldown function, and then you can see the detailed information. It is not possible to update any information in this list.
In the lower part of the tab you will see the amounts in chart form. Under the Chart button ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_chart.png) you can change what data to show and how to show it in the chart.
The list is loaded and displayed based on the selection and settings you have chosen. As in all lists in Monitor ERP, you can drag and drop in order to change the column to group by.
