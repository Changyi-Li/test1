### The Confirmation list
This list contains information about all supplier invoices, including credit invoices, that have been sent in payment files to the bank and should be confirmed as paid.
The information you can update in the list is what is mainly described below.

#### Confirm
With this checkbox you select which invoices should be confirmed as paid.

#### Payment date
Here you see the payment date of the invoice. It is possible to change the date. A validation will be made to make sure that the date belongs to an open period. You cannot confirm a payment if the date does not belong to an open period. Then a warning appears informing you that the date does not belong to the current period.
You can change the payment date without having to execute the payments. This can be necessary if you for example have sent an adjustment task to the bank regarding changed payment date.

#### Paid amount
Here you see the paid amount in the currency of the invoice. You can modify the paid amount for credit invoices. If you modify the paid amount, then Partial payment will be suggested in the Write-off column.

#### Exchange rate
Here you see the exchange rate that applies to the payment. If the invoice is issued in another currency than the company currency, you can here change the exchange rate. The system setting Default exchange rate during outgoing payments determines the exchange rate suggested also when confirming the payment, according to the same logic as during manual payment.

#### Bank charge
Here you can enter the amount, if any, that the bank has charged for the payment order.

#### Write-off
The write-off determines if the invoice will remain in the accounts payable ledger with the remaining amount and how the posting will be made when writing off. If the invoice is a credit invoice, it is possible to change the write-off. Partial payment is selected by default. Write-off codes are handled in the Bank settings procedure.
More info

#### Supplier number
Here you see the supplier’s number or code.

#### Initial rate
Here you see the initial rate of the invoice in the accounts payable ledger.

#### Invoice amount in foreign currency
Here you see the invoice amount in the currency in which the invoice was registered.

#### Invoice amount
Here you see invoice amount in the company currency.

#### Ordered amount
Here you can see the amount that was ordered for payment in the payment file.

#### Due date
Here you can see the invoice’s due date which was included in the payment file.

#### Recipient account
Here you can see the supplier’s bank account which was included in the payment file.

#### Sender account
Here you can see our bank account which was included in the payment file. Recipient account and in particular Sender account determine how the outgoing payment will be posted when it is confirmed.
