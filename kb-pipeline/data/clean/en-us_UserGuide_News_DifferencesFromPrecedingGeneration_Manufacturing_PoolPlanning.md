### Pool planning
- This is a new procedure you can use to priority plan operations in work centers of the type Pool and its related work centers.
- The primary function is to move the operations from the pool work center to any of its related work centers.
- You can get suggestions of which work center to move to based on the lading in each related work center, in order to plan the operation to the work center which can carry out the operation at the earliest time.
