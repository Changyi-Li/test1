### Settings – Manufacturing order

#### Material
- Include cost for rejection – Here you decide if rejected quantity should be included in the statistics.

#### Miscellaneous
- Show values per unit – With this checkbox you decide if the list should display the values per unit.
- Include already excluded orders – Here you decide if orders which have been excluded from the post-calculation of different reasons, should be included in the list. Orders that are excluded are orders that you do not want to include in calculation because they are not representative for different reasons, e.g., it can be a prototype order.

#### Overhead
- Overhead year – Here you can choose which year's overhead costs to base the statistics on.
- Overhead type – Here you can choose the overhead type for the statistics. The overhead type is loaded from the Company emission register procedure.
- Use OH from parts – If this setting is checked, a statistics calculation can be done for multiple parts simultaneously, each with a different overhead. The overhead entered for parts in the Part register is used.
- Emission factor OH – Here you see the emissions factor for the year selected in the field above.
- Allocation key for OH – Here you see the allocation key for OH for the year selected in the field above.
