### Post-calculation
Video: Post-calculation (Swedish)
- The procedures Post-Calculation and Post-Calculation Selection from G4 have been merged into one procedure.
- However, the lists where you could follow up on work center and operations over time have been placed in a separate procedure called Operation follow-up.
- The name of the main part is shown on the summary.
- The order text (order additional text in G4) is also shown under the List tab.
- You can select to where the result from the order should be saved (to Manufacturing cost, Cost price, Quote price).
- More price alternatives to compare to, for example post-calculated mean price.
- New tab: Level list.
- Alternative calculation of setup cost when you have planned but not reported setup time.
- Ability to save the result to several prices at the same time.
- In the Post-calculation procedure it is possible to choose if the cost for rejection on operation should be included or not.
- You can now perform post-calculation grouped by Part code. This allows you to analyze the products ranges for which the company earns the most/least money.
- You can choose to save the planned cost as a complement to the reported cost.
