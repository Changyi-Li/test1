### Price lists
- In G5 you can translate the names of price lists making it possible for the user to see the price lists in his/her own language. (Translation of names and descriptions is a general function in G5.)
- You can delete price lists which are not used. When deleting, a check is made to see if the price list is in use and if so, it cannot be deleted.
- In G5 you can change currency in price lists and by default a recalculation to the correct exchange rate where the price list is used. But the recalculation is optional. No calculation is made in G4. If you in G4 have, for example, a sales price of EUR 75 and then change the currency to USD, this will result in a sales price of USD 75.
