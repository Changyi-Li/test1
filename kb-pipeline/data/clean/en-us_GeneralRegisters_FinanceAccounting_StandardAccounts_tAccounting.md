### Accounting
Under this tab you will see standard accounts related to the Accounting module.
The tab is similar to the other tabs in the procedure. However, you cannot expand the account type rows.
The following accounts are only used for the percentage of completion method in the project accounting:
- Recognized income, not invoiced (balance account)
- Recognized income, continuous price
- Recognized income, fixed price
- Reduce of income/Anticipated loss (balance account)
- Offset account for advances booked on balance sheet (PCM calculation)
