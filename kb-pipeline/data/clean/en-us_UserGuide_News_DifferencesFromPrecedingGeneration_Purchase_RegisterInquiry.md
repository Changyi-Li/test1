### Register inquiry
- The order type Inquiry is now implemented and it determines certain settings and it functions as a selection term.
-   
You find the Supplier's part number under the More info button under the Rows tab in the Register inquiry procedure.
- Inquiry category is implemented.
- The sub-number management is now replaced with the ability to create new inquiries which are related.
- There is a button for price comment on inquiry rows. By clicking the button you see the standard price of the part and all of the supplier's supplier links on the part.
- More settings when using Save as and when you turn an inquiry into an order.
