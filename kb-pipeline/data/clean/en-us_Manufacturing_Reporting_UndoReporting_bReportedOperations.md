### Reported operations
Here you find information about the reported operations that are being undone for the reporting selected in the Reporting box.

#### Part node
Here you can see the part number of the part node in the order structure.

#### Operation number
In this column you can see the operation number of the order.

#### Work center
Here you can see the work center that reported the operation.

#### Name
Here you can see the name of the operation on the order.

#### Reported quantity
In this column you can see the reported quantity of the part that will be undone.

#### Reported time
In this column you can see the reported time of the operation that will become undone.

#### Rejected quantity
Here you can see the rejected quantity, if any, of the part that will be undone.
