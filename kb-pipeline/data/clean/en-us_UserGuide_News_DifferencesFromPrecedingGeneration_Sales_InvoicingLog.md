### Invoicing log
- You can see CMThe contribution margin (CM) is the difference between the standard price and the sales price. and CRThe contribution ratio (CR) is the portion of the invoice amount (sales price) that the contribution margin represents. CR is entered as a percentage. on row level.
- You can see row type 4.
- Handling of stray customer (occasional customer).
- The setting called Price alternative for CM/CR has been divided in two. Now there are two separate settings; one for purchased parts and one for manufactured parts. This way you can have different basis for CM/CR depending on part type.
- Now you can print/e-mail/export PDF files for many customer invoices at the same time (reprint) from this procedure. This can be useful when, for example, exporting customer invoices for an entire year to a directory. This funciton is available via a new button in the function menu in the Detailed list type.
