### The Detailed – Case list
This list type is grouped by case in a detailed view. Each case is shown on a separate row in the list. Some columns have been merged. The columns show either customer number or supplier number, and customer name or supplier name. Since a case cannot have information filled in for both customer and supplier, the columns have been given this design. This also applies for case number and reference.

#### No. of rejections
In this column you can see the number of rejections for the case in question.

#### Planned and Actual cost
In these columns you see totals of all costs of the case. This can be shown per basic type, where for example all customer nonconformities are totaled. This can also be shown as a total per customer. At the bottom of the list you will see a total for the entire list.
