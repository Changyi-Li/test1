### List type Split invoice basis
In this list it it possible to split an invoice basis in two parts. This can be done if a quantity of units have been delivered, but the entire quantity should not be invoiced at the same time. For an invoice basis to be splittable, it must have status 3 – Pending.
The system setting Enable split invoice basis needs to be activated in order for the list to be available.
You split the invoice basis by entering a quantity on the row and then saving. The new invoice basis will then be shown below the first invoice basis. The new invoice basis will also receive status 3 – Pending.
- Invoice bases that contain negative invoice rows cannot be split.
- Only invoice bases with the invoice type Invoice can be split. However, credit invoices cannot be split.
- It is not possible to split invoice bases that have linked invoicing plans.
