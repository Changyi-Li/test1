### Energy sources
Here you enter information about emissions from production of the electricity that the company uses. Enter the types of electricity that your company purchases. You can enter it as a row for the electricity mix which your company is using (the electricity mix is often stated on the electricity bill) or as one row per type.
This information is used in the emission calculation by multiplying it with the power entered for the work center multiplied the time in the BOM and routing.

#### Name
Here you can write a descriptive text as a name. You enter name texts in the company language and they are displayed in the user’s language.

#### Emissions
Here you enter the carbon dioxide emissions in the unit kg CO2e/kW.

#### Percentage
Here you specify how large percentage the energy source accounts for.
