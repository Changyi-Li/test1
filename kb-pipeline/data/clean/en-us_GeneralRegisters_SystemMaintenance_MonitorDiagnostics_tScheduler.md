### The Scheduler tab
Here you see information about Start date, Available threads, and Executed jobs (how many).
By using the Update button ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_update.png) you update the information in the list.

#### Name
Here you see the name of the job that is run.

#### Start date
Here you see the date of when the scheduled job started.

#### Planned start date
Here you see the date when the job was planned to start.

#### Elapsed time
Here you see how much time has elapsed since the job started.
