### Settings

#### Invoice types
Here you decide which invoice types should be included in the list. You can choose from the following: debit invoices, interest invoices, credit invoices, on account invoices, and zero invoices.

#### Payment reminder date
The payment reminder date will be today's date by default, but it can be changed. It is the date that the report will be based on. For example, an unpaid invoice that is due on May 26th, 2015 will be included in the list if the reminder date is set to May 27th or later. This applies provided that the number of days of grace is zero.

#### Days of grace
This is the number of extra days after the due date of an invoice, which the customer has got to make the payment of the invoice before any reminder will be sent. The default value of days of grace is determined by the system setting Days of grace The term days of grace (or grace period) is used at requirements planning in order to calculate rescheduling of actual orders that cover the requirement but that are too late in time, instead of suggesting a new order. for payment reminders. The payment reminder date above takes into consideration the number of days of grace entered here. For example, if the due date is June 17th, 2015 and the days of grace are 7 days, then the invoice will be included in the reminder list if the due date is June 25th or later.
