### Measuring data log – Manufacturing
- In the Measuring data log procedure you can see both planned number of measuring items and reported measuring per operation for a manufacturing order.
- A detailed list is included and it displays reported measuring data on operation rows.
- A new list type called "Trend" with the purpose to show how a numerical measuring value has changed during the course of several reportings.
- The total list provides a total of planned and reported measuring items per operation on manufacturing order.
