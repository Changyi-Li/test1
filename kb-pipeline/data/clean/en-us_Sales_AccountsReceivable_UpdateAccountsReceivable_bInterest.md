### Interest
In this box you handle interest on accounts receivable records.

#### Interest charge
In this field you see if and how penalty interest should be charged in case of late payment of the invoice. The setting is loaded from the Customer register for a new accounts receivable record, but this can be changed also for existing accounts receivable records in the Interest charge basis procedure. The available options are: Interest invoice, Next regular invoice, and No charge.

#### Interest rate
Here you see the interest rate that applies to the invoice. For a new accounts receivable record, the setting is primarily loaded from the customer and secondarily from the system setting Penalty interest, general interest rate. The field is locked for existing accounts receivable records.
