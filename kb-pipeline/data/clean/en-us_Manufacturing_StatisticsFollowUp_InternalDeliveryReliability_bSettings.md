### Settings
Here you configure settings for calculating/measuring the internal delivery reliability.

#### Finish date
In this field you select which finish date to use when calculating/checking the internal delivery reliability: Planned or Initial.

#### Calculate delivery reliability per operation
Not for the "future" list types. Here you decide if the delivery reliability should be calculated per operation, that is, if it should only show one row per operation (the final/last reporting item) even though there are many reporting items made.

#### Include operations with remaining quantity
Not for the "future" list types. With this setting you decide if operations that have a remaining quantity should be included in the calculation of internal delivery reliability. Operations that are delayed and have not yet been reported as finished, will then be given a fictitious log record displaying the delay in number of days if the operation should finish today.

#### Calendar type
All calendar days, National calendar, or Selected calendar. If you choose to use the national calendar it means that Saturday, Sunday and other holidays will be excluded. By using the All calendar days option, both weekends and holidays are included. If you plan, e.g., to deliver on Friday but deliver the following Monday instead, the delivery will be considered one day late using the National calendar. Using the option All calendar days, the delivery will instead be considered three days late.

#### Calendar
This setting is available when you chose the Selected calendar option. A selected calendar can be a customized calendar (own) which you have created to add holiday/vacation periods, bridging days, or other deviations from the national calendar.

#### Allowance before/Allowance after
Here you can enter an allowance, that is, the number of days before and after the planned finish date which you allow the operation to finish and still consider it to be on time. The allowance in number of days can be enter for before and/or after the entered planned finish date. Depending on what calendar you choose, this will be interpreted as work days or calendar days.
