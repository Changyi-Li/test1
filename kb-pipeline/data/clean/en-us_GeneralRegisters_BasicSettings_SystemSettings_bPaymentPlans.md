### Payment plans

#### Handle payment plans on purchase orders
Here you activate functionality for payment plans. A specific tab for payment plans becomes activated in the Register purchase order procedure. Other functionality for this will also be activated. This system setting is deactivated by default.

#### Handle suspended VAT during payment of advance invoice
Here you determine how suspended VAT should be handled during payments of advance invoices. This setting is activated by default. The system setting used for posting suspended VAT when invoicing advances is determined via exception per product group. If you activate this system setting, the VAT amount will be moved from the account for suspended VAT to the correct account for input VAT.
