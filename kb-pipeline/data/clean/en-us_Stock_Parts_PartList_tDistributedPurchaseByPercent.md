### The list Distributed purchase by percentage
In this list you can, for each part where distributed purchase by percentage is applied, when needed update the percentages. The Distribution (%) column is the only thing that can be updated for existing distributed purchases.
It it not possible to add or delete supplier links from the distribution in this list.
The total of the distribution (%) of the rows for supplier links must be 100%. You cannot save the list if the total of the distribution is not 100%.
