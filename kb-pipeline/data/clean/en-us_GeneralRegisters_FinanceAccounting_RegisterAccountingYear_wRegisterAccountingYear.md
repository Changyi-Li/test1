## Register accounting year
When starting up a new company, you register accounting years in this procedure. The system will then create new accounting years automatically when switching year in the Change period/accounting year procedure. However, in some cases it is necessary to use this procedure to register a future accounting year in advance, for example when budgets and accruals should be registered for future years. When you create a future year, all chart of accounts settings are copied from the current year. Any modifications made for the current year can be copied to the future year by using the button ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_copy_to.png) in the Accounting year box.
You can add a previous accounting year that will end up at the top of the list. You can also create a future accounting year that ends up at the bottom of the list. You cannot add an accounting year in the middle of the list. You can also delete added accounting years provided that there no recording has been made in the year in question and that a previous year exists. The oldest year must first be deleted to avoid gaps in the accounting years.

#### Clear existing chart of accounts
By using the button ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_delete_sort.png), you can clear the existing chart of accounts. When clearing other years than the current year, all accounts without any OB/balances/transactions will be cleared. Except if they are used as a system account, for example Standard account. When clearing the chart of accounts in the current year it means all accounts without any OB/balances/transactions will be cleared, and you can choose if system accounts should also be cleared (and be replaced by a temporary system account) or if they should be kept. These system accounts will by default be kept. The temporary system account must be registered in the Chart of accounts. Standard accounts refer to accounts in the procedures Standard accounts, VAT settings, Bank settings, Posting matrix, etc.
