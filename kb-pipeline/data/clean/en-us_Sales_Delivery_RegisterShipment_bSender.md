### Sender
Information regarding sender of the shipment is loaded from the company information, the user or the employee, if such has been registered.

#### Our reference, Phone, Mobile phone, and E-mail
The "our reference person" and his/her phone, mobile phone and e-mail, are loaded according to the Load to our reference on shipment system setting.

#### Customer number, shipping agent
Customer number, shipping agent is the goods address number or customer number that the freight payer has got at the shipping agent’s. This is loaded from customer number at shipping agent in the Company information.

#### Pallet registration number
Here you see/enter the pallet registration number that is printed on your waybills for a delivery. This is loaded from the Company information.

#### Reference
The sender's reference on the shipment is loaded according to the Load to the sender's reference on shipment system setting.
