### Synchronize system
If you have purchased user licenses or options for your Monitor ERP system, you need to synchronize with Monitor's distribution server to activate the new licenses or options. This synchronization takes place automatically once per day (at night), but you can also manually start a synchronization by following the steps below:
1. Log in to Monitor ERP with the user who has the role of ERP manager.
2. Open the desktop's backstage and click on About.
3. Click the button Synchronize at the top of the page.
The synchronization may take a few seconds. Above the button you see the date and time for the most recent synchronization.
