### Comment/Files
In this box you can add comments and link documents.

#### Customer/Supplier comment
Here you see comments entered for the customer in the customer register or comments entered for the supplier in the supplier register. The comment is not printed on any documents. The files you link here can only be displayed. They cannot be printed together with the case documents.

#### Internal comment
Here you can enter an internal comment regarding the case. It can be shown on the internal nonconformity report via settings for the document. The text is also visible e.g. in the Case list procedure. The files you link here cannot be printed automatically.

#### External comment
Here you can enter an external comment for the case. It can be shown on the case documents via case form settings. The text is also visible e.g. in the Case list procedure. The files you link here are possible to choose for automatic printout.
