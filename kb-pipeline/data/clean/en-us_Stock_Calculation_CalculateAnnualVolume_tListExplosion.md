### The Explosion list
This list type loads saved annual volume and runs a calculation with structure explosion in order to calculate the annual volumes for the included/incorporated parts.
After loading the list you find yourself in a mode where you can modify quantity and order quantity, and also select which rows to include in the calculation. If the row has a quantity, you can choose to include in the calculation.
The Last reset of annual volume and Last update of annual volume columns enable you to sort, select, and to exclude parts from previous resets and calculations.
In a second step you start calculation using the Start calculation button ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_run.png) (Ctrl + R) on the toolbar of the procedure. A structure explosion is then made and calculates the resulting annual volumes for the included/incorporated parts.
After the calculation has been made, the tab Annual volume opens. There you can see the selected parts. Here you can modify New quantity. The column Save is selected by default and the new quantity will be saved for the part.
Under the Warnings tab you see warnings, if any, that appear after the calculation run.

#### Quantity
In this column you see the included/incorporated quantity which you can edit.

#### Order quantity
The order quantity is displayed in red and italics if it has inherited the quantity from the Selection tab, due to not having a saved value.

#### Include
In this column you determine if the row should be included in the calculation.
