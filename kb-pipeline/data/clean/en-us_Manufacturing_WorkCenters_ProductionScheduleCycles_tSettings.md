### Settings

#### Schedule number
Here you see/select the schedule number that you want to include in the cycle.

#### Name
Here you see the name of the schedule. You cannot modify the name in this field.

#### Number of days
Here you enter the number of days for which the entered schedule will apply. At the bottom of the box you will see the total number of days. The total number of days must be equal to an even number of weeks, that is, a multiple of 7. A cycle can e.g. have a schedule row with 5 work days and another row with 2 days off for the weekend.

#### Schedule chart
The button in the S column ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_more_info.png) will open a chart of the schedule.
