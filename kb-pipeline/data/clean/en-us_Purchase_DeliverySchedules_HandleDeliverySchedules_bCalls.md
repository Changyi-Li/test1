### Calls
In this box you find a list of all calls in the marked delivery schedule or for the marked part in the delivery schedule.
The list contains all necessary information about each call. This information is loaded partly from previous delivery schedules, partly from purchase order rows and from supplier, and this is what makes it possible to see resulting action for each call based on the new delivery schedule.
For subcontracts, you can see the quantity shipped to the subcontractor and the quantity received from the subcontractor. In addition, the manufacturing order number is displayed.
For blanket orders you see the remaining quantity.
The calls in the list are sorted by part per delivery date. If the delivery date is in past time, it is shown with a pale red background. Delivery dates as of today's date are shown with a light green background. Calls with a delivery date within the part's lead time are shown with a pale yellow background. These colors for the delivery dates are the same as in the planning window in the Part register.
