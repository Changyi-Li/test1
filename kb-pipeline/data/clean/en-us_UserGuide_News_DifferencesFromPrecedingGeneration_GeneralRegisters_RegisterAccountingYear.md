### Register accounting year
- New layout which is easier to grasp where the accounting years are shown and registered in a table.
- It is possible to name the accounting year to other than the year. In G4 the numbering of the year was a problem when the year had been split.
- It is possible to insert old accounting years in arrears. In G4 it was only possible to create next accounting year.
- For each accounting year it is possible to lock opening balance from adjustments.
- There is a function used to clear the chart of accounts in the selected accounting year.
