### Average of total stock turnover ratio by ABC
This box contains a bar chart which shows the average of the total stock turnover ratio per ABCABC codes are used to classify the range of parts by the volumes you sell. The codes are used as a scale for the parts that turn over the most money. The turnover is calculated by multiplying the price of the part by the annual volume. Parts that turn over the most money are called "A-parts", and after that, "B-parts", etc. Code for the selected parts. The green bar shows the calculated turnover ratio and the blue bar shows the actual turnover ratio.
