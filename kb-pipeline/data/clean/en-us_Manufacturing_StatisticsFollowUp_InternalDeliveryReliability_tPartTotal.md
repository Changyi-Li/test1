### The list type Part – Total
This list type shows total information about the internal delivery reliability, grouped by part. The calculation will then only be based on the delivery reliability of the part’s last/final operation. The list can also be presented as a chart in the lower part of the window.

#### Calculated columns:
Total quantity = On time (count) + Late (count) + Early (count)
Early (%) = (Early (count) / Total count) x 100
On time (%) = (On time (count) / Total count) x 100
Late (%) = (Late (count) / Total count) x 100
