### Settings
Settings for export of salary basis.

#### Date interval
Here you select the date interval for when salary basis should be exported. Please note! If a person’s employment ends within the date interval, no dates after the end date of the employment will be included in the basis.

#### Export setting
With this setting you select an export setting for the salary basis. At least one export setting must be registered in the Basic dataWith "basic data" we refer to the static records in a database, for example parts, customers, users, work centers, etc. – Attendance procedure, otherwise there is nothing available to select in this field.

#### Show blocked
With this setting you decide whether or not blocked persons should be shown in the list.

#### Load all non-blocked employees
This setting is only used for export of salary basis for the payroll management system Mepco. When exporting to Mepco, you have an option to export planned absence. Since planned absence is absence that will take place in the future, there are no recording items for the selected date interval. If you activate this setting, all employees (except those that are blocked) and their planned absences will be included in the export file. Planned absence is not exported to any other payroll system.

#### Lock records for adjustment
By default the recording records in the selected date interval will become locked and cannot be adjusted after the export. But you can, if needed, deactivate this setting. The lock means the recording items no longer can be adjusted under the tab Adjust attendance in the Authorize/Adjust recording procedure. However, they can be unlocked in arrears in the procedure Lock/unlock attendance recording items.
