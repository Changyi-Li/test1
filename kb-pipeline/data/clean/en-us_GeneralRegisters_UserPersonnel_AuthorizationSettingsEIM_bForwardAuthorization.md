### Forward authorization
In this box you enter the prerequisites for the authorization flow to run smoothly and to avoid that the invoice gets stuck at a signer. You can activate one control or both. You activate the function under the Scheduling tab. There you also select if the forwarding shall be made based on a specific time interval or if it shall be scheduled.

#### Activate check of due date
Here you determine the number of days before the due date of the invoice until the invoice will be forwarded to another signer.

#### Activate check of long waiting time
Here you determine the number of days that the invoice will stay at the signer before it will be forwarded to another signer.
