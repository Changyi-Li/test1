### The List tab
Under this tab the list is totaled by the selected list type. The list Total by order shows information based on the columns you selected under the Selection tab. At the bottom of the list you find a total of the rows (including a total of small items which is not displayed).
Columns considered as self-instructional are not described here.

#### Order number
In this column you see the manufacturing order number of the orders selected to be included in the WIP value.

#### Warnings (W)
If there are warnings after the calculation, a button appears in this column which you can use to go to the warnings list. The warnings you have not clicked on are displayed with a warning sign ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/warning.png) on the button. The warnings that you have clicked are displayed with an exclamation mark ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/warning_validation.png).

#### Status
In this column you can see a symbol representing the status of the order. The status can be 3 (In progress), 4 (Finished), or 5 (Post-calculated), depending on the order statuses selected to be included.

#### Part number
In this column you see the part number of the main part of the order. If there are several main parts, they will be displayed as several rows with the same order number.

#### Total
Here you can see the total of material + subcontract + processing.

#### Transfer to stock
In this column you see the value of already reported quantity with transfer to stock on the last operation. Transfer to stock is calculated as Finished quantity x Price alternative for transfer to stock.

#### Net value
Here you can see the result of Total - Transfer to stock.
The net value is the WIP value of the order, according to the settings configured under the Selection tab.

#### Last reporting date
Here you can see the date when reporting most recently was made.

#### Planned cost
The Planned cost on the order shows the maximum WIP value if everything is going according to plan.

#### Purchase cost
The Purchase cost of the order is shown here (calculated as Standard price × Planned quantity of the main part). It is the value of the future transfer to stock.
