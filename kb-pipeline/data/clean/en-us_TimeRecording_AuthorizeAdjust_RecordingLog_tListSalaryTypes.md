### The Salary types list (and Salary types – Total)
These list types display the recording items per salary type and person. The lists are grouped by person.
The detailed list shows date, salary types and their names, quantity and action.
The total list type shows quantity totaled by salary type. At the bottom of the list you see a total per salary type for all persons in the list.
The salary types' quantities can be generated by recording items of the day (in hours), entire days, additions (allowance for expenses etc.), and from calculated shorter working hours.
