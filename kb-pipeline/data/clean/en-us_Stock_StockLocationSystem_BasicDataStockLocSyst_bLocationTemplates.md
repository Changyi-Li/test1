### Location templates
In the Location templates box you can create templates to simplify the work of deciding the size of the locations, and enter a maximum weight for the locations.
It is possible to enter both minimum and maximum measurements. The minimum measurement is used to determine, e.g., whether or not half-pallets can be used. (If there is an extra beam in the shelf where half pallets would fit since they are half length).
The templates can later be used in the Locations – Advanced list type in the Location list procedure to easily define the size and weight which will fit in the location.
