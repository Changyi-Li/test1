### BI – Sales
- Essentially the same functionality as in the G4 BI but with the difference that the BI (business intelligence) in G5 is an incorporated part of the program. Settings are configured in the new procedure Settings for BI) regarding when data mining should be performed. In G4 this was configured externally.
- The filtering in G5 is done in every direction in the boxes, that is, if you mark a part you see information about this part in the other boxes, for example Customers and Countries (the map).
- The included views are Sales statistics and Order inflow, but new views will be added by and by. You can create own views in the backstage of the procedure.
