### Correction (existing manufacturing order)
Here you can link one or several operations to the case if you are to correct a problem on an existing manufacturing order.
By clicking the Add new row at the end (F5) button ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_add_row.png), a dialog opens where you can enter a manufacturing order number and then you see the order structure including operations. Once you have selected operations in the dialog you will here see them as rows. For each row you can select if material cost, subcontracting cost, and processing cost, should be charged on the operation. If none of the CM The contribution margin (CM) is the difference between the standard price and the sales price., CS, and CP checkboxes are marked on the row, nothing will be charged for the correction.
