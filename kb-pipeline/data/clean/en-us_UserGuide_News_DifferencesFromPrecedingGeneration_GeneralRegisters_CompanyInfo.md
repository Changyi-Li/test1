### Company information
- Company name is one field (one row) instead of two. This field is extended to allow many characters in company names.
- Time zone on warehouses to provide better system support for Monitor systems run in multiple countries with different time zones.
- Telex does not exist in G5.
- The bank account information and giro numbers have been extracted to a separate bank register. It is possible to enter which bank account should be default in the company information.
- It is possible to enter VAT number per warehouse. This provides better system support for companies which are registered for VAT and invoices in different countries (warehouses).
- The References tab from G4 has be moved to a separate procedure called Personnel records.
