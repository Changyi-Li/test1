### Profit mark-up
Under this heading, you can enter the general profit mark-up in percent that will apply in the calculation. The default general Profit mark-upThe profit mark-up is a percentage mark-up on the cost price which will generate a suggested quote price. in percent, and different exceptions from this, if any, must first be registered in the Calculation mark-up procedure.
By using the setting Calculate profit mark-up for you can determine if Material, Subcontract, and/or Manufacturing should be excluded from the Profit mark-upThe profit mark-up is a percentage mark-up on the cost price which will generate a suggested quote price.. This means that these costs will not be included in the cost price before you calculate the profit mark-up.

#### General profit mark-up
Here you enter the general profit mark-up in percent. For manufactured parts you can also enter an exception. It is always the part's profit mark-up that is used, but you can here change the percentage of the mark-up.
