### Schedule setup
You can create a schedule setup for the schedule you have selected.

#### Breaks
Here you enter the break periods for the schedule in question. If you register breaks here, you must enter a start time and an end time within the schedule limits in order to be able to save.

#### Schedule chart
Here you can see a chart of the schedule on a time line. The schedule limits and the breaks are clearly displayed. At the bottom of the box you will see the total of the available time in the schedule. It is the total of the time within the schedule limits minus the breaks.
