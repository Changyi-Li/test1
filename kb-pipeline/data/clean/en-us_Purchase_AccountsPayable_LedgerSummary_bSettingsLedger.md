### Settings – Ledger

#### Ledger date
Here you select the date for which the ledger should be printed. Today's date is entered by default in this field, but it can be changed. You can, e.g., enter a date in past time to reconcile the general ledger at a given time.
The setting will affect invoices issued in foreign currency. It determines that the remaining amount on the invoices will be based on the selected rate type and not the company currency.

#### Include
With this setting you determine the types of invoices that will be included in the list: debit invoices, credit invoices, zero invoices, and canceled. You can make different selections for customer invoices and supplier invoices.

#### Include fully paid – Accounts receivable/Accounts payable
With this setting you will also include fully paid invoices. You can choose if you want to see fully paid customer invoices, supplier invoices, or both. Invoices with a remaining amount of zero will also be shown.
