### Rows
Under the Rows tab you can add rows for the parts that should be sent using this stock order for sales and information regarding them.
The information on the stock order rows is a part of the information available on the [customer order rows](../RegisterCustomerOrder/tRows.htm). The information which only exists on stock order rows for sales, are described below.
When using traceability for the part, no serial number is created when a stock order row for sales is registered, regardless what is configured on the part.

#### Arrival reported quantity
Under the button More info ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_more_info.png) to the far right on the stock order row, you find this field which shows how many parts have been arrival reported on the corresponding row in the linked stock order for purchase.
