### The Standard list
The data that is loaded to the standard list depends on which presentation you have selected. You can edit most columns except for the two opening columns. They show which customer that each row concerns.
Terms/Prices
In this list you can see and change price settings for the customer and the terms for payments, delivery, delivery method, validity period. The available options are handled in the Terms procedure. You can also change price list, configurator price list (if the option Product configurator is used), and discount category. These alternatives are handled in their respective procedures in General registers. Discount, alloy cost, and invoicing plans, are also handled in the list.
The list corresponds to the boxes Terms and Prices in the Customer register.
Export/Printouts
In this list you can see and change settings regarding export to the customer and settings for how orders and invoices to the customer should be printed. You can for example configure how order printouts and invoice printouts should be performed by default: sent via e-mail or to printer. You can also change the language, currency, and time zone.
The list corresponds to the boxes Export and Printouts in the Customer register.
XML/Documents
In this list you can see and modify the settings made for XML files and customer-specific documents. For example, settings regarding attachment of customer order, invoice, or case as XML files. You can also change the document variant to use for, e.g., quote, order confirmation, customer agreement, delivery note, transport label, and pro forma invoice.
The list corresponds to the settings configured in the XML/Documents box in the Customer register procedure.
Delivery/Shipping
In this list you can see and change settings configured regarding delivery and shipping to the customer. For example, settings regarding delivery rules, delivery days, transport time, calendar, transport distance, delivery instructions, pick instructions, and cumulative quantity from start date.
The list corresponds to the Delivery/Shipping box in the Customer register.
Exceptions
In this list you can see and change exceptions made from general settings for the selected customers. For example exceptions can be made regarding interest charge and penalty interest. The list corresponds to the Exceptions box in the Customer register.
CRM
In this list you can see and change settings made regarding CRM for the selected customer. You can for example see who is the seller, responsible for customer, the customer’s status, and number of employees.
The list corresponds to the Settings box in the Customer register.
Miscellaneous
In this list you can see and change miscellaneous settings made for the customer.
The list corresponds to the settings configured in the following boxes: XML/Documents, Invoicing, and Miscellaneous in the Customer register procedure.
