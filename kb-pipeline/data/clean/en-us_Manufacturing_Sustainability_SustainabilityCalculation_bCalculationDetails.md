### Calculation details
Here, the calculation for each selected part is shown as a tree structure. The emission values in the tree are shown per unit.
Operations are shown under the red expandable row.
Materials are shown under the yellow expandable row.
Under the green expandable row, Sub-nodes are shown. Order driven manufactured parts are shown with information divided into operations, materials, and sub-nodes.
All details are aggregated in the tree so that it is possible to see details, sub-totals, and total amounts.
