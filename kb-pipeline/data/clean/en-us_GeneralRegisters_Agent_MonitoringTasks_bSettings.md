### Settings
In this box you configure settings for the monitoring task.

#### History
By clicking the button Historical ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_more_info.png) you can see a log of when the monitoring term in the task has been fulfilled. You also see the result.

#### Comment
By clicking the button Comment ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_no_comment.png) you can enter a comment to the monitoring task, for example a description. When a comment exists, a filled speech bubble ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_comment.png) is shown on the button.
