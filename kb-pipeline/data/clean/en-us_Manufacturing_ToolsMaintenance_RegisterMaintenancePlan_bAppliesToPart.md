### Applies to part
Here you add one or multiple parts (tools and machines) to which this maintenance plan applies.

#### Part number
Here you see/select that part number for the part that should be linked to the maintenance plan. Once you have selected a part number, you will in the T column see a symbol illustrating the part template selected for the part. When you hover over this symbol you will see the basic type of the part template.
> If many parts should be entered you can paste a selection from Clipboard.
