### Settings

#### Calendar exceptions
The checkbox is available for the list types Desired lead time, Initial lead time, Planned lead time, Actual lead time, and Total.
If the checkbox is not checked, any exceptions entered in the supplier’s calendar will not be taken into consideration. If calendar exceptions is checked, any exceptions entered in the supplier’s calendar will be taken into consideration and deducted when calculating lead times.

#### Calendar
Standard calendar or all calendar days The dates in the selected calendar will be included in the calculation. In the standard calendar. weekends and other holidays are not included. In the option All calendar days, weekends and holidays are included. The standard calendar is selected by default.

#### Part's lead time
Here you decide if the part's lead time should be shown in the list. When the box is checked, the column Part’s lead time is added to the list. The checkbox is available for the list types Desired lead time, Initial lead time, Planned lead time, and Actual lead time. Columns where the list’s calculated lead time is compared to the part’s lead time are also added. You can read more about these columns in the sections about the respective list types.
