### Order registration and printout
> Applies in versions up to and including 25.1.
Subcontract purchase orders can be printed for the manufacturing orders registered. You can either open and print the purchase order by clicking on the link on the operation row of the manufacturing order, or use one of the following procedures:
Print purchase order
This procedure can be used if one order per operation is required. Only purchase orders and delivery notes can be printed. It is not possible to report shipment.
Subcontract documents/Shipped
Purchase orders and delivery notes can also be printed by operation in this procedure. So-called comprehensive documents – containing several purchase orders for the same supplier in the same document – (see example below) can also be printed. The comprehensive document is then given a unique number comprising the various purchase orders included. Shipments can also be reported in this procedure. You can print the order and delivery note with the shipped quantity rather than the planned quantity. Another version of the document is then selected. When printing purchase orders, the supplier and order date are normally selected.
Priority planning
In the procedure, subcontracts can also be loaded into a list. From this list, it is possible to link to material clearance.
Order list – Purchase
In this procedure, all subcontract purchases are loaded into two separate lists: Subcontract and Shipped subcontract, which show shipped subcontract purchase orders that have not been reported.
