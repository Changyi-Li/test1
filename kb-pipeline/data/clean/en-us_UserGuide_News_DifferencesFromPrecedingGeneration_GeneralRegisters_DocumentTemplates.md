### Document templates
- All business documents are a part of the new document concept which means that you yourself can make changes in the layout and you do not have to generate the document after the changes.
- The system provides all basic types of documents and for these you can then create your own variants and edit with a built-in tool called DevExpress Report Designer.
- There are quite a few new settings in the Document settings procedure. These are used to decide what to be shown on the documents.
