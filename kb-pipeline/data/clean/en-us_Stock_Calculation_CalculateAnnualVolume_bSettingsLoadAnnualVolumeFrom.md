### Settings – Load annual volume from
In the Load annual volume from­ table you enter where the calculation data for annual volume should be loaded from. You can mix data from different registers in the same loading of calculation data. You can load annual volume from the following registers:
- Consumption statistics
- Customer order stock
- Sales forecasts – loaded from sales forecasts that have been registered in the Register sales forecasts procedure.
- Sales forecast – Customer – loaded from customer order rows with Type of requirement configured to Forecast.
- Annual volume
- Annual budget
You mark Include and enter the From and To dates for the interval when data should be loaded, and which Factor should be used for upward adjustment. It is not possible to enter date intervals for annual volume and annual budget.
The rows for current pace is only available if you have activated the system setting Show annual budget, annual volume, and order quantity with current pace.
If you choose not to include any registers, a list for manual change of date will be loaded. The same value will then be displayed in both the Old quantity and New quantity columns in the list.

#### Order backlog quantity according to
If you in the table above have selected to include the Customer order backlog, this setting becomes available. This determines how the order backlog should be displayed: according to Remaining quantity or according to Ordered quantity.

#### Budget from year
If you in the table above have selected to include the Annual budget or Annual budget, current pace, this setting becomes available. This determines from which year the budget should be loaded.

#### Total from all warehouses
With this setting you determine whether or not all available warehouses registered on the company, should be total in the list.
