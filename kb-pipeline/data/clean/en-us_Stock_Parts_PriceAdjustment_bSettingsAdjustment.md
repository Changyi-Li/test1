### Settings – Adjustment

#### Adjust with
In this field you select how the price should be adjusted: by percent or amount. The default option is percent. A value field is shown to the right where you can enter a percentage or an amount. If this field is left with the default 0.00, no adjustment will be made and the price in the list has to be manually adjusted part by part.
The formula for copying new price will be: price to copy from × adjust with × exchange rate.

#### Number of decimals in new price
Enter how many decimals should be used when saving the new price. The default option here is 2 decimals. It is possible to use up to 8 decimals.
You can also enter negative decimals to round-off the new price to the nearest 10 or higher. For example, -1 will round-off to the nearest ten, -2 will round-off to the nearest hundred, etc.

#### Number of significant digits in new price
Enter the number of significant digits the different whole numbers in new prices will be rounded to. This field is empty by default, meaning no round-off of integers will take place. For example, one significant digit will round-off the integer 1,453 to 1,000. Two significant digits will result in the rounding of 1,453 to 1,500, etc.

#### Only include parts with staggered prices
Applies to the list types: Change staggered price – Price list/Customer/Supplier. With this checkbox you determine if the list only should contain parts with staggered prices. If this checkbox is not checked, all parts included by the selection will be loaded to the list. The purpose of unchecking this box is that you can create staggering prices for parts that have not previously had staggered prices.
