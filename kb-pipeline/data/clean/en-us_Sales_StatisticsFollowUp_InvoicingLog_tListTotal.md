### Total
This list shows total information from both the invoice header and the invoice rows, as well as CM The contribution margin (CM) is the difference between the standard price and the sales price. and CR The contribution ratio (CR) is the portion of the invoice amount (sales price) that the contribution margin represents. CR is entered as a percentage., by the selected grouping term. On invoice row level you can see the same information as in the [detailed list](tListDetailed.htm). The list can also display values in chart form. For the Grouped by invoice presentation you can also preview the invoice.
