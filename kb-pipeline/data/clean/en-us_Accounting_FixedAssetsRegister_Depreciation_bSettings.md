### Settings

#### Depreciation method
(Only list type Scheduled depreciation.) Here you can choose the method which you’ll use to calculate the depreciation.
The following methods are available:
- Straight-line
- Declining balance
- Declining balance + Straight-line.

#### Depreciation date
Here you can enter the depreciation date. The default date is the last day in the current accounting period. What you enter in this field will be used in the next field.

#### Voucher date
Here you can enter the date when the depreciation should be recorded. Dates that fall within locked periods are not allowed.

#### Voucher text
With this setting you can select the voucher text. These texts are handled in the Voucher texts procedure.

#### Automatic posting active
With this setting you determine if automatic posting/allocation should be generated in the postings created during depreciation (both scheduled and calculated).
> A warning will appear if the fixed assets journal is not linked to a voucher number series. This is handled in the Voucher number series/Journals procedure.
