### The Warning list tab
Here you will see the parts that have received warnings during the calculation and information about the cause of the warnings.
The warning list mainly manages errors from the BOM and routing, such as if no cost factor has been configured, or if the setup time/unit time is missing. If you have entered a date in the field Oldest price in the selection, warnings can be shown for material prices that are outdated and that have not been revised for a long time.
