### Arrival and registration of delivery invoices
Arrival is reported in the usual way.
You arrival report the order as usual, but when the invoice basis is created (when saving), extra rows (services) are inserted. These will deduct the partial invoices in accordance with the payment plan.
Deduction of advance/in arrears invoices does not take place in proportion based on the value reported for arrival (this differs from when delivery reporting in the Sales module). Instead, the full deduction takes place when the order is first arrival reported. This occurs because the system has difficulty knowing how the supplier will invoice the delivery. When the supplier invoices is linked, you can instead allocate to the row/rows for deduction, and enter the amount the supplier has deducted. The remaining portion ends up on a separate invoice basis, which can be linked to the next invoice.
-   
When it comes to orders in foreign currencies, the system has an in-built function whereby rows for deduction are valued at the same exchange rate as the advance invoice. The difference between the rate on the supplier invoice and the rate on the advance invoice is automatically recorded on the standard account for rate differences.
-   
Rows added at the arrival reporting are not considered to be included in the invoicing plan, and therefore no deduction is made for these rows.
-   
The system will never deduct more than the value of the payment in advance/in arrears, for example, if you arrival report a larger quantity that what was ordered.
-   
When deleting the remaining quantity when reporting arrival, deduction will take place based on the arrival reported value. No deduction will be made for the deleted value.
-   
When it comes to orders in foreign currencies, the system has an in-built function whereby rows for deduction are valued at the same exchange rate as the advance invoice. The difference between the rate on the supplier invoice and the rate on the advance invoice is automatically recorded on the standard account for rate differences.
> If the deduction row needs to be adjusted you should leave 1 as quantity, but instead change the price each or the total price according to the invoice’s deduction.
