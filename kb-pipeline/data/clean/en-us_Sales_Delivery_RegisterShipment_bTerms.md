### Terms

#### Delivery terms
The delivery term is loaded from the source of information. You can change it for the specific shipment.
Next to the field you see the code for the delivery term. Code for the delivery term is entered in the Terms procedure.

#### Freight payer
The default freight payer is determined by the selected Delivery term. You can change it for the specific shipment.
- Purchaser pays – With this alternative, the paying customer number will be loaded from Customer no., shipping agents for the customer on the order (the recipient). By clicking the ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_more_info.png) button next to the field, you can enter a Payment reference. Some freight companies demand a payment reference for this freight payer. In cases where the customer's alternative delivery address is used for the order and this address does not have Customer no., shipping agent, the Customer no., shipping agent from the customer will be used as fallback.
- Seller pays – With this alternative, the paying customer number will be loaded from Customer no., shipping agents in the Company information (sender).
- Other payer – With this alternative, it means you can enter a paying customer number in the field below.
- Incoterms – With this alternative, the freight payer is determined according to Incoterms. Incoterms are internationally recognized rules which describe who has the financial responsibility of the goods during the transport.

#### Freight payer address
Here you see the address of the freight payer. This address is loaded based on the selected freight payer. The address is possible to edit.

#### Paying customer number
If Freight payer is set to Other payer, you can here enter the customer number of the paying customer.

#### Place of terms of delivery
In this field you see the place of terms of delivery. This value is loaded from the source of information. The place of terms of delivery indicates the place or city where the financial responsibility for the shipped goods will pass to another party.
