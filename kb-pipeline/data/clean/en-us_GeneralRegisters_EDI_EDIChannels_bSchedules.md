### Schedules
In this table you create schedules for EDI EDI is the acronym of Electronic Data Interchange. EDI is about exchanging electronic business documents with your business partners, e.g. customers and suppliers. The EDI concept can be wide and a bit unclear, and can many times be used about all types of documents which are sent electronically, even if it might be PDF files sent via e-mail or publishing business documents on a website. What we refer to as EDI – and what is traditionally meant by EDI – is structured business documents following given standards, electronically sent or received and which are compiled and interpreted automatically and that is integrated with the customer's/supplier's ERP system. channels. A schedule determines when the channel should be run.
By using the buttons in the function menu you can add, insert, delete, and copy schedules row by row. Each schedule is configured in the Schedule box.
If you delete a schedule that is used for a channel, you must confirm the deletion in a dialog window.

#### Name
Here you enter an optional name of the schedule.

#### Active
You activate a schedule by checking the box Active.
> Please note! Do not create any active schedules that you do not use, since they might cause high server load.

#### Log
By using the button Log ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_more_info.png) you find a changelog for the schedule. Here you see who modified the information as well as when. You also see what has been changed, with the value before and after the change.

#### Used in channel
An active schedule can then be linked to an EDI channel under the Channels tab. This will then determine when the channel will be run. By using the button Used in channel ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_more_info.png) you can see which to which EDI channels the schedule is linked. You can also see if the channels are active.
