### Countries
- The register containing countries has more columns and they determine default values when new customers and suppliers are created. For example, default customer group, supplier group, currency, and language.
- The setting EU country, which in G4 is directly on customer and supplier, is in G5 only available for the country.
- The country you select determines the address format used.
- It is possible to deactivate countries where you do not have business. This way you can minimize the number of selectable countries for customer, supplier, and order.
