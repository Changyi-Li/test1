### Delivered rows
In this box you see a list of delivery reporting items made for customer order rows. If the row which you want to undo is included in a total row (row type 3), then you will also see the total row in this box.
In the list you see the order row's position, part number, name, delivered quantity, reported by (user and person), actual delivery date, and also the log date.

#### Undo
Mark this checkbox to undo delivery reporting of the order row.
