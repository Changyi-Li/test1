### Recording/Printouts
Under this tab you create default settings for the work center's printouts of manufacturing order documents.
If you have installed the option Work recording you can also configure default settings for own work centers' work recording in the Recording terminal. These settings can also be configured individually for each operation in the Priority planning procedure. The settings under this tab do not apply to Subcontract.
Under this tab there are three boxes:
- Work recording
- Printouts
- Printouts in Recording terminal
