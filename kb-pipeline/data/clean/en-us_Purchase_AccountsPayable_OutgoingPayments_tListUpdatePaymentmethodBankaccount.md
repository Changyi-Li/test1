### List type Update payment method/bank account
In this list, you can update payment method and/or bank account (recipient account) on unpaid supplier invoices. By default, the list shows invoices with the statuses Registered, Preliminary recorded, and Final recorded.
By checking Payment method and/or Recipient account under Settings, you can synchronize the data with the supplier register. This way you can quickly and easily update all unpaid invoices with new data, which can be useful if you have, for example, made changes in the supplier register which should also apply to already registered invoices.
You can also manually update this data in the list by loading the list and selecting an option in the dropdown menu under the columns New payment method or New recipient account. The options that are available to select in the dropdown menu are the data that are registered in the supplier register.
To save the new data, check the checkbox in the Include column and then save by using the save button ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_save.png) on the toolbar.

#### Include
Determines which invoices will be updated with a new payment method/recipient account.

#### Invoice type
Shows if the invoice type is Debit, Credit, or On account On account is a partial payment (advance payment) which you have made to a supplier or received from a customer.. On account and credit invoices are shown in red.

#### Status
Shows if the invoice’s status is Registered, Preliminary recorded, or Final recorded.

#### Payment method
Shows which payment method is registered for the invoice (current payment method).

#### New payment method
Shows the payment method that will be registered for the invoice after you’ve saved your changes.

#### Recipient account
Shows which recipient account is registered for the invoice (current recipient account).

#### New recipient account
Shows the recipient account that will be registered for the invoice after you’ve saved your changes.
