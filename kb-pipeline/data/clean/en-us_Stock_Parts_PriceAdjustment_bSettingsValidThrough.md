### Settings – Valid through

#### Copy valid through date
Applies to the Copy price list type. Here you determine if the price you copy should also include the valid through date which applies to the price list you copy from. This setting can only be activated if both prices handle it. If you activate this setting, the field called New valid through becomes unavailable.

#### Include parts with valid through date to
Parts with a valid through date up to and including the date you enter here will be loaded to the list. This field is empty by default, and does not affect the loading of parts. This setting and the setting below, New valid through, are available if the fields Copy from price and Copy to price has been set to prices which handle valid through dates. These prices are supplier price, future supplier price, customer price, future customer price, price lists, and future price lists.

#### New valid through date
Here you can enter a new date for the valid through date. This field is empty by default, but you can select any date if the price you are copying manages this.
