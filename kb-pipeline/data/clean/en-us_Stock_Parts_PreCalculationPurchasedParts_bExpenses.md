### Include
Here you determine what should be included in the calculation of total cost for purchased parts. The alternatives available here are setup, staggered price, and discount, as well as the expenses which have been set as active under the Expenses tab in the Calculation mark-up procedure.
> You must first enter allocations (percentages) for Purchase costs such as, e.g., freight and packaging. This is done in the Calculations box in the Part register procedure before you can perform the actual calculation.

#### Setup
The setup price in price list or in default supplier link.

#### Staggered price
The price each of purchased parts can be determined by the quantity in the price calculation if staggered prices are applied for the price list or the default supplier link. Staggered prices are managed as described below.
If a requirement for the part exists, which is greater than the limit value for a staggered price, then the staggered price will be loaded to the calculation. Otherwise, the regular purchase price for the part will be used in the calculation. In the columns Price each and Staggered qty under the List tab, you will see the staggered price and the staggered quantity. Otherwise, the regular purchase price will be shown and the staggered quantity will be 0 (zero).

#### Discount
Here you determine if any discounts on the supplier links on the parts should be included in the valuation of the purchased parts. There is support for discount categories based on part category.

#### Expenses
In this column you determine which expenses should be included in the calculation. The expenses which have been set as active under the Expenses tab in the Calculation mark-up procedure will be included. The available options are:
- Certificate – Includes expenses for certificates for parts with traceability.
- Alloy cost – Includes alloy cost, if it is used on the parts.
- Freight and packaging – Includes expenses for freight and packaging.
- Customs duty – Includes expenses for customs duty.
- Other – Includes other expenses.
- Expenses 4 and 5 – Includes two optional expenses that you can define yourself.
