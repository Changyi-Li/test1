### The Stock count approval list
As the signer/approver of stock counts, you can approve or reject stock counts. The parts for which you reject the stock count will be marked with Stock count request in the Part register, and a comment is also added about this. The parts you approve will get a record in the stock transaction log.
> If the signer/approver is absent, a user with the ERP manager or System administrator role can approve the stock count.
