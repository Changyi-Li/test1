### The list type Authorization settings – EIM
In this list type you see – and can also modify – authorization settings for electronic invoice management (EIM) for the users who are signers.
This list displays a few of the settings available under the Signers tab in the Authorization settings – EIM procedure. You can update Remind of invoice to authorize, Remind of invoice to final record, Send reminder via e-mail, and User right to view invoice documents.
