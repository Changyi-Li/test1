### Selection criteria
In this box you add rows with selections of location names.
You can check the box Exclude if you want to exclude the selection on the row. You can also use wildcard characters in the same way as in selections made for lists. Read more about available wildcard characters in the section [Advanced selection](../../../UserGuide/GeneralFeatures/AdvancedSelection.htm).
By checking the Use selection criteria priority setting, a column open where you can enter a priority for your selection.
This makes it possible to, for example, putaway primarily on Shelf 1, and then continue filling up the locations in descending order.
