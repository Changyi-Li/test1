### Stock count
- If the part has more than one location these will be shown directly as rows when you have entered the part number.
- Default unit for stock count is the unit on the part which have been selected for stock count and stock reporting.
- Your stock count is saved using the button Save in the procedure. In G4 it was saved when you changed row when a new row was inserted.
- When performing stock count of parts applying best-before date, it is mandatory to enter a best-before date. This also applies when stock counting in the procedure Stock count in list.
