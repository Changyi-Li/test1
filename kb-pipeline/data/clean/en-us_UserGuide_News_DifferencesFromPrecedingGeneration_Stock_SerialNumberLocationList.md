### Serial number location list
- The Serial numberA serial number is a number that is used for traceability for parts on entity level. location list displays an overview of serial numbers and their status. The overview consists of a structure map grouped by the location/site of the serial number. The top level shows if the serial number/tool is with us, at a supplier, or at a customer's location. You can then choose which levels to show in the structure map. Levels possible to choose are: Location, Work centerA work center is a part of the factory. It can be a single machine or a group of machines, a single workstation or a group of workstations., Employee, and Department. For each serial number you can see its status.
