### List
In this list you can:
- Update and release agreement bases for invoicing.
- Undo the release of agreement bases – By marking the Undo release checkbox, you can undo a release of agreement basis. However, you cannot undo a release of agreement basis which has already been invoiced.
- Delete agreement basis – By marking the Delete basis checkbox, you can delete agreement basis. You can only delete bases which have not yet been released.
