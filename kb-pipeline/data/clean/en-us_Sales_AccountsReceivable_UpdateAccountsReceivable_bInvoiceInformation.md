### Invoice information
In this box you find basic invoice information. You can modify all fields for new accounts receivable records until you save the first time. After saving, the information/fields will be locked (except for Payment terms and Due date).

#### Invoice type
Here you enter if it is a regular invoice or an interest invoice.

#### Credit
With this checkbox you determine if a credit invoice should be created if it is a new accounts receivable record.

#### Invoice date
The invoice date in this field will by default be today's date for a new accounts receivable record.

#### Payment terms
Here you select the payment terms of the invoice. The customer's payment terms are displayed by default for a new accounts receivable record. The payment terms affect the due date of the invoice.

#### Due date
Here you enter/see the date when the invoice will become overdue. Due date is set automatically based on the selected payment term. If the payment term is linked to cash discounts, this button appears ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_more_info.png) where you can see the cash discounts that apply to the selected payment term.

#### Invoice amount
Here you enter/see the total amount of the invoice including VAT. The amount will be locked for a new accounts receivable record if you mark the checkbox Posting in the Posting box. If you then need to change the amount before you save, you will need to uncheck that box. The currency is loaded by default from the customer for a new accounts receivable record.

#### Exchange rate
If the currency of the invoice is not the company currency, you can here enter the exchange rate. Under the button ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_more_info.png) you can see a log of earlier exchange rate changes. Using the button Go to Currencies ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_link.png) you can link to the Currencies procedure.
When registering a new invoice, the current exchange rate will be used. This is the rate type entered for the customer in the Customer register.

#### VAT group
Here you select the VAT group of the invoice. The VAT group determines the VAT amount and the posting of VAT, if any, generated in the posting window. The customer's VAT group is displayed by default for a new accounts receivable record. The VAT group will be locked for a new accounts receivable record if you mark the checkbox Posting in the Posting box. If you then need to change the VAT group before you save, you will need to uncheck that box.

#### VAT
Here you enter the VAT amount of the invoice. The VAT amount will be locked for a new accounts receivable record if you mark the checkbox Posting in the Posting box. If you then need to change the amount before you save, you will need to uncheck that box.
