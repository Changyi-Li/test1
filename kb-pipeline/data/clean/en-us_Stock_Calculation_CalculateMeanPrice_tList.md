### List
In this list you find information about the selected parts that are loaded in order to calculate a new mean price on purchased parts.
On each part row you can click the drill down button ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_drill_down.png) in order to see information about arrival reported purchase order rows and their purchase prices which are used as basis for the mean price calculation. Rows which have not been arrival reported are displayed with blue text.
Part rows

#### Save
Here you determine for which parts the mean price should be saved.

#### Last calculation date
Here you see the last calculation date.

#### Mean price
Here you see the part's existing mean price.

#### New mean price
Here you see the new mean price from the calculation. If you select Save on the row, you can also manually enter a new mean price.
If for example purchase order rows with deviating purchase prices exist or if the new mean price is less than or equal to zero, then you also see a warning symbol ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/warning.png) in the left column. A tooltip is shown when you hover over the symbol, displaying the cause of the warning in text form. Warnings with clarifying texts are also displayed in the validation window in the bottom right corner of the procedure window.

#### Diff. %
Here you see the difference between the existing mean price and new mean price.

#### Price type
Here you see which price type was used in the calculation.

#### Purchase orders
Here you see the number of purchase orders on which the calculation is based.
Purchase order rows

#### I (Include)
In this column you select which order rows you want to include in the calculation. You can for example deselect order rows that deviate a lot. Rows that are not marked are shown in italic font.
If a purchase order row in the list has a deviating purchase price, all information on that row is highlighted in red and a warning symbol ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/warning.png) is shown in the column New mean price on the part.
