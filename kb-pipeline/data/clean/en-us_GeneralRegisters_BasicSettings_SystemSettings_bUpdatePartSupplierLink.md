### Update part and supplier link from order row
It is possible to update part and supplier link from the following procedures: Register inquiry, Register purchase order, Register supplier invoice, and Authorize supplier invoice.

#### Update standard price
The available alternatives used to update standard price from order row are Current and Future with the following selections:
- Show – The field will be displayed but cannot be updated.
- Show, and possible to update – The field will be displayed and can be updated. However, the Update box will not be checked by default.
- Show, and default to update – The field will be displayed and the Updated box will be checked by default.
Please note! It is logged by whom and when the setting was changed.

#### Update supplier link
Here you select what should be possible to update on supplier links from order rows: Currency, Price, Staggered prices, Setup price, Discount, Supplier's part number, Lead time Number of days between ordering date and delivery date. Normally used for purchased parts., Valid through, Future price, Future setup price, Future valid through, Price comment, and Comment. The following selections can be made for all alternatives above:
- Show – The field will be displayed but cannot be updated.
- Show, and possible to update – The field will be displayed and can be updated. However, the Update box will not be checked by default.
- Show, and default to update – The field will be displayed and the Updated box will be checked by default.
Please note! It is logged by whom and when the setting was changed.

#### Automatically transfer future price to price
If Yes, this means that if a supplier link has an expired price and there is a price in the Future price column, purchase orders created after the expiration date will automatically be updated with the new price. The supplier link in the Part register will also be updated with the new price.
