### Traceability structure
In the two boxes Consists of and Included in under this tab you can analyze if the loaded serial number or batch number consists of other serial numbers/batches and/or is included in other serial numbers/batches. In both boxes you can use the button Load selected record to the record selector ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_replace.png) to load the selected row's serial number/batch in the procedure.
Using the Filter button![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_filter_15x15.png) you can filter the contents in the Consists of box. The following filters are available: Include parts without traceability, Show stock count rows without balance charge, and Show original structure without modifications.
