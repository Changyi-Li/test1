### The Budget and result tab
In the table under the Budget and result tab, you enter your company's annual emissions for the year you entered/selected in the Year field. The table is divided by rows according to the predefined scopes from the GHG protocol. For the definitions of what should belong to each scope, we refer to the GHG protocol's website.
You can add predefined sub-categories by clicking the button called Add new row at the end ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_add_row.png). The pre-defined sub-categories must be registered as Other emissions in the Basic dataWith "basic data" we refer to the static records in a database, for example parts, customers, users, work centers, etc. – Sustainability procedure. You can also enter sub-categories directly in the table. The sub-categories provides you with a more descriptive specification regarding where the values are loaded from. The values of the sub-rows are automatically added together for each category.

#### Total emissions (budget)
In the Total emissions (budget) column, you enter, in each of the sub-category rows, the value for emissions according to that category. These are then added together in the gray category rows and for each scope.

#### %
In the percentage column, you can see how much the different scopes and categories represent on an annual basis.

#### Comment
Here you can enter a comment for the scope.

#### Active
With this checkbox you decide if the scope is active and is used in the sustainability calculations.

#### Total emissions (result)
In the Total emissions (result) column you enter, in each of the sub-category rows, the value for emissions according to that category. These are then added together in the gray category rows and for each scope.

#### Diff.
In the Diff. column, the difference between budget and result is shown.

#### % of budget
In the % of budget column, the difference between budget and result is shown as a percentage.
