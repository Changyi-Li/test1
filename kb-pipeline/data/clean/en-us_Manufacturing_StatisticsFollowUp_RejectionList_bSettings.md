### Settings
Here you can configure settings for type of cost and SO/SC mark-up for the cost calculation of reported rejections.

#### Cost factors
Here you select the types of costs that will form the basis when calculating reported cost and planned cost for the reported quantity. The costs that are selected in the Default cost factors system settings will be shown as default.

#### SO/SC mark-up
With this setting you determine if the material's SO mark-up and the subcontract's SC mark-up should be added to the material and subcontract costs in the cost calculation.
