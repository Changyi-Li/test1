### Result – Reprint
In the left part of the tab you find a table showing which journal (or journals) will be reprinted. In the right section of the tab you see the journal you have selected from the table. You change the journal shown in the Preview box by clicking on a journal in the table to the left.

#### Journal number
In this column you see the number of the journal.

#### Period
Here you see the period which the journal concerns.

#### Approved by
In this column you see who approved the journal.

#### Approval date
Here you see the date when the journal was approved.
