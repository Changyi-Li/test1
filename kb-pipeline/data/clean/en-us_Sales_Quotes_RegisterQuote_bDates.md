### Date
In this box you can see/enter different dates and times regarding the quote.

#### Inquiry date
Here you enter the date when the inquiry arrived from the customer. Today's date is suggested by default. If you have received the inquiry earlier, you will need to change the date manually. This date will not be displayed on quotes, but you can use it in internal lists in order to see how fast you are to register quotes for received inquiries.

#### Quote date
In this field you enter the date from when the quote is valid. Today's date is suggested by default. The quote date is printed on the quote.

#### Validity period
The validity period indicates for how long the quote will be valid in number of calendar days. This is calculated from the quote date. The default validity period is loaded from the customer on the quote. You can select among validity periods registered in the Terms procedure, but you can also enter a text for the validity period that is not registered among the terms. In that case, you must enter the number of calendar days in the Number of days field below. The maximum number of days you can enter here are 365. The text for the validity period is printed on the quote.

#### Valid through
The valid through date is by default set to quote date + validity period, but this can be changed. The valid through date is used as a date to follow up the quotes with valid through date about to expire. When the validity period is in past time, it will be displayed in red.

#### Delivery time
Delivery time is a type of term with which you indicate how long after a customer order is registered, that the customer can expect to receive the delivery. This is entered in number of work days. The suggested delivery time is the one set as default in the Terms procedure. You can select among validity periods registered in that procedure, but you can also enter a text for a delivery time that is not registered among the terms. In that case, you must enter the number of calendar days in the field below. The delivery time is printed on the quote. It is used on quote rows where delivery date is missing, when quotes are turned into customer orders. Customer order rows created from the quote rows get the same delivery date as order date + delivery time.

#### Last contact date
The last contact date is by default empty for new quotes, but you can enter the date when you last were in contact with the customer regarding the quote.
