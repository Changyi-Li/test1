### Terms
In this box you configure different terms for the customer. The terms here are payment terms, any cash discount, delivery terms, delivery method, and validity period.
Here you also find a field regarding invoicing plan. This is available if the system setting Handle invoicing plans on quotes/customer orders is activated. In that field you can select an invoicing plan for the customer. You select among the templates registered in the Invoicing plans procedure.
If you add one or multiple alternative delivery addresses for the customer, then you can also select delivery terms and delivery method – per delivery address. On a new customer order, these terms will then be determined by the selected delivery address on the order.
If the option Warehouse is installed, you can also make exceptions for delivery terms and delivery method, per warehouse and the alternative delivery address which is default for the customer. For a new customer, the same delivery terms and delivery method are saved on all warehouses. If you change warehouse for the customer and select a different term, then these values will only be saved for the warehouse in question and for the alternative delivery address which is default. You can enter a different delivery address as default and select other of these terms, per warehouse for that address. On a customer order, these terms will then be determined by both the selected warehouse and the selected delivery address on the order.
Monitor ERP is delivered with a number of predefined standard terms and in addition to these it is possible create own terms that suit your company and your specific work methods. These terms must first be registered in the Terms procedure. Read more about the different terms in the online Help function for that procedure. If a specific term is missing altogether, the field will be gray. In that case, you will see the button Go to procedure ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_link.png) to the right of the terms field. Click this to go to the Terms procedure.

#### Invoicing plan COT
In systems with the Customer order transfer option, you can select an Invoicing plan COT and it will be used in the production company..
The invoicing plan you select in this field will only be used for transferred orders.
This makes it possible to have two invoicing plans for one customer. One of them is applied when manually registering a customer order, and the other one is applied when a customer order is automatically registered via customer order transfer.
This setting is also available as a field that is possible to edit in the Customer list procedure and it can be added using the backstage of the procedure.
