### View

#### Date
Here you enter between which dates the time check should be made.

#### Date alternative
For results referring to reporting on manufacturing orders it is possible to select which date to use. Actual date means that the entered date for the reporting will be used. Log date means that the reporting date will be used.

#### Only main/sub-projects
With this setting you decide if you only want to load projects which are main or sub-projects, the other projects will be disregarded.
