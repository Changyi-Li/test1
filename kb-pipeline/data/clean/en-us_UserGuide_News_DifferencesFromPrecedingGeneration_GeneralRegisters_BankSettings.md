### Bank settings
- This procedure is new and it does not have an equivalent in G4. However, some of the functionality found in other places in the system in G4, can now be found here, such as Central bank codes.
- You can register the company's own bank accounts with corresponding giro numbers in a separate bank register. The factoring companies' bank information is also registered here.
- There is a table containing payment methods. You can configure settings for these, such as entering which accounts and payment formats they should be linked to. It is also possible to create own payment methods.
- There is a table containing write-off codes. In G4 they were called Diff. codes.
- It is possible to configure for multiple bank accounts (in the same currency) to be shown in the footer of a document, such as an invoice.
