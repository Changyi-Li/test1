### Settings – Purchase order

#### Grouped by
Here you select if one or more operations will end up on the same purchase order.
If you choose to group by Operation, the result will be one (1) operation per purchase order. The options that can provide multiple order rows are:
- Supplier
- Project
- Work centerA work center is a part of the factory. It can be a single machine or a group of machines, a single workstation or a group of workstations.
- Manufacturing order
