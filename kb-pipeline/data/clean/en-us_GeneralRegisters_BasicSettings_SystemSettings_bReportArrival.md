### Report arrival
Here you configure settings that are specific for the mobile client.

#### Suggest quantity for material row
With this setting you can configure the default value for quantity on a material row when arrival reporting manufacturing orders to stock. The following options are available:
- Zero (0)
- Dispatched quantity
- Remaining quantity

#### Suggest quantity for subc.
With this setting you can configure the default value for quantity for subcontracts when arrival reporting to stock. The following options are available:
- Zero (0)
- At supplier
- Remaining quantity

#### Suggest quantity for stock order
With this setting you determine the suggested quantity for stock order. The following options are available:
- Zero (0)
- In transit
