### The list type Material – Total
The list is loaded and displayed based on the selection and settings you have chosen. As in all lists in Monitor ERP, you can drag and drop in order to change the column to group by.
This list shows information in total about reporting made for material. The information shown here is planned and reported quantity, and reported cost both per material and as a total.
The presentation called "Grouped by material's part number" is available as standard.
