### The Detailed list
In this list you can see status and result for each row which has been filled in when maintenance has been reported. The list is available in a general presentation which is not grouped, as well as two presentations grouped by service number and serial number.
The presentation General is not grouped. It shows each row on which maintenance has been reported with information from reported rows.
The presentation Grouped by service number is grouped by service number and it has a sub-grouping in the heading in forms. The list shows information from reported rows corresponding to the General presentation.
The presentation Grouped by service number is grouped by service number and it has a sub-grouping by service number. The list shows information from reported rows corresponding to the General presentation.
