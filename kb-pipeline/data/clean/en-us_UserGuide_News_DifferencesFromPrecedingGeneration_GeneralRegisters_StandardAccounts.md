### Standard accounts
- The standard accounts have been divided under tabs per module.
- There are fewer standard accounts than in G4. All standard accounts concerning input and output VAT have been moved to VAT settings and the liquid accounts have been moved to Bank settings.
- It is possible to enter standard account per customer group/supplier group.
- It is possible to have separate accounts for VAT clearing and VAT recoverable.
- You can enter standard account for excess depreciation.
