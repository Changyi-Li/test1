### Document information
Under this tab you can add, delete and modify rows with currencies which you link to bank account, factoring account, and client number factoring. This information will be printed in the footer on quotes, orders, and invoices.
The purpose is to determine which bank information should be included in the document footer, depending on to which currency the document applies. If you for example invoice in USD you might want to show the company's USD account information in the invoice footer instead of the company's regular bank account.
The table under this tab is empty when you start up the system for the first time. When the table is empty, the information on the document is loaded from the company's default bank account registered in the Company information procedure.

#### Currency
Here you select the currency you want to link to the bank account. You can select among the currencies registered in the Currencies procedure. You can register the same currency multiple times and a warning is displayed if you have selected the footer which does not show multiple bank accounts in the Document templates procedure.

#### Bank account
Here you select the bank account you want to link to the currency, using a maximum of 50 characters. You can only select a bank account that is defined under the Bank accounts tab.

#### Factoring account
Here you select the factoring bank account you want to link to the currency. The field is optional. You can only select a bank account that is defined under the Bank accounts tab. The information will be loaded to the document for factoring customers.

#### Client number factoring
Here you enter the client number for factoring at the bank that applies to the current currency using a maximum of 15 characters. The client number is not shown on the document. It is only used during export of factoring files.
