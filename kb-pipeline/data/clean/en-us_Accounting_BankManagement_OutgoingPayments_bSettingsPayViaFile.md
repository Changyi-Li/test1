### Settings
In this box you select which payment method should apply to the invoices. In the box below you order outgoing payments of invoices that should be sent to the bank via file. The registration is performed by manually entering which invoices to be sent for payment. If you instead want to see a list including the invoices you can pay, you can go to the List tab to see the payment suggestions loaded from the Selection tab.

#### Payment method
Here you select which payment method should be used for the payment. The selected payment method will apply to all invoices added to the Outgoing payments box. The available payment methods are payment methods of the type electronic outgoing payment under the Payment method tab in the Bank settings procedure. If you have entered a default payment method for electronic outgoing payment in the Bank settings procedure, this payment method will be suggested.
