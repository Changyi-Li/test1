### Settings – Selection

#### Ledger date
In this field you enter for which date the statement applies. The purpose of this is to print a statement for a specific date in passed time. In order for this to work, the invoice dates and payment dates has to be taken into consideration for the records that will be included. If you enter a date in the past, then no invoices issued after that date will be shown in the result. The invoice will be shown as unpaid if has been printed on the ledger date or before the ledger date, even if the payment was made after the date.
The remaining amount on the document considers when the payment was made. The Ledger date is shown on the document, but no time will be shown. The field shows today's date by default.

#### Number of copies
Here you select the number of copies that should be printed. The default value here is 1. If more than 1 copy is printed it will say "COPY" on all documents following the first printout. This feature can be turned off with the setting Mark as copy for each document in the Document settings procedure.
The default number of copies is loaded from the settings for statements in the Document settings procedure.

#### Our reference
Here you enter your own reference that will be shown on the documents.

#### Supplier’s reference
The statement is sent to a specific supplier and you can enter which reference at the supplier’s that should be stated on the document. This field is a text field. If you leave this field empty, the reference marked as default in the supplier register will be printed on the document.

#### Include
Determines which types of records should be included in the bank statement. The options are: final recorded invoice, payment, and ordered payment. By default, final recorded invoice and payment are included in the statement.
> When you choose to include ordered payments, the date selection will be based on the date when they where sent to the bank, not when the payment out should take place.

#### Include suppliers with no balance
With this setting you decide if documents should be shown for suppliers that do not have a balance for the selected time period (ledger date).

#### Include suppliers with no transactions
With this setting you decide if documents should be displayed for suppliers that do not have any transactions for the selected time period (ledger date).
