### Settings

#### Update to status
With this setting you determine which status that the manufacturing orders in the list will be updated to when saving. The setting also determines which manufacturing orders that will be displayed in the list. If you choose to update to status 6, the list will only show manufacturing orders with status 4 and 5. If you choose to update to status 9, the list will show manufacturing orders with status 4, 5, and 6.

#### Pre-select "Include"
- All orders – This option determines that the checkbox Include will be checked for all the manufacturing orders in the list, regardless of any warnings.
- Only orders without warnings (default) – This option determines that the checkbox Include only will be checked for the manufacturing orders in the list that do not have any warnings regarding: 1) operation or material with remaining quantity, 2) operation missing reported time or cost.
All selected manufacturing orders will be included in the list regardless of this setting. The manufacturing orders that fulfill the condition will be selected by default to be included in the status update.
