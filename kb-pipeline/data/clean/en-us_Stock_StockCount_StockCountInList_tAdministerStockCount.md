### The list type Manage stock count
You use this list to report, pause, or delete stock count bases. Under the List tab you choose the stock count basis/bases you wish to manage.
With the Start stock count button ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_run.png) you load data for the selected lists and the Reprint and Reporting tabs becomes available. The you can start entering the result of the stock count. It is also possible to add a new location, enter quantity, revision, batch/serial number, and enter a comment. You can also reprint of stock count bases.
> You cannot change location during stock count.
With the button called Pause stock count ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/StatusOpPaused.png) you can pause the reporting of a stock count. If you have paused a stock count list you can continue the reporting at a later time.
Using the Delete stock count button ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_trash.png) you can delete incorrect/out of date stock count bases.
