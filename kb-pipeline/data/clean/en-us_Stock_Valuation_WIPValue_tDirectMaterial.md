### The Direct material tab
Under this tab you will see information about planned and reported quantities, the reported material cost excluding SO, and the reported SO mark-up.
Using the button Go to the level list ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_link.png) on each row, you can link to the material under the Level list tab and see where it is located in the structure.
The tab is similar to the corresponding tab in the Post-calculation procedure. If rejections should affect the post-calculation is determined by the setting Include cost for rejection. In the Post-calculation procedure rejections are part of the cost.
