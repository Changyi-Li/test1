#### Heading for cost factor 1–3
Here you enter the headings for the three different cost factors that are configured for work centers.

#### Time horizon filters by
This setting affects the filtering by time horizon in the priority plan in the Priority planning and Recording terminal procedures. There are three alternatives to choose from: New finish date, Planned start date, and Planned finish date.
In the Work center register procedure you enter a Time horizon in priority plan in number of work days for each work center.
