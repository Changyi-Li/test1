### The Sales price list
This list shows detailed information about sales price changes for the selected parts.

#### Log date
Here you see the date when the price change was made.

#### Time
Here you see the time when the price change was made.

#### Price list
Here you see the price list.

#### Old price
Here you see the price before the change was made.

#### New price
Here you see the price after the change was made.

#### Price difference
Here you see the difference between the old and new price.

#### Discount
Here you see the discount, if any.

#### Price alternative
Here you see the current price alternative.

#### Price type
Here you see the price type, for example Price each and Staggered price Staggered price means the part has one price for one sales volume/purchase volume (quantity) and another price for another sales volume/purchase volume, etc. Staggered prices can also be called a "price ladder". each.

#### Staggered quantity
Here you see the staggering limits.

#### User
Here you see the user who has changed the price.

#### Type of change
Here you see how the price change was made. That is, if it was manually changed or if it was changed by a pre-calculation.
