### Manual level list
In the two boxes called Consists of serial numbers/batches and Included in serial number/batch which you find under this tab, you can analyze if the loaded serial number consists of other serial numbers/batch numbers. You can also see if it is included in other serial numbers/batch numbers.
In the Consists of serial numbers/batches box, you can also add and delete serial numbers and batch numbers. Here you see if a consists of serial number/batch number has replaced another serial number/batch number. You see the date from when it was replaced and a date to when it was replaced, if such date has been entered, and you also see the serial number/batch number which was replaced. By clicking the Changelog button ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_log.png) you can see what type of changes have been made in the box, by whom it was made and when.
In both boxes you can use the button Load the selected serial number to the record selector ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_replace.png) to load the selected row's serial number to the procedure.
