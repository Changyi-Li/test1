### Columns
In this box you can select the type of records that you want to include in the cash flow forecast.

#### Include
With this checkbox you determine whether or not records of this type will be included in the report.

#### Include overdue
If Include is selected, you can in this column choose whether you wish to include overdue/lagging records or not.
For certain types of overdue records (e.g. customer order rows), you can choose whether lagging records will be kept or moved. You might e.g. wish to see what the forecast looks like if the planned delivery date of the overdue/lagging customer order rows (that is, customer order rows with a planned delivery date in past time) would be moved up to Start date plus the number of days in the delivery terms of those orders. In that case, you select Yes, move lagging records.
