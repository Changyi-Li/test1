### Invoice address/Delivery address
In this box you see the customer's invoice address as well as delivery address. The information in this box corresponds to the information in the [Invoicing](../../Orders/RegisterCustomerOrder/bInvoicing.htm) box under the Invoice address button on a customer order. When needed, you can change address information for the specific invoice basis. You can also see, modify, or change the customer's delivery address. See below.
The address information is by default loaded from the Customer register procedure.

#### Delivery address
By clicking the button Delivery address ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_more_info.png) you see the delivery address for the customer on the invoice basis. You can change all address information for the specific invoice basis. By clicking the button Change address ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_more_info.png) you can change to a different delivery address. The delivery address determines different terms. It also determines posting and VAT.
