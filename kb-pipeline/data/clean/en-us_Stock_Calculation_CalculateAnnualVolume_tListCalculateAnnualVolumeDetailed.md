### The list Calculate annual volume – Detailed
This list type is a combination of the two lists called Update annual volume and Explosion. The list loads saved annual volume for main parts.
After loading the list you find yourself in a mode where you in the Standard box can modify the new quantity and order quantity, and also select which rows to include in the calculation.
The Last reset of annual volume and Last update of annual volume columns (found by clicking the More info button ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_more_info.png) in the Standard box), enable you to sort, select, and to exclude parts from previous resets and calculations.
In a second step you start calculation using the Start calculation button ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_run.png) (Ctrl + R) on the toolbar of the procedure. A structure explosion is then made and it calculates the resulting annual volumes also for the included/incorporated parts.
In the box to the right, Consists of, you see which incorporated parts the main part consists of, showing the new quantity and order quantity which can both be changed.
In the two boxes called Work centers and Time distribution in manufacturing you see setup times and unit times per work center or per department in a chart. By using the Show/hide charts button ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_chart.png) on the function menu in the Standard box, you can choose to display or hide those boxes.
