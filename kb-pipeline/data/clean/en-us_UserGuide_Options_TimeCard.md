# TimeCard
![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/Options/TimeCard.png)
This option is perfect for repairmen, assemblers, and other employees who work off-premises, or who do not have access to a recording terminal. TimeCard is a convenient solution for salaried employees who have non-regulated working hours, and only approve their time worked and report deviations, such as absence. You can use TimeCard at any time and anywhere by logging in via mobile, tablet, or computer.
Monitor ERP TimeCard is a web-based solution that allows you to easily report work and attendance subsequently without direct access to the Monitor client or a recording terminal. Your reporting items are saved directly to Monitor ERP.
As a basic requirement, those using Monitor ERP TimeCard must have the Time recording module in Monitor ERP.
The schedule of the logged in user in Monitor ERP is shown in the calendar in TimeCard. The user’s parameter group and settings in the personnel records determine what is accessible in TimeCard.
All recording items are saved directly in Monitor ERP.
[![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/Options/TimeCard.png)](../../../Resources/Images/Options/TimeCard.png)

#### Reporting
All work reported in TimeCard is followed by the corresponding attendance recording. So every time unit a user reports work, the attendance for the day will increase correspondingly. Please note! The time is added in the order in which the work is registered.
