### Authorize/Adjust recording
- All work recording items are found under a tab in the procedure Authorize/Adjust recording. Here you can also view the reported material for each recording.
- If you want to delete an operation you must first adjust the quantity to zero and save, then you can delete that row. When you adjust the quantity for an operation with transfer to stock, you can in the location window enter location information for the quantity affected by the adjustment. If you change from 3 pcs to 4 pcs you should in the location window enter transfer to stock for 1 piece. If you change from 4 pcs to 3 pcs an alternative location window will open where you enter from where 1 piece should be taken.
- When you have marked one of the operation rows it will be shown in the material section under the tab. There you see the material list which was reported in the recording item in question. You can also make adjustments.
- If you want to adjust quantity for an operation without affecting the material, then you uncheck the checkbox Automatic withdrawal of material.
- It is possible to allow an addition to affect a time bank. You both increase a balance or decrease a balance. This can for example be used when cashing out saved time or to add/assign hours as "emergency compensation". You find settings for this in the Basic dataWith "basic data" we refer to the static records in a database, for example parts, customers, users, work centers, etc. – Attendance procedure.
