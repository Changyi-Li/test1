### Settings

#### Payment date
Here you select the payment date when the invoices should be set-off. Today's date is displayed by default, but it can be changed. The payment date that is selected here will apply as default payment date for the invoice at the set-off. If the payment date entered here is outside the open accounting periods, an error message will appear.

#### Voucher text
Here you select the text that will be used as voucher text for set-offs that you register. The default text is “Set-off”. In the Voucher texts procedure you can create texts to use as pre-defined voucher texts.
