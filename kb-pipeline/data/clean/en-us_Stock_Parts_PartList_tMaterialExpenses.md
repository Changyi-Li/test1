### The Expenses list
In this list you can see different mark-ups such as purchase expenses for parts. It is possible to make the list updateable in order to modify these expenses. The expenses shown are the expenses which have been set as active under the Expenses tab in the Calculation mark-up procedure.
In the list it is also possible to update quantity per package and net weight. You can also select alloy code/mark-up for the parts.
Expenses and mark-ups are used to make pre-calculations and to calculate cost price, standard price, and quote price for the purchased parts. This can be made for several parts at a time in the Pre-calculation – Purchased parts procedure or per part under the Purchase tab in the Part register procedure.
