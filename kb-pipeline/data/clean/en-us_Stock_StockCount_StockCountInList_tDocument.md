### The Documents tab
Here you can preview the Stock count basis document before printout. The sorting and the settings made under the Selection tab affects the contents of the document.
You print by clicking the Print button ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_print.png) (Ctrl + P) on the toolbar of the procedure.
By using the Export stock count basis button ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_export.png), you can export the basis to Excel (.csv) in order to do the reporting in Excel and then import the result back into the Manage stock count list.

#### Printed by (employee)
Here you can choose which person should appear on the document (who printed the document), in cases where you did not choose this in the setting under the Selection tab.
