### Measuring templates
- The Measuring templates procedure in G5 corresponds to the procedure called Measuring Points - Manufacturing in G4.
- Settings for frequency have been expanded so you when selecting Interval can choose how many details should be measured each time.
- There is also a new option called Other where you can enter a text describing when measuring should take place. This option will not provide any automatic warnings if you have missed to perform a measuring.
- The rows with measuring points are added in a form in the Measuring forms procedure.
- Measuring template and forms can be linked to parts.
