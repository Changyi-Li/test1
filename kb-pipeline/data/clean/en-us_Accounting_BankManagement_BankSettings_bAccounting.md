### Accounting

#### Bookkeeping account
Here you enter the account in the chart of accounts on which transactions with the bank should be recorded. The account is loaded when making outgoing and incoming payments where electronic payment methods defined under the Payment method tab are used.

#### Record bank charge separately
Check this box if the bank charge should be charged separately from another account. If you check the box, you select on which account the bank charge should be posted in the field below.

#### Bookkeeping account charges
Here you enter the account for bank charges if this should be recorded separately.
