### Settings

#### Include records for which a dispatch advice has already been sent
Not showing the dispatch advice which have already been sent, is the standard mode. By activating this setting, the already sent dispatch advices, will also be included in the list. This can be useful if you wish to resend the dispatch advice.

#### Include records where "Exclude from EDI" is activated
With this setting you can choose to include records in the list which are marked as excluded from EDI EDI is the acronym of Electronic Data Interchange. EDI is about exchanging electronic business documents with your business partners, e.g. customers and suppliers. The EDI concept can be wide and a bit unclear, and can many times be used about all types of documents which are sent electronically, even if it might be PDF files sent via e-mail or publishing business documents on a website. What we refer to as EDI – and what is traditionally meant by EDI – is structured business documents following given standards, electronically sent or received and which are compiled and interpreted automatically and that is integrated with the customer's/supplier's ERP system.. These records cannot be marked and cannot be used to send dispatch advice for.

#### Exclude records without shipments
Records not connected to a shipment, are included in the list as a standard. If you want to make sure that a dispatch advice is not sent until a shipment has been created, you can activate this setting. When this setting is activated, only records with linked shipments will be included in the list.

#### Pre-select "Include"
This setting determines whether or not the “Include” box should be checked by default for all rows in the list/result.
