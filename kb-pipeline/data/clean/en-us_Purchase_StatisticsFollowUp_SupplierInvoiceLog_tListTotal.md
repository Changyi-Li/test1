### The Total list
In this list you can see total information from the supplier invoice log. On each row you can drill down and view the detailed information. The list shows information both from linked order rows and posting rows in the final coding. Total price difference (TPD) and Total price difference % (TPD %) are calculated for entire order. It is not possible to update any information in this list.
The list is loaded and displayed based on the selection and settings you have chosen. As in all lists in Monitor ERP, you can drag and drop in order to change the column to group by.
