### Periods
In this box you will see one row per month in the budget chart. You enter the percentage distribution for each month. The total percentage distribution for all months must be 100%.
By using the Create even distribution button ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_auto_withdrawal_material.png) you can automatically create an even distribution i a budget chart based on the entered number of periods.

#### Percentage distribution
Here you enter the percentage distribution for each month. You can enter a portion in percent with a maximum of four decimals.
