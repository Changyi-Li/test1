### Release customer agreement basis
Instead of releasing or undoing a release under the Agreement basis tab in the Register customer agreement procedure, you can in this procedure release multiple agreement bases in a list.
Here you create a list containing the agreement bases you want to work with and then you mark the ones you want to release or undo release for.
