## Production calendar
In this procedure you save all deviations/exceptions from the planned schedule. This applies to work centers with hourly planning or capacity via schedule. Deviations/exceptions from the planned basic time are also saved in this procedure. This applies to work centers with day planning. Deviating capacity can also be entered in the Work center A work center is a part of the factory. It can be a single machine or a group of machines, a single workstation or a group of workstations. register, but in that procedure you can only handle one work center at a time and register one deviating capacity per row.
In this procedure you can make a selection of several work centers and register deviating capacity for several dates at a time. How you enter the deviating capacity (either according to a schedule or basic time in hours) depends on the time precision of the work centers. You can only select by time precision if you have activated the system setting Enable hourly planning for work center.
> In this procedure you can also register deviations in capacity/vacations for subcontractors.
