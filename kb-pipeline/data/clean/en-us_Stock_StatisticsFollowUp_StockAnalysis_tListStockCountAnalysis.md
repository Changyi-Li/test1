### The Stock count analysis list

#### Year
Here you select for which year you wish to analyze planned and completed stock counts, as well as stock counts in progress.
In the boxes for the list you see the result for the selected year (with the year shown within parenthesis in the box headings).
See descriptions of the different boxes vi the links in the side menu to the right in the online help.
> By clicking the Floating button ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_float_window.png) (Ctrl + U) at the top right corner of each box, you can unpin the box from the procedure window and enlarge it.
