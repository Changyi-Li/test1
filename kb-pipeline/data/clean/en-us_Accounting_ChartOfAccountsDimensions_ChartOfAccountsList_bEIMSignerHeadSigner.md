### EIM signer/Head signer
In this list type you can see and modify signer/approver and head signer for accounts. If you apply posting on row level for expense invoices (activated via the system setting called Use extended authorization on posting rows, this tab will be available.

#### Signer/Approver
Here you see the signer/approver for the account.

#### Head signer
Here you see the head signer for the account.
