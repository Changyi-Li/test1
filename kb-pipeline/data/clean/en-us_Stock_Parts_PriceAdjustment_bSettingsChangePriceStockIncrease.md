### Settings – Change price on stock increases – FIFO
If you do not configure any settings here, you must enter the values you want to use in the New price column.
Prices

#### Copy price
Here you decide if price should be copied to update the price in the list.

#### Copy from price
Here you decide which price to copy from.

#### Price list
This field is only available if you have selected Price list or Future price list as price to copy from.

#### Customer number
This field is only available if you have selected Customer price as price to copy from.
Adjustment

#### Adjust with
In this field you select how the price should be adjusted: by percent or amount. The default option is percent. A value field is shown to the right where you can enter a percentage or an amount. If this field is left empty, no adjustment will be made and the price in the list has to be manually adjusted part by part.
The formula for copying new price will be: price to copy from × adjust with × exchange rate.

#### Number of decimals in new price
Here you enter how many decimals should be saved/managed in the new price. The default option is 2 decimals, and the maximum is 6 decimals.

#### Number of significant digits in new price
Here you enter how many significant digits should be managed in the new price. This is empty by default. If you select, for example, 1 it means integers will be used. If you select 2, tens will be used.
