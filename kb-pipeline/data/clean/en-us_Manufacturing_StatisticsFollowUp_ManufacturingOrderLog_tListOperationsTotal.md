### The list Operations – Total
The list is loaded and displayed based on the selection and settings you have chosen. As in all lists in Monitor ERP, you can drag and drop in order to change the column to group by.
This list shows information in total about reporting for operations. The information displayed is reported quantity, reported setup time and unit time, rejected quantity, reported setup cost and unit cost.
The list is added together according to selected presentation. Total by date, Total by work center, and Total per employee, are the presentations available as standard. When added together (total) by date you can also select to group dates by day, week, month, quarter, or year.
