### Calendar view
In this box you can see a calendar view for the work center that been checked (is in focus) in the work center table. For each day you can see the work center’s regular capacity and its deviating capacity. A total of the work center’s current capacity per week is shown to the right in the calendar.
If you want to enter a deviating capacity for a certain day in the calendar, then you mark that day in the calendar view and enter the deviating capacity. Enter basic time and number of machines directly on the day in the calendar. For work centers of the type Manual work you can only enter basic time.
You can enter deviating capacity for multiple work centers and calendar days at the same time. For those work centers you should then check Include in the table of work centers. In the calendar view you can then mark one or several days. To mark more than one day, click and hold down the left mouse button, then drag and mark the dates you wish to choose. You can also mark more than one day by pressing the Ctrl key and clicking the individual days you wish to choose. Another way to do it is to mark one calendar day, hold down the Shift key and then click on a different day. This way you mark all days between the days in question. In the Deviating capacity box you finally enter the deviating capacity for these selected days.
You can only register deviating capacity for work centers with the same time precision.
