### Environmental impact

#### Material type
Here you select the material type for the part. The material type can determine Emissions in the field below. This is primarily used for purchased parts where you do not have exact data from the supplier.
> It is important to make sure that the part’s net weight is correct before you add the material type. If the net weight is changed after the material typpe was selected, NO AUTOMATIC CALCULATION will take place. You can synchronize emissions for parts which have had their emission values or weights updated in the Sustainability list procedure.

#### Emissions
If you selected a Material type in the field above, the general emission factor registered for the material type will be loaded. These values are loaded from the Basic dataWith "basic data" we refer to the static records in a database, for example parts, customers, users, work centers, etc. – Sustainability procedure. If you have received exact values from the supplier, you should apply these instead of general values.

#### Overhead
Here you select the type of overhead emissions for the part. The overhead types must first registered in the Basic data – Sustainability procedure. Overheads are then used in the Sustainability calculation and Emission statistics procedures.

#### Emission calculations
For manufactured parts, the result of the most recently saved emission calculation is displayed here.
