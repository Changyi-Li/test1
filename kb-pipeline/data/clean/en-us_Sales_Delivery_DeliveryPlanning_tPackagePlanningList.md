### The Planned packaging consumption list
When delivering parts there is in most cases also a need of packaging material. If you have linked packaging material (or packaging templates) to parts, you can use this list to see the need for different packaging material as well as when they are needed. The list is based on all order rows where final delivery has not yet been made, and shows the demand of packaging required to deliver the remaining order quantity.
> Create a customized presentation of the list type where you exclude the column Type of requirement. You will then get a list which provides a total of the demands for Fixed order and Forecast for Period and Packaging parts.

#### Period
This is based on the delivery date of the order row. The format depends on the grouping under the Selection tab.

#### Packaging part
Here you see the part number of the packaging part.

#### Name
Here you see the name of the packaging part.

#### Quantity
The quantity required of the packaging part for the selected period.

#### Type of requirement
Here you see the type of requirement (from the order row). You configure which requirements to show under the Selection tab. The following requirements exist:
- 1 Fixed order
- 2 Manufactured
- 3 Buy material
- 4 Forecast
