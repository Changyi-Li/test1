### Report traceable material
- A completely new procedure where you in a more rational way can report withdrawal of material traceable at batch level or at serial number level.
- The difference between this procedure and the Report material procedure is that here the reporting focuses on batch/serial number. Multiple batches/serial numbers can be reported on a material's report number. It is also possible to use a bar code scanner to load/read the batch number/serial number.
- Since traceable material is rarely automatically reported when the operation is reported, this reporting is often done separately and preferably prior to reporting the operation.
- The report number for the material is manually entered or loaded via the bar code and then you select batch or serial number (depending on the traceability level of the part).
