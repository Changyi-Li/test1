### The Total list
In the total list you can drill down to reach more detailed information. At the bottom of the tab you can see a chart containing the amount.

#### Forecast code
Here you can see the forecast code. This code is a unique number for the forecast.

#### Name
Here you can see the name of the forecast.

#### Active
With this checkbox you determine if the forecast is active or not.

#### Customer, customer group, and district
Here you see a certain customer, customer group, or district, linked to the forecast. It is only possible to have one of these linked to the forecast. For forecasts linked to a certain customer, only customer orders for that specific customer will be deducted from the forecast.

#### Amount
The amount is calculated as the Price each in the company currency x Quantity.
