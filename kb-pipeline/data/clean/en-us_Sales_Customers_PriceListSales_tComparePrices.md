### The Compare prices list
The prices alternatives you select under settings are compared in this list. On each row you see the part number and part name of the part to which the price refers. The price alternatives are shown in separate columns. When comparing, the difference between the prices is calculated in the company currency. Difference in percent is also shown. This can be used to calculate a part's contribution margin The contribution margin (CM) is the difference between the standard price and the sales price. and contribution ratio The contribution ratio (CR) is the portion of the invoice amount (sales price) that the contribution margin represents. CR is entered as a percentage..

#### Difference (Diff.)
The difference is calculated as Price alternative 1 - Price alternative 2.

#### Difference in percent (%)
The difference in percent is calculated as Difference ∕ Price alternative 1.
