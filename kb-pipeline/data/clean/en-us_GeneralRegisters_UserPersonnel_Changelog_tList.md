### The List tab
In the changelog list you see information about changes made on data records in different registers.
The available information is date and time of the change, who made the change, which record was changed, in which register, type of change, which field was changed, as well as the value before and after the change.
The list is available in four presentations: Log record – A non-grouped presentation. Time – A presentation grouped by date and time. User – A presentation grouped by user. Register record – A presentation grouped by register records.
