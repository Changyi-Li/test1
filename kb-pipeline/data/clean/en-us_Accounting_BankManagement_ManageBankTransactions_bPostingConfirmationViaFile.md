### Posting – Manage transactions via file
In this box you see the posting of the payments. The postings are shown for the invoice you have selected above. This applies to matched payments. If you select a ledger record which has not been matched, no posting will be shown.
The account for the confirmation is loaded as follows:
1. The entered account for the currency in question for the payment method which is set under the Payment method tab in the Bank settings procedure.
2. The account entered for the currency in question under the Bank accounts in the Bank settings procedure.
3. If no bank account has been entered for the currency under the Bank accounts tab, then the account entered in this procedure (Incoming payments) will be loaded.

#### Total accounting order
At the top of the box you will see the setting Total accounting order. If you check this checkbox, you will see a total posting for the postings of all the invoices. This allows you to easier check that everything is correctly recorded before you save the incoming payment batch. When you activate the setting Total accounting order, the postings will be automatically total and it is no longer possible to change these postings in the box. If there are different payment dates among the registered payments of the batch, the total posting will be divided by payment date.
