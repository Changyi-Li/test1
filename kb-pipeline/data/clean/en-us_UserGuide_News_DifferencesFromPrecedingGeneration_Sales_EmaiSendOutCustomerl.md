### E-mail – Customer
- New separate procedure for e-mailing customers. In G4 you send e-mails to customers via a specific list in the procedure Print Customer Label.
- You can select to which Recipient of you want to send e-mails. For example you want to send an e-mail to all customer addresses listed as recipient of invoice. In G4 it is only possible to choose between sending e-mail to the customers' references or to the main addresses.
- You can choose if the e-mail should be logged in the CRM.
- You can decide if all e-mail addresses should be copied to the Bcc field (blind carbon copy) in the e-mail message you create for the e-mail. Otherwise they will be copied to the regular e-mail address field.
