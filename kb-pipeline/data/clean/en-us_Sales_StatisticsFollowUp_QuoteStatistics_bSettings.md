### Settings

#### Price alternative
With this setting you determine which price to use to calculate the contribution margin/contribution ratio, that is, which price to compared with the sales price. There are four options:
- Standard price – With this alternative, a manually entered standard price on the quote row will primarily be loaded. Secondarily, the standard price will be loaded from the part register. This price is selected by default.
- Future standard price – With this alternative, the future standard price will be loaded from the part register.
- Post-calculated mean price – With this alternative, the post-calculated mean price will be loaded from the part register.
- Price list – This alternative makes the next setting available. There you can select which price list to use for valuation of the contribution margin.

#### Price list
Here you select the price list to use for valuation of the contribution margin.

#### Include only main rows
With this checkbox you determine whether or not only main rows should be displayed in the list. The setting is activated by default. If you uncheck the box, sub-rows will also be included in the list.

#### Include rows set as excluded
With this setting you determine if quote rows which you during the registration configured to be Excluded from statistics, should be shown in the list. By default, this setting is not activated.
