### The Total list
This list type shows total information about the internal delivery reliability. You can total the list by work center or by actual (reporting) date. The list can also be presented as a chart in the lower part of the window.

#### Calculated columns:
Total quantity = On time (count) + Late (count) + Early (count)
Early (%) = (Early (count) / Total count) x 100
On time (%) = (On time (count) / Total count) x 100
Late (%) = (Late (count) / Total count) x 100
