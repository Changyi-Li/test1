### The Reprint tab
This tab becomes available when you have started the stock count under the List tab. Here you can preview the printout of the stock count bases you chose under the List tab. In the left section of the tab you mark the stock count basis you want to preview. Next to this column you can also see who printed the document the first time.
When needed, you can reprint the marked stock count basis by using the Print button ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_print.png) (Ctrl + P) on the toolbar of the procedure.
