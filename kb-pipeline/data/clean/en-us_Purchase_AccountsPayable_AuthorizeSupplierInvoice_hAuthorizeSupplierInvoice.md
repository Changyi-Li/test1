### Header row

#### Consecutive number
Here you enter the consecutive number of the supplier invoice. Only invoices that have been sent to you or to your signer group for authorization will be displayed.

#### Supplier
In this field you see/enter the supplier who has sent the invoice.

#### Order number
If the invoice in question has a link to a purchase order, the purchase order number will be shown in this field. The information is updated if you make any changes in the order links under the Order link tab.

#### Reason for authorization
Here you see the reason why the invoice needs to be authorized.
