### Priority plan
The priority plan box shows the pool’s priority plan and columns including information about the operations. The information on the rows are loaded from the manufacturing order. Please see the online help function for [Priority plan](../PriorityPlanning/bPriorityPlan.htm) in the Priority planning procedure and the online help function for [Order](../../Orders/RegisterManufacturingOrder/bOrders.htm) and [Operations](../../Orders/RegisterManufacturingOrder/bOperations.htm) in the Register manufacturing order procedure. There you find descriptions of functions and information that are included in the priority plan.
By using the Suggest new work center button ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_streamline.png) on the function menu, the system will suggest a work center to change to for each operation, based on the work center that will get the earliest date for New finish. The suggested work center is shown in the New work center column.

#### Include
You here select the rows you wish to include. Primarily, this is used when replanning the operations of the pool and delegating them to the pool's related work centers. The rows can also be included when you go to other procedures by linking, or be included for direct material clearance.

#### New work center
In this column you can manually change to another work center by selecting another one of the related work centers. You can also drag and drop the row on the new work center in the Related work centers box. Each work center is given a specific color in the box.
