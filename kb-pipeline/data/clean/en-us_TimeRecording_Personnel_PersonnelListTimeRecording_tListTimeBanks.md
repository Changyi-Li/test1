### The Time banks list
In this list you can see information about the personnel's balances in time banks. It can for example be how much flex time or makeup time the members of the personnel have. The list is grouped by employee number and cannot be updated.
The Time bankTime banks are used in order to save recorded time outside scheduled working hours. Different time banks are for example used for comp time, makeup time, flex time (positive/negative), and shorter working hours. column in the list displays the time banks that exist for the staff. In the Balance column you see the time balance which exists in each time bank.
At the bottom of the window you find a summary of the total time balance in each time bank, for the personnel selected for the list.
