### Terms (Authorization limit exceptions)
In this box you add the terms that should apply to the selected authorization limit in the exception.

#### Based on
Here you determine on what the term should be based. You can choose from Supplier category, Supplier group, or Supplier.
For supplier category, you can read how to create/construct categories in the online help function for the [Categories](../../Categories/Categories/wCategories.htm) procedure.

#### From
Here you enter or choose the selection on which authorization limit exceptions should be based. For supplier group and supplier, you can enter an interval in this field. The CS field will then be activated. For supplier category, you can enter a category or use the button in the CS field in order to select a registered supplier category.

#### CS
If you have selected to base the term on supplier category, you can here use the button ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_more_info.png) to select a registered supplier category. If you have no registered supplier categories the button is deactivated. You must then enter a category in the From field.
If you have selected to base the term on supplier group or supplier, this field works as a To field for the interval.
