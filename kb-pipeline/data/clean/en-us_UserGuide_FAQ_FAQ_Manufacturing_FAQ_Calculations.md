### FAQ – Calculations
What are pre- and post- calculations used for?
Pre-calculations are used to see planned costs, throughput times, and lead times, based on data from BOM and routing, parts, and work centers.
Post-calculations are used to calculate and compare reported costs per manufacturing order, project, or part, with the planned costs.
You can read more about [pre-calculations](../../Using/Calculation/Precalculation.htm) and [post-calculations](../../Using/Calculation/Postcalculation.htm) here.
Why does the price save when I save a pre-calculation?
If you want prices to be saved, they need to be entered under the Selection tab under Saving.

Save prices in Pre-calculation.
Why does post-calculation show different reported cost values under the List and Summary tabs?
Manufacturing cost is always shown as Reported cost in the List tab in the Post-calculation procedure. If you want to compare planned cost and reported cost, we recommend using the Summary tab.
Which list type should I choose to export the post-calculation to Excel?
Use the Details tab for export to Excel. You may have to scroll down to see the Details tab.
You can read more about [the Details tab](../../../Manufacturing/Calculations/PostCalculation/tDetails.htm) here.
