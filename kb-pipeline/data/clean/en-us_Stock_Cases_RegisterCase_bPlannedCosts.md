### Planned costs
In this box you can modify the planned costs for the case. The costs suggested are determined by the template entered on the header row. In order to calculate the costs, click the button Calculate costs ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_calculate.png) in the toolbar of the procedure.
The planned costs can be of different types. Some activities have a planned time and an hourly cost which gives a planned cost that is totaled on one row. There is a planned hourly cost for the activity. This is entered in the Basic dataWith "basic data" we refer to the static records in a database, for example parts, customers, users, work centers, etc. – Cases procedure.
Other types are linked manufacturing orders and operations with material, subcontract, and processing costs. Together they form a planned manufacturing cost, similar to a pre-calculation. Other costs are loaded from the basic data of the case type. These additional costs can be added, deleted, or modified.
The total time and cost are shown as a total at the bottom of the box.
