## Report material
In this procedure you can easily report quantity for one material at a time, by entering the report number of the material.
If you are making a partial reporting for the material, the system will add up the quantity for each partial report that is made. The remaining quantity (that reserves material) is deducted by the corresponding quantity that was reported. The reporting that takes place will deduct the stock balance for the material, and you can see it in the Post-calculation procedure. It is possible to report traceable material which is linked to subcontract.
In the Report operation procedure you can report material automatically, provided that you have activated the setting Material follows operation in the procedure and the setting Exclude from automatic reporting of material can not be selected for the work center in the Work centerA work center is a part of the factory. It can be a single machine or a group of machines, a single workstation or a group of workstations. register.
Sometimes the actual material consumption can be difficult to calculate during in the BOM and routing, for example for raw materials such as sheet-metal. During automatic reporting of material, it might occur that the planned quantity, and later on the reported quantity, might not fully correspond to the actual stock withdrawal. In that case you can adjust the quantity after the fact in this procedure. Material rows with a suggested material withdrawal from pick location, or pick location for work center, are shown in italics.
An already performed reporting of material can be undone in the Undo reporting procedure. This situation can arise if, for example, an incorrect quantity was reported.
