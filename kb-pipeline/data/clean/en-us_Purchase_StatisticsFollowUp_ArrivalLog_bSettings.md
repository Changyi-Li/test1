### Settings

#### Price alternative PD/PD %
Here you select a price alternative used to compare with the purchase price on the arrival reported order rows. Differences between purchase price compared to the selected price alternative are shown in the columns PD The price difference (PD) is calculated as purchase price minus standard price for purchased parts. Regarding subcontracts, PD is calculated as actual cost for subcontract (the price on the supplier invoice) minus planned cost for subcontract. and PD % (price difference) in the list.

#### Price list
If the Price list alternative has been selected in the previous setting you can in this field select which price list that the purchase price should be compared to.

#### Show supplier invoice price
With this setting you decide if the Supplier invoice price (that is, the price you find on the supplier invoice) and Supplier invoice value columns should be shown in the list. This provides you with a better ability to follow the data of the stock transaction log. This way you can follow the arrival reported price and the price according to the supplier invoice.
