### The Detailed – Customer/Supplier list
This list type is grouped by customer/supplier in a detailed view. Each customer/supplier is shown on a separate row in the list. Some columns have been merged. The columns show either customer's case number or supplier's case number, and customer reference or supplier reference. Since a case cannot have information filled in for both customer and supplier, the columns have been given this design.

#### No. of rejections
In this column you see a total of the number of rejections for the customer/supplier in question.

#### Planned and Actual cost
In these columns you see totals of planned and actual costs per customer/supplier. This can be shown per basic type, where for example all customer nonconformities are totaled. This can also be shown as a total per customer. At the bottom of the list you will see a total for the entire list.
