### Result
In this box you see the suppliers loaded according to the selection and settings. Select the suppliers to which you want reminders to be printed/sent by e-mail. The e-mail addresses are loaded from the supplier register. If addresses are missing or if they have been incorrectly entered, you will be asked if you wish to enter them manually.
After having selected the rows you want to include, and printed or e-mailed the rows in question, you can approve the printouts. You approve the printouts by checking the Approve box and clicking the button ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/StatusFinished.png). If you need to reprint, you cancel by clicking the button ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_cancel.png). The documents can then be reprinted, using the same or another method.

#### Include
With this checkbox you determine whether or not the reminder will be printed/sent to the supplier.

#### Supplier
Here you can see the supplier number and name for the supplier that you are reminding. If the supplier is a stray supplier, the name is loaded from the order.

#### Printing method
Here you determine how the reminder will be sent. The available alternatives are printer and e-mail.

#### Approve
After having printed the reminders, you can approve them. If you are not pleased with the printouts, you uncheck the rows in this column. You can then reprint or resend the reminders, regardless of the printing method selected the firs time.

#### Total
At the bottom, you will see a total of the number of included suppliers, and how many that will be e-mailed and how many that will be printed.
