### Invoice in sales company

#### Show production company's delivery note number
With this setting you determine whether the production company’s delivery note number should be shown on the invoice. The following options are available:
- No (default)
- Yes, only for deliveries from production company to end customer – If this setting is used and the delivery goes straight to the end customer from the production company, the delivery note number from the production company is transferred to the sales company. This delivery note number is then used on the invoice and when arrival reporting the purchase order.
