### Terms
In this box you configure different terms for the supplier. The terms are payment terms, any cash discount, delivery terms, delivery method, response time, packaging terms, and payment plan.
If you have installed the option Warehouse you can also make exceptions for delivery terms and delivery method – per warehouse for the supplier. For a new supplier, the same delivery terms and delivery method are saved on all warehouses. If you change warehouse for the supplier and select another of these terms, these will only be saved for the warehouse in question. On a purchase order, these terms are determined by the selected warehouse on the order.
Monitor ERP is delivered with a number of predefined standard terms and in addition to these it is possible create own terms that suit your company and your specific work methods. These terms must first be registered in the Terms procedure. Read more about the different terms in the online Help function for that procedure. If a specific term is missing altogether, the field will be gray. In that case, you will see the button Go to procedure ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_link.png) to the right of the terms field. Click this to go to the Terms procedure.

#### Delivery date
Here you can enter a general delivery date for the order. The date entered here will be used by default for the first order row. If the field is empty, the delivery date will be entered according to the system setting called Default delivery date on order rows. If you change the delivery date for an existing order, you can also replan the order using a new delivery date for each order row.
