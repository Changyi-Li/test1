### Terms
- For all types of terms it is now possible to enter a default term. This is then the term which will be default when you create new customers and suppliers. (In G4 it was the term at the top which was selected by default.) For example you can configure a default payment method when registering cash receipt.
- For payment terms you can enter up to three limits for cash discount for the same term.
- For delivery method you can link, shipping agent, shipping service, and shipment template.
- You can use the payment term called "End of Month" (EOM).
