### Settings – Storage overhead mark-up
Under this heading you can enter the general storage overhead mark-up (SO) and subcontract cost mark-up (SC) in percent that will apply in the calculation. The default general mark-up for SC (Subcontracting cost) The SC is a percentage mark-up of the subcontract cost in order to cover all of the costs for the subcontract. Subcontract cost + SC together describe costs for purchase, freight, customs, handling, and stock-keeping. and SO (Storage overhead) The SO mark-up is a percentage mark-up of the material cost in order to cover all the costs for the material. Material cost + SO together describe costs for purchase, freight, customs, handling, and stock-keeping. in percent, and different exceptions from these, if any, must first be registered in the Calculation mark-up procedure.

#### General SC mark-up
Here you enter the general subcontract cost mark-up in percent. This percent value is possible to change.

#### General SO mark-up
Here you enter the general storage overhead mark-up in percent. This percent value is possible to change.
