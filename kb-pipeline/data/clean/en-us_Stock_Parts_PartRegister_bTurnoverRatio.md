### Turnover ratio
Here you see the part's turnover ratio which is calculated and saved for the part via the Turnover ratio list type in the Stock analysis procedure.

#### Actual
Here you see the part's actual turnover ratio. This is calculated as Last year's consumption / Last year's average stock.

#### Calculated
Here you see the part's calculated turnover ratio. This is calculated as Annual volume / Calculated average stock.
Calculated average stock = Safety stock + Order quantity / 2.
