### The Calculate expenses list
This list is used to calculate and analyze purchase expenses and mark-ups based on a selected volume of purchased parts and price type.
After you have loaded the list you can change quantity and order quantity and in a second step you can do a calculation with structure explosion, based on the selection made for the list and based on the entered quantity. The calculation is started by using the Start calculation button ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_run.png) (Ctrl + R) on the toolbar of the procedure.
During the explosion, the expenses are calculated for each purchased part based on the quantity. This is displayed under the Result tab.

#### Quantity
Here you see/enter the quantity on which the expenses should be calculated. The default value is loaded from where you selected to load volume in the settings for the list.

#### Order quantity
Here you see/enter the part's order quantity. If the part has no order quantity, the value 1,00 is displayed in red and italic font. The value shown here is either the part's order quantity or the quantity entered in the Fallback quantity field. In the latter case, the value is displayed in red and italic font.

#### Include
Here you determine whether or not the part should be included in the calculation.
