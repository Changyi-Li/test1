### Supplier’s most recent invoices
When you have registered a supplier invoice it becomes possible to see information about how the supplier has invoiced you on previous occasions. The information might be useful when you are handling an invoice from the supplier in question. When handling an electricity bill you can for example wish to see previous electricity bills, the amounts, and how these have been posted. You can also see the images of the most recent invoices and compare these to the invoice you are working on.
In the box you can see the most recent invoices from the supplier in question. The invoices are shown in descending order based on invoice date. The most recent invoice is shown first. By default the 10 most recent invoices are displayed, but this can be changed in a field underneath the table. The posting of the invoice selected in the list is shown in the bottom box in the tab.
