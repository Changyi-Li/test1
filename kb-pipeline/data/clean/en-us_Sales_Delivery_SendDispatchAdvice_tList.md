### The List tab
In this list you see the records which should be advised via EDI EDI is the acronym of Electronic Data Interchange. EDI is about exchanging electronic business documents with your business partners, e.g. customers and suppliers. The EDI concept can be wide and a bit unclear, and can many times be used about all types of documents which are sent electronically, even if it might be PDF files sent via e-mail or publishing business documents on a website. What we refer to as EDI – and what is traditionally meant by EDI – is structured business documents following given standards, electronically sent or received and which are compiled and interpreted automatically and that is integrated with the customer's/supplier's ERP system.. You dispatch advice by marking Include for the rows you want and click ![](https://help.monitorerp.cn/CN-MONITOR_G5/en-us/Content/Resources/Images/button_run.png) Send dispatch advices (Ctrl + R).
