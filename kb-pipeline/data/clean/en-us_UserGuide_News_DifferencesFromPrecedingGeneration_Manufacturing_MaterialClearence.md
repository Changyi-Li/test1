### Material clearance
- Clearance is performed on location level which is then used when reporting the material.
- The procedure has a selection (which it does not have in G4).
- The shortage list is a document which contains more information and the user can modify the design of it.
- The check of disposable material takes place on order, part, or operation. The end result is that entire order, whole part, or entire operation will be cleared if all material is disposable. If material is missing, no clearance will be made. There are options to clear separate material even though there is shortages on other materials on order, part, or operation.
- Options to optimize full clearance, to make as many orders, parts, and operations cleared in cases where they have material in common which is not sufficient for all.
- There is a link to the pick list for cleared materials.
