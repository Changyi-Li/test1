### The list type Reconciliation/day
This list is used to find the cause of any differences between the remaining amount in the accounts payable and the balance in the general ledger. In the list you see a summary per day. You also see for which days within the selected time period that a change made in the accounts payable does not coincide with a change made in in the general ledger. You also see warnings here if the journals have not been transferred to the accounting. The Warnings button links to the Warnings tab. Here you see information about the cause of the warning. You also find a link here that is used to perform necessary adjustments. In the upper box you see the account's transactions for the selected date interval. In the lower left box, you see data from the accounts payable. In the lower right box, you see data from the accounting from the current account and date interval.
