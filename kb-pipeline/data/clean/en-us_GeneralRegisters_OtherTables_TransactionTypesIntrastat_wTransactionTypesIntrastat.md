## Transaction types – Intrastat
In this procedure you find a table with transaction types that applies to deliveries (of customer order rows and purchase order rows) made within EU. This is used during Intrastat Intrastat is the system which gathers statistics relating to trade in products within the European Union. Gathering of Intrastat statistics is handled in the same way by all EU member states. reporting.
You normally do not have to edit the table since it already contains the most common transaction types.
